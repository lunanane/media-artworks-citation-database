# The Treachery of Sanctuary

**Chris Milk**, 2012 · Projection / Camera / computer vision / Sensor / interaction

The Treachery of Sanctuary is an interactive triptych of three 30-foot-high white panels hung from the ceiling behind a shallow reflecting pool. Hidden Kinect cameras capture visitors' shadows and project them onto the panels. In the first panel the shadow dissolves into birds that fly up to join a flock. In the second, birds swoop down and tear pieces off it. In the third, the shadow grows large wings that follow the visitor's arm movements. It opened at The Creators Project at Fort Mason, San Francisco, in 2012 and then toured.
