# Broken Spectre

**Richard Mosse**, 2023 · Projection / Sound / Camera / computer vision

Broken Spectre (2022) is a feature-length immersive video installation (about 70–74 minutes) on deforestation and environmental crime along the Trans-Amazonian Highway in the Brazilian Amazon, made with cinematographer Trevor Tweeten and composer Ben Frost. It alternates footage from an aerial multispectral camera flown from a helicopter, ultraviolet macro time-lapse of the forest floor, and black-and-white infrared 35mm film of ranchers, gold miners and Indigenous communities, including a speech to camera by Yanomami woman Adneia. Visitors watch it in a gallery with a spatialised soundtrack; it received the 2023 S+T+ARTS Grand Prize – Innovative Collaboration.
