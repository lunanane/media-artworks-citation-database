# The Echinoidea Future – Adriatic Sensing

**Robertina Šebjanič**, 2024 · Material / sculpture / Screen / monitor / Network / data

An installation developed from the Zero Pollution Adriatic residency (UR Institute, STARTS4Water, 2021/2022) on how anthropogenic liquid waste and low oxygen levels affect sea urchin habitats in the southern Adriatic. It combines glassblown sculptures based on laboratory experiments exposing sea urchins to pollutants with experimental setups and synchronised screens showing three decades of Copernicus environmental data (temperature, pH, oxygen, salinity) transformed with AI. It received an Honorary Mention at the S+T+ARTS Prize 2024.

## In their words

> The storytelling of the artwork will represent the current situation of changes in the marine environment that are mostly caused by human presence (residue waste, oil, low oxygen, hypoxia). I want to go beyond showing the current situation and show the future potential of coexistence in the coastal areas.
>
> — Robertina Šebjanič, ["The Influence of Pollutants on the Adriatic Sea – in conversation with Robertina Šebjanič and Marjan Žitnik", S+T+ARTS](https://starts.eu/?p=7781)
