# Our Islands 11°16’58.4”N 123°45’07.0”E

**Martha Atienza**, 2017 · Screen / monitor / Projection / Performance / body

A video in which underwater compressor divers from Bantayan Island, Philippines, stage a version of the annual Ati-Atihan parade on the floor of the Visayan Sea, wearing costumes that reference religious, animistic and pop culture figures. The coordinates in the title were chosen by the divers according to tide, current and time of day. It was shown in the Statements sector of Art Basel 2017 with Silverlens and screened nightly on Times Square billboards in July 2024 as part of Midnight Moment.

## In their words

> What started out as the simple idea of trying to understand my father soon became a way for all of us on Bantayan to start difficult dialogues around family and community.
>
> — Martha Atienza, [Art Basel Stories, interview by Stephanie Bailey (2024)](https://www.artbasel.com/stories/martha-atienza-bantayan-island-divers-times-square?lang=en)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> The questions that occurred then and are still relevant today, include: Are we developing for the better? Are we still a community that helps one another? How do we preserve who we are but move with our time? But also: Can art trigger social, environmental, and even economical change here on Bantayan?
>
> — Martha Atienza, [Art Basel Stories, interview by Stephanie Bailey (2024)](https://www.artbasel.com/stories/martha-atienza-bantayan-island-divers-times-square?lang=en)

<sub>asks · about their practice</sub>

> This work is about connecting to other island nations; about creating and sharing models to deal with climate change and the protection of our land, sea and people and using our seafaring cultures and learn from one another. One ocean, one people.
>
> — Martha Atienza, [Art Basel Stories, interview by Stephanie Bailey (2024)](https://www.artbasel.com/stories/martha-atienza-bantayan-island-divers-times-square?lang=en)

<sub>says</sub>

> In fact, things kept getting worse. There’s not enough fish, people are still compressor diving, families are being displaced, young people don’t even know what an intact sea looks like anymore. It was depressing, coming back and wondering what else we can do
>
> — Martha Atienza, [Vogue Philippines, 'Island Story' by Audrey Carpio (May 2025)](https://vogue.ph/lifestyle/art/martha-atienza-island-story-bantayan/)
