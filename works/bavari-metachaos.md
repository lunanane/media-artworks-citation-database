# Metachaos

**Alessandro Bavari**, 2011 · Screen / monitor

Metachaos is an 8-minute 27-second short film by Alessandro Bavari, made with digitally manipulated photography, animation and a shaky 'camera tremula', in black and white and sepia tones with a soundtrack by Jeff Ensign. Anthropomorphic, identity-less figures overrun a fortress in a spaceless, timeless landscape. The film is part of a multidisciplinary project that also includes a photographic series and mixed-technique paintings; it won the Golden Nica for Computer Animation at Prix Ars Electronica 2011.

## In their words

> The purpose of the project is to represent the most tragic aspects of the human nature and of its motion, such as war, madness, social change and hate.
>
> — Alessandro Bavari, [Metachaos project text, alessandrobavari.com](https://www.alessandrobavari.com/metachaosvideo)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> In fact the bodies represented in METACHAOS, even though they are characterized by an apparently anthropomorphous appearance, in reality they are without identity and conscience.
>
> — Alessandro Bavari, [Metachaos project text, alessandrobavari.com](https://www.alessandrobavari.com/metachaosvideo)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> I represented these figures with bodies of human appearance but without any identity, without an individual ego but with a collective ego devoid of rational thought, reason and feeling, endowed only with an instinctive need for conflict and destruction, a formless mass without conscience which, like a single organism, constantly mutates in a sort of involution, in an escalation of dehumanisation until it reaches the monstrous.
>
> — Alessandro Bavari, [Interview, 'HUMANS. Video-ritratti della società contemporanea. #16 Conflitti', Artribune, June 2022 (translated from Italian)](https://www.artribune.com/?p=801408)
