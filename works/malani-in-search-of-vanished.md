# In Search of Vanished Blood

**Nalini Malani**, 2012 · Projection / Sound / Robotics / kinetics

In Search of Vanished Blood is a room-filling video/shadow play commissioned by dOCUMENTA (13) in 2012: six synchronised films are projected through five rotating Mylar cylinders reverse-painted with figures of soldiers, animals, gods and guns, so that moving images and shadows pass over the walls, floor and visitors. A soundscape, voiced and designed by the artist, speaks from the perspective of Cassandra and draws on texts by Faiz Ahmed Faiz, Christa Wolf, Mahasweta Devi, Samuel Beckett and Heiner Müller. The title comes from a 1965 Urdu poem by Faiz; a single-channel video version is in the M+ collection, Hong Kong.
