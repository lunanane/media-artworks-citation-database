# Different Shades of Water

**Osinachi**, 2021 · Screen / monitor / Network / data

A series of five digital paintings made in Microsoft Word and issued as NFTs, restaging David Hockney's Portrait of an Artist (Pool with Two Figures) (1972) with a Black man as the poolside bather under different daylight and water colours. The works were shown at 1-54 Contemporary African Art Fair at Somerset House, London, in October 2021 and auctioned online by Christie's, the first NFT by a contemporary African artist offered by Christie's in Europe.

## In their words

> I did Different Shades of Water primarily because I wanted to challenge my medium, to paint believable water bodies using the limited tools I have on Microsoft Word.
>
> — Osinachi, [objkt blog, 'From Word to the World: an Interview with Osinachi' (2025)](https://blog.objkt.com/post/from-word-to-the-worldan-interview-with-osinachi)

<sub>says</sub>

> For most Black people who saw these five artworks, it was a thing of beauty and validation to see a Black body having a good time in and around the pool. Years ago, this was not possible because of racial segregation in public pools.
>
> — Osinachi, [objkt blog, 'From Word to the World: an Interview with Osinachi' (2025)](https://blog.objkt.com/post/from-word-to-the-worldan-interview-with-osinachi)

<sub>says</sub>

> Through my art, I want to tell authentic stories influenced by my personal experience. One of the things I have experienced as a Nigerian is injustice. Another is inequality. The list goes on. I use my art to highlight these issues.
>
> — Osinachi, [MoreBranches interview, 13 February 2020 (also quoted in the Christie's lot essay for the series)](https://www.morebranches.com/meet-osinachi/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> My practice is geared towards pointing attention to the other way that a man can also be and look and act; and, by so doing, normalize this otherness. This is me protesting by showing things as they are.
>
> — Osinachi, [MoreBranches interview, 13 February 2020](https://www.morebranches.com/meet-osinachi/)
