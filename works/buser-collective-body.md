# Collective Body

**Sarah Silverblatt-Buser**, 2025 · VR / AR / XR / Performance / body / Sound

Collective Body is a 19-minute multi-user virtual reality dance experience set in a New Mexican storm, in which participants are guided through stages: beginning alone, discovering the unfamiliar, first encounters and moving together. An algorithmic system analyses each participant's movement and assigns one of sixteen avatars, each represented as a natural element with its own musical theme; participants then meet one other person and finally the whole group. It runs on Meta Quest 3 headsets with almost full-body tracking and no other wearables; it was shown at Venice Immersive 2025 and at Lincoln Center.

## In their words

> Collective Body asks what it means to experience ourselves in relation to others — without words and beneath the surface. How does a body hold histories over time? How do those histories get expressed and interpreted? How do many bodies create their own ecology?
>
> — Sarah Silverblatt-Buser, [Main Creator's Statement, Collective Body, Venice Immersive 2025, La Biennale di Venezia](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2025/venice-immersive/collective-body)

<sub>asks</sub>

> I was asking myself, “What does this mean for our embodied selves? What does it mean for the way we express ourselves physically if we’re only going to be these avatars? How can we represent ourselves differently in the virtual space? How could I create something that actually used the virtual to bring us together in real life?”
>
> — Sarah Silverblatt-Buser, [The Dance Enthusiast Asks, interview by Sarah Cecilia Bukowski (2025)](https://www.dance-enthusiast.com/features/the-dance-enthusiast-asks/view/3690)

<sub>asks</sub>

> I wanted to find something that could question this potential virtual future and remind us of the importance of physicality in the sense of how our bodies express and represent our ingrained histories that we don’t necessarily speak about. So I wanted to do something that would allow us to meet each other through movement.
>
> — Sarah Silverblatt-Buser, [The Dance Enthusiast Asks, interview by Sarah Cecilia Bukowski (2025)](https://www.dance-enthusiast.com/features/the-dance-enthusiast-asks/view/3690)
