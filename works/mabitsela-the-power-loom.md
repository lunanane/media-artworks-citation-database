# The Power Loom

**Lesiba Mabitsela, Meghna Singh, Simon Wood**, 2025 · AI / generative / VR / AR / XR / Material / sculpture

The Power Loom is a multimedia installation that digitally weaves African textiles. It is one part of The Founders Pillars; the other part is a site-specific augmented reality experience that uses the same patterns to wrap the columns of the New York Stock Exchange as a memorial to enslaved peoples. It had its world premiere at Tribeca Immersive 2025 ('In Search of Us', WSA / Water Street Projects, 6–29 June 2025), and the project received a S+T+ARTS Prize Africa Award of Distinction in 2025.
