# Homeland

**Halil Altındere**, 2016 · Screen / monitor / Sound

Homeland is a 10-minute HD video by Halil Altındere, commissioned and co-produced by the 9th Berlin Biennale (2016), with voice and lyrics by the Syrian rapper and activist Mohammad Abu Hajar and music by Nguzunguzu. Abu Hajar's song accompanies scenes of refugees' journeys, partly based on real-life footage and partly staged, from a border crossing to the waterways of Istanbul and ending at the former Tempelhof airport in Berlin, then used as a refugee camp. It was later shown at the 13th Bienal de Artes Mediales in Santiago.

## In their words

> Space Refugee and Homeland have the same feeling, the same sense of “hope”. I don’t like complaining, showing crying people – hopeless, vulnerable and desperate refugees. I prefer to focus on the good parts of really difficult situations.
>
> — Halil Altındere, [Amanda Ribas Tugwell, 'Halil Altindere interview', The Berliner, 13 September 2016](https://www.the-berliner.com/art/halil-altindere-interview/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Homeland blended fantasy with real-life footage, but my sense of humour effected the real footage – everyone actually thinks it’s all fantasy.
>
> — Halil Altındere, [Amanda Ribas Tugwell, 'Halil Altindere interview', The Berliner, 13 September 2016](https://www.the-berliner.com/art/halil-altindere-interview/)

<sub>says</sub>

> When I was invited to have a solo show at NBK, I wanted to do a project on the refugee crisis. Then I got the invitation from HAU and I offered them Köfte Airlines, and then I got the invitation from the Berlin Biennale and I decided to link all of the works. In Homeland you can see references to Köfte Airlines.
>
> — Halil Altındere, [Amanda Ribas Tugwell, 'Halil Altindere interview', The Berliner, 13 September 2016](https://www.the-berliner.com/art/halil-altindere-interview/)
