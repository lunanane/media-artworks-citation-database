# rheo: 5 horizons

**Ryoichi Kurokawa**, 2010 · Screen / monitor / Sound

rheo: 5 horizons is an audiovisual installation of five vertical flat-panel displays and five speakers set in a row, each display paired and synchronised with its own mono sound channel. Video and field recordings of real landscapes are transformed by digital processing into graphic analyses and re-syntheses, so that the movement of the images indicates the position and direction of the sounds. It received the Golden Nica in the Digital Musics & Sound Art category of the Prix Ars Electronica 2010.

## In their words

> By synthesising the individual images, the sound cognition can be enhanced accordingly – in order for the behaviour of the resulting imagery to reveal its own sound source position, sonic direction and drive.
>
> — Ryoichi Kurokawa, [Interview with Ryoichi Kurokawa: Synesthetic Experience, Sedition](https://www.seditionart.com/magazine/interview-ryoichi-kurokawa-synesthetic-experience)

> As the work provides the space to rediscover the sonic movement, position, and the relation between ego and sound. These resonances have an impact on sensory perception in order for it to develop a similar synesthetic experience.
>
> — Ryoichi Kurokawa, [Interview with Ryoichi Kurokawa: Synesthetic Experience, Sedition](https://www.seditionart.com/magazine/interview-ryoichi-kurokawa-synesthetic-experience)

<sub>says</sub>

> Rheo is a concert piece which is presented as three vertically long screens with surround sound, inspired by the words ‘panta rhei’ that were left by Heraclitus. The term ‘rheo’ as the title means flow, current, or stream from the Greek word.
>
> — Ryoichi Kurokawa, [Interview with Ryoichi Kurokawa: Synesthetic Experience, Sedition](https://www.seditionart.com/magazine/interview-ryoichi-kurokawa-synesthetic-experience)

> Nature inspires my work a lot, and my attempt to reconstruct nature has been one of my main concerns from early on in my career, but to qualify that, I in no way attempt to celebrate nature.
>
> — Ryoichi Kurokawa, [Interview with Ryoichi Kurokawa: Synesthetic Experience, Sedition](https://www.seditionart.com/magazine/interview-ryoichi-kurokawa-synesthetic-experience)
