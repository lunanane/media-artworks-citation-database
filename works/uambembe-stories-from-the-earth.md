# Stories from the Earth

**Helena Uambembe**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / Sound

A video work commissioned for the 36th Bienal de São Paulo, shown in a film booth that is introduced by a painted mural at its entrance, with the artist's own narration. The narration retells Greek mythology and African folklore through twin giant brothers whose rivalry splits the earth, and stages the split between Pão de Açúcar in Rio de Janeiro and Morro do Moco in Huambo, Angola.
