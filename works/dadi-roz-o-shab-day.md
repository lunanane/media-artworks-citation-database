# Roz o shab (day and night)

**Iftikhar Dadi and Elizabeth Dadi**, 2018 · Light / Material / sculpture

Roz o shab (day and night) is a site-specific neon installation made for the first Lahore Biennale (LB01, 2018) in the Summer Palace of the Lahore Fort. A maze of blue neon is threaded with red neon tracing the one route through it; its layout recalls the labyrinthine bhool bhulaiyan of Mughal architecture, and the Biennale relates the red and blue paths to verses by Muhammad Iqbal and to the cycle of night and day. According to Iftikhar Dadi, the work was shown for two weeks in March 2018 and destroyed after the Biennale ended.

## In their words

> For example, the neon work Elizabeth and myself made for the Lahore Biennale, Roz o shab, was destroyed after being shown for two weeks in March, once the Biennale was over. It was made for a specific site, a limited duration and a particular event – as an ephemeral architectural intervention in a heritage site.
>
> — Iftikhar Dadi, [Ammara Ahmad, 'Whither authenticity?', The Friday Times, 4 May 2018](https://www.thefridaytimes.com/2018/05/04/whither-authenticity/)

<sub>says</sub>

> The question of time is an important one for contemporary artists. Modernism sought to create permanence and durability, but artists today are not necessarily seeking that. Their work may recognise time itself as a medium, and acknowledge the question of permanence and transience as a central concern in their work.
>
> — Iftikhar Dadi, [Ammara Ahmad, 'Whither authenticity?', The Friday Times, 4 May 2018](https://www.thefridaytimes.com/2018/05/04/whither-authenticity/)
