# Versión libre

**Clemencia Echeverri**, 2011 · Projection / Sound

Versión libre is a multichannel video and sound installation (five simultaneous projections, 5.1 sound and five large-format photographs) made with demobilised members of Colombia's armed conflict, first shown at the VI Premio Luis Caballero at Galería Santa Fe, Bogotá, in 2011. A large central screen carries the testimony of balaclava-wearing ex-combatants, whose voices spread through the room as echo and murmur, while three narrow, larger-than-life projections show bodies walking back and forth and another video shows people walking away with their backs turned.

> It is a tracing through elapsed time, loaded with stories that are hard to tell, with hidden truths, with episodes of our barbarity contained in layers of victims and perpetrators.
