# Dreams Have No Titles

**Zineb Sedira**, 2022 · Screen / monitor / Material / sculpture / Sound

Sedira's installation for the French Pavilion at the 59th Venice Biennale (2022): a series of interlocking film sets, including a re-creation of her London living room, a shelf of film reels referencing the Cinémathèque Algérienne, a ballroom bar and a cinema room, combined with a film of re-enactments and 'makings of' scenes. It draws on Algerian, French and Italian militant cinema of the 1960s and 1970s, including The Battle of Algiers and the rediscovered Les Mains libres. Visitors walk through the sets, watch the film and at times meet two performers dancing the tango.

## In their words

> I also relate the notion of remake to that of the mise en abyme, which often crops up in my work. I myself am an artist-director making a film about films. My personal history is the starting point for a mise en abyme of the history of cinema by means of different strategies designed to create a fiction-reality.
>
> — Zineb Sedira, [e-flux announcement, French Pavilion, Venice Biennale 2022](https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/438576/zineb-sediradreams-have-no-titles/)
