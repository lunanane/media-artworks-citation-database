# VOID

**Joshua Serafin**, 2022 · Performance / body / Light

VOID (2022-ongoing) is a touring performance in which Joshua Serafin embodies a nonbinary deity, drawing on myths of the creation of the Philippine archipelago; it is part of the series Cosmological Gangbang. At the 60th Venice Biennale (2024, Nucleo Contemporaneo, Arsenale) audiences in a dark room watched Serafin move around a central crater in the floor while covering their body in a black, viscous substance, with light and smoke, video and sound; spectators were given ponchos.

## In their words

> Void considers what is the club as a creature? What is this god within the darkrooms, toilets and bathrooms? You know, where we take all these drugs secretly…What is that entity in the darkroom? What are the energies of human behaviour in this equation? How do I become that entity? How does the entity move? Breathe? Look? It’s world-making.
>
> — Joshua Serafin, [10 Magazine, 'Joshua Serafin: Touching The Void' (Laurie Barron, 2024)](https://10magazine.com/?p=388233)
