# The East Japan Earthquake Archive

**Hidenori Watanave**, 2013 · Network / data / Online / net art / VR / AR / XR

The East Japan Earthquake Archive is a digital archive on Google Earth that maps photographs, 360-degree panorama images, video testimonies of disaster victims (collected with the Asahi Shimbun and recorded binaurally by student volunteers) and a layer of geotagged tweets from the 24 hours after the earthquake of 11 March 2011. Users browse the material from a bird's-eye view and along a timeline; an iPhone/iPad app, eARthquake 311, shows the data on maps and overlaid on the street through augmented reality. It received an Honorary Mention in Digital Communities at the Prix Ars Electronica 2013.
