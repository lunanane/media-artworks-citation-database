# Reality Looks Back

**Anne Jeppesen**, 2025 · Projection / Sound

A 21-minute dome documentary, world-premiered in the IDFA DocLab Competition for Immersive Non-Fiction in 2025 and shown in the DocLab Exhibition and at the Planetarium, directed by Anne Jeppesen with co-director Omid Zarei (Superposition XR Studio). Jeppesen's narration moves through quantum observation, split déjà vu, parallel realities and the death of her elderly hamster Oliver, over documentary footage processed into Gaussian splats. The work began as an audio piece made in the YASS! mentorship programme (Lucia Festival / Radio Papesse), premiered in Florence in 2024.
