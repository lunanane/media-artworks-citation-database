# Conspiracy: Conjoining the Virtual

**Kristin McWharter**, 2018 · VR / AR / XR / Material / sculpture / Performance / body

A participatory sculpture in which five people, each wearing a VR headset, are joined to one armature and play a game together in virtual space while the structure constrains their movement to side-to-side steps. Bellows between the headsets compress as participants move, so that the sculpture appears to breathe. The artist's site lists the work as 'Conspire' (2018): VR headsets, kinetic armature and an audio coach.

## In their words

> In my work I am most immediately trying to understand how people experience relationships, and specifically how people experience the tension between competition and intimacy. I use these tensions within participatory sculpture as tools to re-frame how we see or identify with this larger social context.
>
> — Kristin McWharter, [Prix Ars Electronica archive, catalogue text (Interactive Art +, Honorary Mention 2018)](https://archive.aec.at/prix/237373/)
