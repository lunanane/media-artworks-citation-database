# Horn Orchestra

**Younes Baba-Ali**, 2012 · Sound / Sensor / interaction / Material / sculpture

Horn Orchestra is a sound installation of ten car horns suspended in an enclosed space. Silent when no one is there, it is triggered by the presence of visitors who walk into the space and then sounds at random. It won the Léopold Sédar Senghor Grand Prize at the Dak'Art Biennale in Dakar in May 2012.
