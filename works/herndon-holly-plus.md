# Holly+

**Holly Herndon & Mat Dryhurst**, 2021– · Sound / AI / machine learning / Network / data

A public website where anyone can upload audio and get it back 'sung' in a machine-learning model of Holly Herndon's voice, built with Never Before Heard Sounds. What people make is theirs to use; 'official' Holly+ works are voted on by a DAO of stewards, sold as NFTs, with profits split between the maker, the DAO treasury and Herndon.
