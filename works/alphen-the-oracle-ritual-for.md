# The Oracle: Ritual for the Future (for humans and non-humans)

**Victorine van Alphen**, 2025 · Performance / body / AI / generative / Projection

The Oracle: Ritual for the Future is a 60-minute immersive performance by Victorine van Alphen (Studio Brave New Human) in which the audience moves through a 360-degree screen environment. Starting from the personal story and archival material of two AI enthusiasts, the audience is first given control and then overtaken by non-human entities and AI-generated images; in one scene, audience members who consented have their image altered by AI. It premiered at IDFA DocLab 2025.
