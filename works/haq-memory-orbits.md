# Memory Orbits

**Ehsan ul Haq & Iqra Tanveer**, 2024 · Material / sculpture / Projection / Light

A site-specific installation across the five underground rooms of the Barracks at Nasir Bagh, Lahore, commissioned for Lahore Biennale 03 (2024). Visitors descend into the former air-raid bunkers and pass through objects, ceramic sculptures, a mud-dyed suspended cloth, plant material and light and video projections, including a room with forty images of the sun's phases on the curved wall facing a row of forty wax candles. The work is built around the image of an eclipse.
