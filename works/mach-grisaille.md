# Grisaille

**Teek Mach**, 2019 · VR / AR / XR / Projection / Performance / body

Grisaille is a two-part mixed-reality painting experience by Teek Mach, with composer Joel Douek, performed in the Sundance Film Festival's New Frontier section from 25 January to 2 February 2019. In part one a visitor puts on an Oculus Go headset in a dark hallway and moves through a virtual copy of it, past a hologram of the artist and a series of her hand-painted VR portraits; in part two the artist, present in the room, draws the visitor's silhouette with Tilt Brush on an HTC Vive Pro, projected live onto the wall and joined to the silhouettes of earlier visitors, while Douek layers festival sounds with original music. Mach performed it eleven hours a day for the nine days of the festival.

We still experience the same things. We have feelings of isolation and the feeling that we’re not a part of something bigger, but we are ultimately at our core connected as a species.
>
> — Teek Mach, ['Inception: Artist Teek Mach Paints VR Visions To Bring People Together', Character Media, 13 March 2019](https://charactermedia.com/inception-artist-teek-mach-paints-vr-visions-to-bring-people-together-asian-american-vietnamese-american-artist-virtual-reality/)
