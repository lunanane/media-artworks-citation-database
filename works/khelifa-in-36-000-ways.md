# In 36,000 Ways (Site-Specific Installation)

**Karim Ben Khelifa**, 2025 · Sensor / interaction / Sound / Material / sculpture

In 36,000 Ways (Site-Specific Installation) is a single-visitor booth in which the visitor is shown a piece of missile shrapnel the artist collected on front lines in Ukraine. The visitor's presence and physiological signals, including heartbeat, are picked up without contact and shape a low-frequency soundscape. The title refers to the roughly 36,000 fragments a fragmentation weapon breaks into; the booth is a variation of an ongoing project, shown in the IDFA DocLab Competition 2025, with a larger walk-through version in development.
