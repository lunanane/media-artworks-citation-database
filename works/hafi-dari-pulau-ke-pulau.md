# Dari Pulau Ke Pulau (From One Island to Another)

**HAFI**, 2025 · Projection

A projection mapping work shown on the facade of CHIJMES at the Singapore Night Festival 2025 (22 Aug-6 Sep), made by HAFI (Hafizah Jainal) as part of the festival's third Projection Mapping Masterclass, led by the Malaysian collective Filamen. Using illustration and moving images, it traces the routes of the artist's maternal and paternal grandparents from Alor Gajah, Melaka, and Pacitan, East Java, to Singapore.

>

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> Rooted in rich family history and ancestral memory, preserved through cherished oral storytelling and treasured heirlooms, this work constructs a compelling visual narrative. Through a combination of illustration and moving images, it vividly reimagines the routes taken by these ancestors, mapping not only the geographical landscapes they traversed but also the profound emotional experiences that shaped their arrival in Singapore.
>
> — Singapore Night Festival 2025 (programme text), [Singapore Night Festival 2025 programme page (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20251015055747/https://www.heritage.sg/sgnightfest/whats-on/dari-pulau-ke-pulau)
