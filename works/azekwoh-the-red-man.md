# The Red Man

**Anthony Azekwoh**, 2020 · Screen / monitor / Online / net art

A digital portrait painted in Photoshop and posted online in June 2020: a figure in red hues with uncoloured eyes against a red background. After it circulated widely on social media, the artist sold prints of it and it was later sold as an NFT on SuperRare.

## In their words

> The Red Man was one of those rare moments where I decided to work on something for myself, away from all the works that influenced me. I wasn’t trying to be like any of my art idols; it was something fun for me.
>
> — Anthony Azekwoh, [Zikoko, “Man Like” interview](https://www.zikoko.com/man/i-had-to-bet-on-myself-and-make-it-work-man-like-anthony-azekwoh/)

<sub>says</sub>

> It was maybe 2am and I didn’t have time to \[color the eyes\] so I just said let me leave it like that, it looks nice. A person’s eyes, you can see so much in them –it’s a focal point.
>
> — Anthony Azekwoh, [Olongo Africa, “How Anthony Azekwoh is creating a future of myths”](https://olongoafrica.com/how-anthony-azekwoh-is-creating-a-future-of-myths/)

<sub>says</sub>

> See, it didn’t just change things financially, it also changed things mentally. That was the first time I said to myself that art isn’t just a side mission, it’s THE mission.
>
> — Anthony Azekwoh, [PiggyVest Blog, “How a viral painting changed my life”](https://blog.piggyvest.com/life/how-a-viral-painting-changed-my-life/)

<sub>says</sub>

> I kept trying to recreate what happened with The Red Man so I could be counting millions every month, but everything flopped. I was, like, “Am I a one-painting wonder?”
>
> — Anthony Azekwoh, [PiggyVest Blog, “How a viral painting changed my life”](https://blog.piggyvest.com/life/how-a-viral-painting-changed-my-life/)

<sub>asks</sub>

> I'd always wanted to sculpt, and if you look at paintings like Red Man and how the features are rendered, that's very much in the way you sculpt.
>
> — Anthony Azekwoh, [OkayAfrica, “How Anthony Azekwoh is pushing the boundaries of what it means to be a digital artist”](https://www.okayafrica.com/how-anthony-azekwoh-is-pushing-the-boundaries-of-what-it-means-to-be-a-digital-artist/1411266)
