# #MementoMori

**Gabriel Tagle Petrone**, 2016 · Light / Text / print

#MementoMori is a 2.35 x 1.45 m light box with a backlit print lit by LEDs, connected to a pre-programmed light-flashing device with 10 reactions. According to its signed text, it puts together 'the serialization of Lincoln' and a phrase taken from Facebook recalling a friend who disappeared in the 'War on drugs'. It was shown at the 13th Bienal de Artes Mediales in Santiago de Chile (2017).

## In their words

> #MementoMori, allows to assemble two vertiginous times, that of the serialization of Lincoln and the absent presence in the phrase of a disappeared person

> A friendly, ironic and suspicious gesture, which aims to excavate in the museum as well as in the infosphere, in homage to the mothers of the disappeared in the mountains, in search of the body / memory / of the presence of the fallen and disappeared friend in the name of the imperial democratic mold, at the same time succumbed in the virtual saturation of the new global cybernetic cemetery.
>
> — Gabriel Tagle / Colectivo Vitrina Distópica / Mariagrazia Muscatello, [#MementoMori, 13 Bienal de Artes Mediales work page (English version)](https://13.bienaldeartesmediales.cl/obra/mementomori-2/)

<sub>says</sub>

> #MementoMori is then a question for the limits of memory today, for the absent presences and the restless memories that do not fit in the panoptic circulation of the hashtag and are inexhaustibly scratched on the pale faces, too frigid of the monumentalization.
>
> — Gabriel Tagle / Colectivo Vitrina Distópica / Mariagrazia Muscatello, [#MementoMori, 13 Bienal de Artes Mediales work page (English version)](https://13.bienaldeartesmediales.cl/obra/mementomori-2/)
