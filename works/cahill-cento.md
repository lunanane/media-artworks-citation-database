# CENTO

**Nancy Baker Cahill**, 2023 · VR / AR / XR / Online / net art / Screen / monitor

CENTO is an augmented-reality creature, geolocated above the eighth-floor terrace of the Whitney Museum of American Art, together with a video that tours the creature's imagined habitat (nest, refueling chamber, and the space where it metabolizes fuel and takes flight). Using the free 4th Wall app, on site or from anywhere, viewers choose one of twelve feathers, each with a function such as communication, navigation or energy conversion, and add it to the creature's body, which is updated over time. It is a Whitney artport commission; the title refers to a poem made of lines taken from other poets' works.
