# Carbon Black

**Anaïs Tondeur**, 2019 · Text / print / Material / sculpture

Carbon Black (Noir de carbone) is a series of 15 photographic prints of the sky made in collaboration with climate modellers Rita van Dingenen and Jean-Philippe Putaud (Joint Research Centre, European Commission). From Fair Isle, the scientists traced the black carbon particles in the air back to their emission point in Folkestone harbour; Tondeur then travelled the 837-mile route by foot, boat and car, photographing the sky each day and filtering particles through a breathing mask. The particles were extracted and turned into ink, with which each photograph is printed; the exhibition also shows maps, masks and the extracted particles.
