# Speak Slowly and It Will Become a Song

**Kwon Byungjun**, 2025 · Sound / Sensor / interaction / AI / generative

Speak Slowly and It Will Become a Song (2025) is an outdoor sound installation on the Lawn Square of the Aichi Prefectural Ceramic Museum in Seto, commissioned for the performing arts programme of Aichi Triennale 2025. Visitors walk the lawn wearing headphones; with high-resolution GPS and three-dimensional audio, the sounds change with the visitor's movement. The material includes folk songs and life songs improvised by local women, voices and interviews from Seto, and sounds of clay, water, fire and vegetation, with surrounding background sound that the artist says was interpreted by AI.

## In their words

> Before the work for Aichi was decided, I had the chance to hear a lecture by Kim HeyJung, a researcher of Korean folk songs, and I became interested in women's folk songs. Unlike men's, women's folk songs are said to be characterised by carrying the story of one's own life in the song. I thought it was wonderful how narration becomes melody and turns into music, and I wondered what meaning such folk songs have in the present day.
>
> — Kwon Byungjun, [BRUTUS.jp, 'Korean Cultural News' Vol.22 Artist Kwon Byungjun (translated from Japanese)](https://brutus.jp/en/post-449507)
