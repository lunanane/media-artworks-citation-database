# Mother Courage

**Warwick Thornton**, 2012 · Projection / Sound / Material / sculpture

Mother Courage is an installation in a white Kombi camper van that houses a dual-screen projection of an elderly Aboriginal woman artist, played by artist Grace Rubuntja, painting dot paintings among paint pots, utensils and bedding, while her grandson sits listening to Aboriginal rock music on a radio in the van. Visitors look in through the van's windows and rear doors. Commissioned by dOCUMENTA (13) and ACMI, it was first shown at dOCUMENTA (13) in Kassel in 2012, where the van was moved around the city and parked at different sites, and then installed in a gallery at ACMI, Melbourne, in 2013.

>
> — Warwick Thornton, [Andrew Stephens, 'In the spirit of mischief', The Age, 19 January 2013](https://www.theage.com.au/entertainment/art-and-design/in-the-spirit-of-mischief-20130118-2cxyf.html)

>
> — Warwick Thornton, [Andrew Stephens, 'In the spirit of mischief', The Age, 19 January 2013](https://www.theage.com.au/entertainment/art-and-design/in-the-spirit-of-mischief-20130118-2cxyf.html)
