# Drifting Bodies

**field-0 (Jingru (Cyan) Cheng and Chen Zhan)**, 2025 · Projection / Sound

A four-channel video installation (colour, sound, 10 min 30 sec, with curtains and mattress) that pairs a silent projection of the Jewel Rain Vortex indoor waterfall at Singapore's Changi Airport with three channels of footage and spatialised sound from Thailand's Vajiralongkorn Dam Reservoir, where displaced Karen families live on floating rafts; the two sites are linked by a shared hydroelectric power grid. Visitors walk through the waterfall projection screens to reach the reservoir footage. It was first shown at Storefront for Art and Architecture, New York (2025), and at Singapore Biennale 2025 alongside the sound work The Humming of the Power Grid and a fundraising Water Campaign for the reservoir community.

## In their words

> By placing the waterfall projection at the front of the space, we ask visitors to confront this iconic image, and to literally break the waterfall by walking through the screens, to enter the hidden world and the lived realities behind it.
>
> — field-0 (Jingru (Cyan) Cheng and Chen Zhan), [In Conversation: field-0 — Following Drifting Bodies (CNTRFLD.ART, 2026)](https://www.cntrfld.art/journal/in-conversation-field-0)
