# Where Do the Ants Go?

**Afrah Shafiq**, 2022 · Sensor / interaction / Screen / monitor / Material / sculpture

A multiplayer video game installation inside a large walk-in sculpture of an anthill, designed with architect Jeremy Waterfield, in which visitors together steer a digital colony of programmed ants on screens around the interior. Players feed the colony with thoughts they type into the system, and depending on which thoughts are taken up the colony can burn, drown or starve; there is nothing to win or lose. It was first shown at Dhaka Art Summit 2023 and then travelled (Horst Arts and Music Festival, Zeppelin Museum, Pivô São Paulo), with the anthill rebuilt in local materials at each site.
