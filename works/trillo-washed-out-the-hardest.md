# Washed Out "The Hardest Part"

**Paul Trillo**, 2024 · AI / generative / Screen / monitor

An official music video for the Washed Out song "The Hardest Part", made with OpenAI's text-to-video model Sora: a continuous forward push through time following a couple from school through marriage and later life across several decades. Trillo generated around 700 clips and edited about 55 of them into the roughly four-minute video; it received the 2024 Prix Ars Electronica Golden Nica (AI in Art) and was shown in the Ars Electronica Festival exhibition.
