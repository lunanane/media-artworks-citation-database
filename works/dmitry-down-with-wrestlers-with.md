# Down with Wrestlers with Systems and Mental Nonadapters!

**Kawarga Dmitry & Elena**, 2013 · Robotics / kinetics / Sensor / interaction / Material / sculpture

An interactive installation in which a visitor walks on a treadmill that sets in motion many small figures built into a 'social mechanism' of conveyor belts. Speaking the absurdist manifesto 'DA-DA' into a microphone sends a low-frequency pulse through a subwoofer that shakes hundreds of figures in a cellular structure above, so that some fall out of it. A miniature video camera on one conveyor shows on a monitor the inside of a black cube containing biomorphic sculptures; the installation measures 500 x 500 x 500 cm and was made with the Robotics Laboratory of the Moscow Polytechnic Museum.

## In their words

> He feels himself a godlike being on whose steps this movement depends and, at the same time, one of the figurines among the others. A splitting of roles takes place in his mind – does society absorb each of us? or do we ourselves form the enslaving mechanism?
>
> — Dmitry & Elena Kawarga, [Project text, kawarga.ru (translated from Russian)](http://kawarga.ru/Da-Da/DaDa.htm)
