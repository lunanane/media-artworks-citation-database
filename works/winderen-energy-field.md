# Energy Field

**Jana Winderen**, 2010 · Sound

A studio album (CD and digital, Touch TO:73, released April 2010) of three tracks, about 50 minutes, composed from hydrophone and microphone recordings Winderen made in the Barents Sea, Greenland and Norway, including inside glacier crevasses, in fjords and in the open ocean. Listeners hear layered underwater and shoreline sound, among it crustaceans and fish such as cod, haddock, herring and pollock, as well as wind, ravens and dogs. It won the Golden Nica for Digital Musics & Sound Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2011.

## In their words

> I am concerned with the invisible topography of the oceans; invisible, but audible; a soundscape which covers 70% of our planet.
>
> — Jana Winderen, [Ed Bendorf & Tobias Fischer, 'Interview with Jana Winderen', Tokafi (15 Questions), 2010](http://www.tokafi.com/15questions/interview-jana-winderen)

> I am fascinated by uncharted territory - there is not much generally known about sound communication in the oceans between fish, or how the inhabitants of the oceans orientate themselves with sound.
>
> — Jana Winderen, [Ed Bendorf & Tobias Fischer, 'Interview with Jana Winderen', Tokafi (15 Questions), 2010](http://www.tokafi.com/15questions/interview-jana-winderen)
