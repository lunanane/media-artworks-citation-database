# Soundtrack

**Guy Ben-Ner**, 2013 · Screen / monitor / Performance / body

Soundtrack is a color video with sound (11 min 12 sec) that takes eleven minutes of the soundtrack of Steven Spielberg's 2005 film War of the Worlds as a readymade and sets to it images shot in Ben-Ner's kitchen with his family. Ben-Ner and his two older children mouth the actors' lines while domestic actions such as blending a smoothie, swatting a fly, putting out a small kitchen fire or dropping a bottle of vodka follow the film's sound, intercut with internet footage from LiveLeak of the Israeli-Lebanese and Palestinian conflicts. It was shown at Aspect Ratio Gallery, Chicago, in 2013.
