# Résonances

**Louis-Philippe Rondeau**, 2021 · Sensor / interaction / Screen / monitor / Sound

Résonances is an interactive installation in which a visitor's gestures return as a visual and sound echo: earlier actions resurface and overlap with present ones, so the visitor can choreograph the present on top of their own past. It was unveiled in December 2021 in the CORPS DATA exhibition at Îlot Balmoral in Montreal with ELEKTRA, and shown at the 6th International Digital Art Biennial (BIAN) at Arsenal Contemporary Art Montreal; an outdoor edition was presented at i Light Singapore 2023 and Future Media FEST 2024 in Taipei.

## In their words

> RESONANCES is a metaphor for the passage of time. However, instead of expressing the linearity of time, this interactive installation evokes the way in which the actions of the present are overlapping with those of the past.
>
> — Louis-Philippe Rondeau, [RÉSONANCES (2021), project page on the artist's website PATENTEUX](https://patenteux.com/wp/portfolio/resonances-2021/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Like a visual and sound echo, RESONANCES represents our previous actions that resurface, amplifying the present, and becoming in turn past, like a perpetual temporal mise en abyme. This visual and sound loop allows the interactor to choreograph their present by building it on their own past, in a playful and singular way.
>
> — Louis-Philippe Rondeau, [RÉSONANCES (2021), project page on the artist's website PATENTEUX](https://patenteux.com/wp/portfolio/resonances-2021/)

<sub>says</sub>

> His research is focused on human representation and performance in a media-archeological context. It is inspired by a desire to reinterpret forgotten and overlooked forms of media, using contemporary digital tools.
>
> — Louis-Philippe Rondeau, [About text on the artist's website PATENTEUX](https://patenteux.com/wp/)
