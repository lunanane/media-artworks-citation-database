# Terminus

**Jess Johnson & Simon Ward**, 2018 · VR / AR / XR / Material / sculpture / Sound

Terminus is a virtual reality work in five parts built from drawings by Jess Johnson, animated by Simon Ward with developer Kenny Smith and soundtracks by Andrew Clarke. Commissioned by the National Gallery of Australia and The Balnaves Foundation, it opened at the NGA in May 2018, where each part was housed in its own architectural structure on a full-scale tessellated floor map. Wearing a headset, the visitor passes through five realms (Fleshold Crossing, Known Unknown, Scumm Engine, Gog & Magog, Tumblewych), selecting each by looking at a portal, with no hand controllers.
