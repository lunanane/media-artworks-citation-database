# Light and Belief

**Dinh Q. Lê**, 2012 · Screen / monitor / Text / print

Light and Belief: Sketches of Life from the Vietnam War is an installation commissioned by dOCUMENTA (13), Kassel, 2012, that pairs about 100 drawings and watercolours made by artists who served as military painters in the North Vietnamese Army with a 35-minute video of interviews Lê conducted with them. The video, punctuated by animated sequences, records the artists' recollections in relation to their drawings, which depict soldiers' portraits and everyday moments between battles. Sets of the work were later shown at the Carnegie International (2013), the Mori Art Museum (2015) and Asia Society, New York (2017).
