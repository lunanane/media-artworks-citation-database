# Everything

**David OReilly**, 2017 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative / Sound

Everything is a video game and nature simulation for PC, Mac, Linux, PlayStation 4 and later Nintendo Switch in which every thing the player sees, from animals and plants to planets and galaxies, can be played as a character; the player moves between scales from the microscopic to the cosmic, and can read the thoughts of other things. It has no tasks, objectives or scores, plays itself if left unattended, and includes recorded talks by the philosopher Alan Watts as narration. Its gameplay film received the 2017 Golden Nica in Prix Ars Electronica's Computer Animation/Film/VFX category and was shown in the CyberArts exhibition at OK Center in Linz.
