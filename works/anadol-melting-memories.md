# Melting Memories

**Refik Anadol**, 2019 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative / Network / data

Melting Memories is a project by Refik Anadol Studio comprising data paintings, augmented data sculptures and light projections made from EEG recordings of participants asked to focus on specific long-term memories, gathered with the Neuroscape Laboratory at the University of California, San Francisco. The data were processed with custom software (VVVV) and recurrent neural nets into moving forms shown on a 5 x 6 metre LED media wall, 65-inch OLED screens and CNC-milled foam pieces. It was exhibited at Pilevneli Gallery, Istanbul, from February to March 2018, and received the Lumen Prize Gold Award in 2019.

## In their words

> These are very fundamental questions in Archive Dreaming or Melting Memories. What will happen to our memories? Many of my artworks create these speculations.
>
> — Refik Anadol, [Alexandra Gilliams, 'Augmenting Perception: An Interview with Media Artist Refik Anadol', xibt magazine, October 2020](https://www.xibtmagazine.com/en/2020/10/augmenting-perception-an-interview-with-media-artist-refik-anadol/)

> What happens when we remember is Melting Memories and also what happens when we learn, what happens when we imagine, or dream? Can we catch these moments? These are “mind spaces.”
>
> — Refik Anadol, [Alexandra Gilliams, 'Augmenting Perception: An Interview with Media Artist Refik Anadol', xibt magazine, October 2020](https://www.xibtmagazine.com/en/2020/10/augmenting-perception-an-interview-with-media-artist-refik-anadol/)
