# Soak

**Yunsil Heo, Hyunwoo Bang**, 2011 · Sensor / interaction / Light

Soak is an interactive installation in which an elastic spandex canvas glows in a dark room; when visitors touch, poke, rub or lean their whole body into it, the surface stretches and fills with colours spreading as if the cloth were absorbing dye. The colour is a simulated watercolour computed by GPU-accelerated cellular automata (built with Processing and Kinect, per the artists' site). It was a Jury Selection in the 15th Japan Media Arts Festival Art Division.
