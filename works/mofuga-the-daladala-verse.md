# The Daladala Verse

**Aurelio Mofuga**, 2026 · VR / AR / XR

An XR project on Tanzania's daladala minibuses, developed in the S+T+ARTS4Africa residency hosted by OnaStories and shown at Ars Electronica 2024. The 2026 version is a 5-minute 360° immersive documentary for Meta Quest in which the viewer rides along with Fatuma, the only female conductor on the Mawasiliano–Masaki route in Dar es Salaam.
