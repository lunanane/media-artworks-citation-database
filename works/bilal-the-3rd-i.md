# The 3rd I

**Wafaa Bilal**, 2010 · Camera / computer vision / Performance / body / Online / net art

The 3rd I (3rdi) is a camera surgically mounted on the back of Wafaa Bilal's head that, from 15 December 2010 and for a planned year, took an image automatically once a minute and sent it via a laptop carried on his body and a 3G connection to the website 3rdi.me. Commissioned by Mathaf: Arab Museum of Modern Art, Doha, for the exhibition Told/Untold/Retold, it was also shown there as a three-room installation in which banks of screens display the images and respond to the number and movement of visitors.
