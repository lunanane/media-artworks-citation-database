# Endangered Gestures

**Bruce Eesly**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative / Text / print

A short film (4K single-channel video, 6'10'') and artist book (Small Book of Endangered Gestures) showing hand gestures generated with AI imaging for words and phrases that began disappearing from U.S. government websites, documents and archives in March 2025. Each censored word is paired with a proposed gesture made by hands that are almost but not quite human. It received a Prix Ars Electronica Honorary Mention (New Animation Art) and was shortlisted for the LUMA Rencontres Dummy Book Award in 2026.
