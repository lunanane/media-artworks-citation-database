# Dispatch

**Edward Robles**, 2018 · VR / AR / XR / Sound

Dispatch is a four-episode animated virtual-reality miniseries written and directed by Edward Robles at the studio Here Be Dragons, made in Unity with Oculus as financing partner, for the Oculus Rift and Gear VR. The viewer follows Ted, a small-town police dispatcher voiced by Martin Starr, through an all-night crime spree; the scenes on the other end of his calls are shown as sparse wireframe/vector graphics while most of the story is carried by voices and sound. The first episode debuted at the 2017 Venice International Film Festival and the last at the 2018 Sundance Film Festival (New Frontier).
