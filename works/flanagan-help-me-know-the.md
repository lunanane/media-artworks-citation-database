# \[help me know the truth\]

**Mary Flanagan**, 2018 · Camera / computer vision / Screen / monitor / Projection

\[help me know the truth\] is a networked software installation in which visitors take a selfie that is sent to tablets around the gallery. Using the reverse-correlation method from cognitive neuroscience, the software adds noise to the face and other visitors choose between two altered versions in response to prompts such as which face is more trustworthy or which is the terrorist. The face resulting from repeated choices is shown on a large projection.

> The intent behind \[help me know the truth\] is to question the accuracy of computational identification and facial recognition in the face of bias. It also exposes the categorizing systems of the mind, and how they are therefore subject to socially constructed fears and values. The work further calls into question notions of truth. Do we trust computers to categorize our physical appearances? Do we trust ourselves?
>
> — Mary Flanagan, [Artist's website, \[help me know the truth\]](https://maryflanagan.com/help-me-know-the-truth/)
