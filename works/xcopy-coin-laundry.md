# Coin Laundry

**XCOPY**, 2025 · Network / data / Material / sculpture

Coin Laundry is an installation by the pseudonymous artist XCOPY, presented on the Nguyen Wahed stand in the Zero 10 section of Art Basel Miami Beach in December 2025 and staged as a laundromat. According to the gallery, participants enter an ephemeral network through fungible tokens inscribed in code, in a collective performance planned to last a decade; the work was presented in partnership with Shape.

## In their words

> I think it’s really important to do everything I can to maintain the work I’ve put out into the world. You can mint new tokens, but you can’t truly replace the original, especially years later. I used to think of my NFTs as a chronological archive, but now I see them as more like a garden that needs to be maintained.
>
> — XCOPY, [Interview with Jason Bailey, Right Click Save (2022)](https://www.rightclicksave.com/article/an-interview-with-xcopy)
