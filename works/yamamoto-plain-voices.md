# plain voices

**Yoshihiro Yamamoto**, 2011 · Projection / Sound / Text / print

plain voices (Que voz feio) is an eight-minute two-channel video in which twin sisters, filmed in separate places, each recount in Portuguese the same incident from their early childhood, with subtitles. Shown on two screens at once, the two accounts differ slightly, and following one image, voice and subtitle means missing the other. It was shown at the Image Forum Festival 2011 and won the Grand Prize in the 15th Japan Media Arts Festival Art Division.
