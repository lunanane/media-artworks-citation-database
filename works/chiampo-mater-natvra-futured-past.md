# MATER NATVRA (FUTURED PAST series)

**Andrea Chiampo**, 2025 · Material / sculpture / Screen / monitor / Network / data

A 1/1 edition in digital and physical form, part of the FUTURED PAST series, commissioned for the Zero 10 section of Art Basel Miami Beach 2025 and shown on the Asprey Studio stand. A digitally sculpted figure of Mother Nature, whose placenta becomes a blind serpent coiled around her leg, was translated into sterling silver by Asprey Studio silversmiths. The digital artwork, issued on a blockchain, is an interactive catalogue in which viewers browse views of the sculpture and a twelve-step creation timeline.
