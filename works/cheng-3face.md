# 3FACE

**Ian Cheng**, 2022 · Online / net art / AI / generative / Network / data

An edition of 4,096 NFT artworks on the Ethereum blockchain, released through the platform Outland. Each 3FACE reads its owner's wallet and public transaction history, interprets it through a personality model of Nature, Nurture and Posture developed by Cheng, and renders a generative portrait; the owner can update the reading as the wallet accrues more history. MoMA and the Whitney Museum acquired editions in 2023.
