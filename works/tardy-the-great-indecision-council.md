# The Great Indecision Council

**Romain Tardy**, 2018 · Light / Sound / Network / data

The Great Indecision Council is a large-scale outdoor light and sound installation, a circle of light about 25 metres across with 12-channel sound, that translates in real time the most frequently searched terms on Google Search and Google News in the host country into words, visual pulses and computer-generated chants. Visitors step inside the circle while others watch from nearby. It was first produced for the Into The Great Wide Open festival on the island of Vlieland (NL) in September 2018 with MU art space, and received an Honorary Mention in Computer Animation at Prix Ars Electronica 2019.
