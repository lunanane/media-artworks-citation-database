# I Am Your Grandma

**Jillian Mayer**, 2011 · Online / net art / Screen / monitor / Performance / body

I Am Your Grandma is a short music video in which Jillian Mayer addresses her unborn grandchildren, appearing in a series of costumes, masks and make-up while she mouths a song repeating 'I am your grandma' (music by Michael John Hancock). It was first shown in her solo exhibition Family Matters at David Castillo Gallery, Miami, in April 2011, and then posted on YouTube, where it was watched millions of times. Wikipedia lists it as a short film made with Lucas Leyva.

## In their words

> I Am Your Grandma is an autobiographical video diary log that Mayer records for her unborn grandchildren. The work challenges notions of mortality, celebrity, and the universal impetus for creation and legacy. By placing the video in a public forum (Youtube) Mayer conducts a phenomenological study of why people ultimately share their personal feelings with anonymous strangers.
>
> — Jillian Mayer (artist's website), [I Am Your Grandma (2011), work page, jillianmayer.net](https://www.jillianmayer.net/i-am-your-grandma)
