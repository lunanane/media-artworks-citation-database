# Ebb and Flow (A Onda Traz, o Vento Leva)

**Gabriel Mascaro**, 2012 · Screen / monitor

Ebb and Flow is a 28-minute film produced by the Han Nefkens Foundation as part of a project of the ArtAids foundation, and shown at MACBA, Barcelona, 31 May to 4 June 2012. It follows Rodrigo, a young deaf man in Recife, northeast Brazil, who installs car stereos in a small dealership; a fixed camera stays close to his daily routine and his exchanges with others. Rodrigo is a fictional persona (played by Márcio Campelo Santana) and parts of the plot were scripted by Mascaro.
