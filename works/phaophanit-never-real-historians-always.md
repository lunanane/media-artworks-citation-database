# Never real historians, always near poets

**Vong Phaophanit and Claire Oboussier**, 2019 · Projection / Sound

A video projection installation commissioned for Singapore Biennale 2019 and shown at National Gallery Singapore. It centres on members of Phaophanit's family who made different decisions after the 1975 regime change in Laos, some leaving for France and others staying, with footage and interviews filmed in Savannakhet, Laos, and in St. Denis near Paris. The soundscape was composed by the artists' son, Savanh.

>
