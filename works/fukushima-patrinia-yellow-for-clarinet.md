# "patrinia yellow" for Clarinet and Computer

**Satoshi Fukushima**, 2014 · Sound / Performance / body

An eleven-minute composition and live performance for clarinet and computer expressing the annual cycle of the yellow patrinia (ominaeshi) plant. The sound consists of the clarinet, parts of the clarinet recorded in real time, and computer processing of those recordings, in three sections: clarinet and computer together, a computer solo, and clarinet and computer again. It premiered in Korea in 2013 and received an Excellence Award in the Art Division of the 18th Japan Media Arts Festival (2014).
