# MANTRAcks and Sonic Fields: A VirtuAural Duology

**Francisco López**, 2017 · Sound

A long-duration sound piece in two parts made from environmental recordings at two locations. Part 1, untitled #352, uses recordings from an urban boiler plant (RCU) in Fontenay-sous-Bois near Paris and was first developed as the audio-only soundtrack for Anne Collod's choreographic project Exposure, premiered for blindfolded audiences in 2017; part 2, Sonic Fields Vlieland, uses recordings from the North Sea island of Vlieland and was conceived to be listened to at geo-located positions on the island, as a listening walk produced by Soundtrackcity for the Into The Great Wide Open festival. The duology received an Honorary Mention in Digital Musics & Sound Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2019.

## In their words

> Unlike canonical ‘field recordings’ or ‘soundscapes,’ this piece moves forcefully away from representation or documentation and delves into a territory of ontological deep exploration of sonic substance.
>
> — Francisco López (Prix Ars Electronica catalogue text, no named author), [Prix Ars Electronica 2019, Digital Musics & Sound Art, Honorary Mention: catalogue text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/244246/)

> It explores a decidedly compositional sonic realm whose ultimate goal is not ‘abstraction’ but rather a much deeper connection to the matter of ‘reality.’ Not representational but immersive. An aural virtuality without simulation (or ‘VirtuAural’).
>
> — Francisco López (Prix Ars Electronica catalogue text, no named author), [Prix Ars Electronica 2019, Digital Musics & Sound Art, Honorary Mention: catalogue text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/244246/)
