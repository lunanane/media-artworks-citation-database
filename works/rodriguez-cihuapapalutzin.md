# Cihuapapalutzin

**Fernando Palma Rodríguez**, 2019 · Robotics / kinetics / Sensor / interaction / Material / sculpture

A swarm of 104 robotic monarch butterflies made from recycled aluminium beer and soft-drink cans, with electronics, mechanics and software. It was commissioned by the Toronto Biennial of Art and shown at 259 Lake Shore Blvd East in 2019. The butterflies are programmed to respond to seismic frequencies and are split into two groups of 52, which refers to a Mesoamerican ceremony that links the 365-day and 260-day calendars.
