# Momentum

**United Visual Artists**, 2014 · Light / Robotics / kinetics / Sound

Momentum is an installation of light, sound and movement commissioned by the Barbican Centre for its 90-metre-long Curve gallery in London, shown from 13 February to 1 June 2014. Visitors walk at their own pace through a darkened space in which 12 software-controlled pendulums with changing white lamps swing on two axes, sometimes stopping mid-swing, and project shadows and planes of light across the walls and curved floor, accompanied by a soundtrack.
