# Soft Collision

**Anna Schaeffner**, 2024 · Robotics / kinetics / Material / sculpture / Sensor / interaction

Soft Collision is a deformable pneumatic silicone membrane with embedded sensors that wraps a UR5 industrial robot arm. A person physically pushes and guides the arm to program its movement, then adjusts its trajectory through the sensors while it replays; air chambers inflate in short bursts as feedback. It was developed in the VOJEXT S+T+ARTS residency and shown with a performer.
