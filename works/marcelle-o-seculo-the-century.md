# O Século (The Century)

**Cinthia Marcelle & Tiago Mata Machado**, 2011 · Screen / monitor / Sound

A video of about nine and a half minutes (MASP lists 9'37") with a fixed view of a street. It begins with only the sound of people running; then objects of every sort are thrown into the frame until the street is filled with debris, dust and smoke, while the people involved stay out of view.

## In their words

> So, to me, both O Século and Rua de Mão Única are a Benjaminian branch of this research, like an archaeological dig used as a privileged entry point into the present.
>
> — Tiago Mata Machado, [Interview with Graham Domke, Biennale de l'Image en Mouvement, Geneva, 2016](https://2016.biennaleimagemouvement.ch/cinthia-marcelle-tiago-mata-machado/)

<sub>says</sub>

> If you think retrospectively, O Século was filmed two years before the protests in Brazil in June 2013, which is the closest we’ve come recently to having an insurrection in the country. O Século is the pre-June (I would even say the proto-June) and Rua de Mão Única is the post-June, when the Black Blocs had already entered the scene (in this case literally, given that we had invited them to take part in Rua de Mão Única).
>
> — Tiago Mata Machado, [Interview with Graham Domke, Biennale de l'Image en Mouvement, Geneva, 2016](https://2016.biennaleimagemouvement.ch/cinthia-marcelle-tiago-mata-machado/)

<sub>says</sub>

> So, to begin to answer your question, our films became more incendiary perhaps because we started admitting (formalizing) the violence in and around us. Brazil is a very violent country that is incapable of acknowledging its own violence.
>
> — Tiago Mata Machado, [Interview with Graham Domke, Biennale de l'Image en Mouvement, Geneva, 2016](https://2016.biennaleimagemouvement.ch/cinthia-marcelle-tiago-mata-machado/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> In this dialogue-confrontation with Tiago, I tend to test the limits of the disorder of creation; our joint works are less controlled.
>
> — Cinthia Marcelle, [Interview with Graham Domke, Biennale de l'Image en Mouvement, Geneva, 2016](https://2016.biennaleimagemouvement.ch/cinthia-marcelle-tiago-mata-machado/)
