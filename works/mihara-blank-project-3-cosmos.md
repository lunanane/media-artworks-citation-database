# Blank Project #3 Cosmos

**Soichiro Mihara**, 2016 · Material / sculpture / Robotics / kinetics

The third work in Mihara's Blank Project, begun after the 11 March 2011 Tohoku earthquake, built from moss, soil and electricity (the artist's site also lists mirror, spectral film and motor). In the Seoul version a moss-covered marble rolls around the exhibition hall; Mihara researched Korean moss on site and compared it with Japanese moss. At SeMA Biennale Mediacity Seoul 2016 it was shown at three museum branches on different dates.

## In their words

> Blank Project #3 Cosmos, a project started by the Great East Japan Earthquake, continues to create in-progress works on the theme of energy. The project explores the possibilities of future art, from the energy of the sun, which is the root of this theme, to the artistic application of microbial fuel cells, which are the equivalent of biological activity and electronic energy.
>
> — unattributed (artist's website), [Soichiro Mihara website, cosmos page](http://mhrs.jp/cosmos)
