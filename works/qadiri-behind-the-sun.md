# Behind the Sun

**Monira Al Qadiri**, 2013 · Screen / monitor

Behind the Sun is a 10-minute single-channel video commissioned by Beirut Art Center in 2013. It combines amateur VHS footage, filmed in 1991 by Adil Al Yousifi, of the Kuwaiti oil fields set ablaze during the retreat of invading forces after the first Gulf War with audio monologues from Islamic television programmes of the same period, taken from the Kuwait Television archives. It was shown in the Berlinale Forum Expanded in 2014.

## In their words

> The initial idea I had for Behind the Sun was to explore my biographical relationship with oil – petrol – in its most basic material form, and how that first encounter transformed my perception of my country's economy as well as my own identity as a Kuwaiti.
>
> — Monira Al Qadiri, ['Flash Futures', Monira Al Qadiri in conversation with Stephanie Bailey, Ibraaz, 8 May 2014](https://archive.ibraaz.org/interviews/126)
