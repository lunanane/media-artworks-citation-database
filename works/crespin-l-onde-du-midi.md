# L'Onde du Midi

**Elias Crespin**, 2020 · Robotics / kinetics / Material / sculpture

L'Onde du Midi is a permanent kinetic sculpture commissioned by the Musée du Louvre and inaugurated in January 2020 above the Escalier du Midi (Sully wing). It consists of 128 aluminium tubes suspended from nearly invisible cables driven by motors; computer-programmed sequences move the roughly 10-metre horizontal plane in slow undulations between 3 and 4.5 metres high. Visitors on the staircase watch the shifting configurations.

## In their words

> I use as little visual resources as as I can: simply the movement of the line, to explore the world of motion and matter moving in time.
>
> — Elias Crespin (interview by Fanny Revault), [ART Interview — Elias Crespin](https://www.artinterview.com/en/interview/elias-crespin/)

> I believe that I create “real virtual reality”; I imagine all these choreographies, which are then saved in the computer’s memory.
>
> — Elias Crespin, [ART Interview — Elias Crespin](https://www.artinterview.com/en/interview/elias-crespin/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

>
> — Elias Crespin, [ART Interview — Elias Crespin](https://www.artinterview.com/en/interview/elias-crespin/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> I think l’Onde du Midi is both a continuation of kinetic art and a break.
>
> — Elias Crespin, [ART Interview — Elias Crespin](https://www.artinterview.com/en/interview/elias-crespin/)

>
> — Elias Crespin, [Musée du Louvre press release (English version)](https://presse.louvre.fr/elias-crespinbr-londe-du-midibr-a-new-permanent-installationbr-for-the-louvre)
