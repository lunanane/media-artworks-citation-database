# Crossings

**Leila Alaoui**, 2013 · Screen / monitor / Sound

Crossings is a three-channel video installation (6 minutes 20 seconds per Galleria Continua) about sub-Saharan migrants who leave their home countries hoping to reach Europe, and about their passage through Morocco. Told from the migrants' point of view, it combines voice-over testimonies, still portraits and reconstructed video landscapes to recreate the sensations of the journey. It was shown at the 5th Marrakech Biennale in 2014, at the Ecole supérieure des arts visuels (ESAV), with which it was produced.

## In their words

> Throughout my adolescence in Morocco, stories of migrants drowning at sea became regular on the news. In my eyes, these stories were constant reminders of deep-rooted social injustice.
>
> — Leila Alaoui, [Nahrain Al-Mousawi, 'When we spoke to Leila Alaoui on tackling taboos in art', Al Jazeera, 19 July 2015](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2015/7/19/when-we-spoke-to-leila-alaoui-on-tackling-taboos-in-art)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> As a Moroccan, I felt very ashamed and felt some kind of responsibility to fight against the violent treatment of sub-Saharan migrants, who because of their “clandestine” status are deprived of basic human rights.
>
> — Leila Alaoui, [Nahrain Al-Mousawi, 'When we spoke to Leila Alaoui on tackling taboos in art', Al Jazeera, 19 July 2015](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2015/7/19/when-we-spoke-to-leila-alaoui-on-tackling-taboos-in-art)

> This experience nurtured the idea of an immersive audiovisual experience through which to share personal stories of migration and re-enact the disturbing sensations of the journey. I truly wanted to do a project that humanised and dignified the migrants.
>
> — Leila Alaoui, [Nahrain Al-Mousawi, 'When we spoke to Leila Alaoui on tackling taboos in art', Al Jazeera, 19 July 2015](https://www.aljazeera.com/features/2015/7/19/when-we-spoke-to-leila-alaoui-on-tackling-taboos-in-art)
