# A Performing by Flash, Afterimage, Velocity and Noise

**siren eun young jung**, 2019 · Screen / monitor / Sound / Performance / body

A multi-channel audiovisual installation with stereo and 5.1 surround sound, shown in the Korean Pavilion (History Has Failed Us, but No Matter) at the 58th Venice Biennale in 2019. It presents the performance of second-generation yeoseong gukgeuk male-role actor Lee Deung Woo together with four contemporary performers: electronic musician KIRARA, actor Yii Lee, performer and director Seo Ji Won, and drag king AZANGMAN. It is part of the artist's Yeoseong Gukgeuk Project (2008–).

## In their words

> Around 2015, I started to work with the younger generation of the LGBTQ community and with queer performers using Yeoseong Gukgeuk principles as our basis.
>
> — siren eun young jung, [Debaser (Platform) Magazine, interview by Renèe McMillan](https://www.platform-magazine.com/art/siren-eun-jung)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> Conventionally, the audience goes into a dark room. There is a flat screen there. The audience is expected to sit in that room and just gaze at the screen. They hear and see what the artist delivers. I always think, “That is not enough to change our society,” so I put more screens with images that quickly change so the audience cannot really see them. I want to make the audience feel uncomfortable in the room. Change is always uncomfortable for people.
>
> — siren eun young jung, [Debaser (Platform) Magazine, interview by Renèe McMillan](https://www.platform-magazine.com/art/siren-eun-jung)
