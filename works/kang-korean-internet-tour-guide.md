# Korean Internet Tour Guide

**E Roon Kang, Achim Koh and Wonyoung So**, 2016 · Online / net art / Text / print / Network / data

A research project commissioned by SeMA Biennale Mediacity Seoul 2016, consisting of a website (k-www.kr), a booklet and two related programs. The website lists "tourist attractions" collected from the Korean internet with related essays, starting from two interface software particular to Korea, ActiveX and Zeroboard; selected content was published as a book and displayed at the Biennale.
