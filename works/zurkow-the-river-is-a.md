# The River is a Circle

**Marina Zurkow**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative / Network / data

A software-driven generative animation made with James Schmitz and Blake Goble and shown on a large video wall on the Whitney Museum's fifth-floor terrace (Hyundai Terrace Commission, April 2025 – January 2026). It shows the Hudson River split between life above and below the water and changes by algorithmic probability with real-time weather, season, tides and marine traffic. The terrace also holds oyster reef balls and marine debris, and a special edit ran nightly on Times Square billboards in September 2025 as a Midnight Moment.
