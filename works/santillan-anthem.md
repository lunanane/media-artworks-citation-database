# Anthem

**Óscar Santillán**, 2025 · Sound / Sensor / interaction / AI / generative

Anthem is an installation of four suspended devices modelled on tree tumours (burls), 3D-printed and fitted with speakers, microphones and sound processors. It responds in real time to visitors' vocalisations with machine-generated animal-like calls. It was first shown at the centre of Santillán's exhibition ¿Qué se siente ser tierra? at the Centro de Arte Contemporáneo de Quito (2025–2026) and again at the 2026 Diriyah Contemporary Art Biennale.
