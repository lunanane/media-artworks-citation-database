# Kissing of the Threshold

**Fazal Rizvi**, 2025 · Sound / Material / sculpture / Text / print

Kissing of the Threshold (Urdu: آستان بوس) is a project by Fazal Rizvi centred on Shia traditions of mourning and remembrance, shown as a new commission at Sharjah Biennial 16 (2025). Dawn's review describes a large-scale installation with buckets of water, a mound representing a grave, and an eight-channel audio work in which the artist's voice recites soz; the Sharjah Art Foundation also published it as a two-part artist book of calligraphy and performative photographs referring to the Ghusaals in Pakistan, who wash and prepare the bodies of the dead.

## In their words

> Departing from a space of personal loss and collective grief, Fazal Rivzi's two-part publication project Kissing of The threshold آستان بوس (2024) centres Shia traditions of mourning and remembrance. In this artist book, Rizvi foregrounds the relations of care that guide the passage from grief to memorialisation, evoking the knowledge of Ghusaals in Pakistan, who tend to the bodies of those who have died and perform ghusl \[washing\] as part of the final rites.
>
> — unattributed (Sharjah Art Foundation publication page), [Sharjah Art Foundation, Fazal Rizvi: Kissing of the Threshold (publication)](https://www.sharjahart.org/en/resources/publications/details/fazal-rizvi-kissing-of-the-threshold)
