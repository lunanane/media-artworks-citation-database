# Subnormal Europe

**Óscar Escudero & Belenish Moreno-Gil**, 2021 · Performance / body / Sound / Screen / monitor

A 60-minute music theatre piece commissioned by the City of Munich for the Münchener Biennale and co-produced with the ZKM Hertz-Lab, performed by contralto Noa Frenkel and sound engineer Sebastian Schottke in an installation of three large screens, nine loudspeakers, MIDI-DMX lighting and a wooden cube. Its framing text presents it as an EU-commissioned project in which the performers create live replicas of moments from audiovisual media history, with an interview series published in parallel on Instagram. It received an Honorary Mention in Digital Musics & Sound Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2021.

## In their words

> Quoting the text of the agreement between the parties, “the main purpose of this initiative is the exact technical reproduction of those instants, which have marked our history of progress in the audiovisual field. The collection must be composed by a set of files, which may be exhibited together with their originals, not being able to distinguish one from the other.”
>
> — Óscar Escudero & Belenish Moreno-Gil (project text), [Catalogue text, Prix Ars Electronica 2021 archive](https://archive.aec.at/prix/255800/)

>

> The outcomes of the process will be eventually compiled and stored by the EU in order to fulfill the goals of the project, among them, and quoting again the award agreement: “to take the pulse of the current state of the European values and to reaffirm the sense of citizenship of its inhabitants, as well as the knowledge of our common history and its contributions to human development in difficult times for Europeanism.”
>
> — Óscar Escudero & Belenish Moreno-Gil (project text), [Catalogue text, Prix Ars Electronica 2021 archive](https://archive.aec.at/prix/255800/)
