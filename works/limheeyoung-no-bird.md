# No Bird

**UJOO+LIMHEEYOUNG**, 2026 · Robotics / kinetics / Sensor / interaction / Material / sculpture

A kinetic installation (265 x 265 x 150 cm) on a sand surface with two machines: a bird-like walking machine that leaves bird footprints in the sand, and a trace-erasing machine following behind that scratches out the tracks with a toothbrush, smooths them with a wire rake and flattens the surface with soft bristles. According to designboom, the movement is triggered by a sensor when a viewer comes close and runs for about two minutes while the viewer stays in range. The duo (Yoo Byoung-Joon and Lim Hee-Young, Seoul) received an Honorary Mention in Interactive Art + at Prix Ars Electronica 2026.

## In their words

> The origins of this work lay in imagining these now-vanished flightless birds. Perhaps they might have survived if their footsteps were erased?
>
> — UJOO+LIMHEEYOUNG, [No Bird, work description on the artists' blog, January 2026](https://u-joolimheeyoung.blogspot.com/2026/01/no-bird.html)

<sub>asks</sub>

> The repetitive cycle of these two machines—endlessly leaving and erasing—reveals a state in which action is continuously produced yet exhausted without leading to any change or direction.
>
> — UJOO+LIMHEEYOUNG, [No Bird, work description on the artists' blog, January 2026](https://u-joolimheeyoung.blogspot.com/2026/01/no-bird.html)

<sub>says</sub>

> This serves as a metaphor for the conditions of a post-automation society, where humans remain alive but exist as beings unable to intervene in the world.
>
> — UJOO+LIMHEEYOUNG, [No Bird, work description on the artists' blog, January 2026](https://u-joolimheeyoung.blogspot.com/2026/01/no-bird.html)

> What vanishes is not only the flightless bird. What is disappearing is the human role, no longer summoned in a post-automation society, along with the conditions of meaning and dignity that humans have given to themselves.
