# Interlooped

**Maria Guta, Robin Mange, Javier Bello Ruiz, FlexFab**, 2019 · VR / AR / XR / Camera / computer vision / Performance / body

Interlooped is a multi-user mixed-reality installation and live performance in which the visitor, wearing a VR headset, is captured in real time as a low-resolution volumetric (voxel) hologram by depth-sensor cameras, using technology by the Geneva company Imverse. The visitor's movements and those of performer Maria Guta are recorded and replayed as time loops, so the virtual room fills with multiple versions of both; in a second part Guta joins live, dressed as in the recordings. It premiered in New Frontier at the Sundance Film Festival in January 2019.
