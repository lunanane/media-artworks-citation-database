# Island Waters

**KangLi**, 2025 · Projection

A 60-second animated video projection-mapped onto the facade of CHIJMES at the Singapore Night Festival 2025 (22 Aug-6 Sep), commissioned by the National Heritage Board and made by illustrator and motion designer Lim KangLi in the festival's Projection Mapping Masterclass. The animation moves from a ripple down to the seabed, up through maritime routes and back to the surface, with colours drawn from Peranakan porcelain.

## In their words

> This year, we were each tasked to create a 60 second animated video to the theme of Island Nights, a reflection of what it means to be islanders in spite of the rush of city life.
>
> — Lim KangLi, [Lim KangLi, 'Singapore Night Festival 2025 – Projection Mapping', artist website](https://limkangli.net/nightfest-projection-mapping/)

<sub>says</sub>

> “Island Waters” invites viewers on an immersive exploration of underwater realms, highlighting the profound connections between human life and our oceans. The journey begins with a simple ripple, then plunges into the seabed’s depths, ascends through bustling maritime pathways, and finally resurfaces.
>
> — Lim KangLi, [Lim KangLi, 'Singapore Night Festival 2025 – Projection Mapping', artist website](https://limkangli.net/nightfest-projection-mapping/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Inspired by the vibrant hues of Peranakan porcelain, this artwork celebrates the rich diversity of local marine flora and fauna. It also emphasises the dynamic interconnectivity inherent in island life, weaving together threads from the past, present, and future, showcasing the vital role of water in our shared existence and cultural heritage.
>
> — Lim KangLi, [Lim KangLi, 'Singapore Night Festival 2025 – Projection Mapping', artist website](https://limkangli.net/nightfest-projection-mapping/)
