# Awakening

**Tororo.aoi**, 2025 · Projection

A projection mapping work shown on the facade of CHIJMES at the Singapore Night Festival 2025 (22 Aug-6 Sep), made by Tororo.aoi (Asher Yeo) as part of the festival's third Projection Mapping Masterclass, led by the Malaysian collective Filamen. According to the festival, it takes Singapore's island and port history as its subject, starting from a metaphorical dive into a shipwreck.

## In their words

> Awakening delves into Singapore's unique island history, sparked by an epiphany and a metaphorical dive into a shipwreck. The artwork explores the folkloric dimensions of this little island nation, examining the significant role of its port — a destiny foisted upon it by historical currents.
>
> — Singapore Night Festival 2025 (programme text), [Singapore Night Festival 2025 programme page (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20251015060459/https://www.heritage.sg/sgnightfest/whats-on/awakening)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> More profoundly, Awakening serves as a heartfelt celebration of the diverse people and vibrant cultures that have been drawn to Singapore's shores by the unstoppable tides of trade. It's a contemplative piece that reflects on the past to illuminate the present identity, emphasising the intricate tapestry woven by human movement and commercial exchange.
>
> — Singapore Night Festival 2025 (programme text), [Singapore Night Festival 2025 programme page (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20251015060459/https://www.heritage.sg/sgnightfest/whats-on/awakening)
