# Lost in Trans(mission)

**Jackie Karuti**, 2015 · Screen / monitor

Lost in Trans(mission) is a 2015 video by the Nairobi-based artist Jackie Karuti. It was shown in her first solo exhibition, Labyrinth, at Kuona Trust Arts Centre in Nairobi in January 2015, together with the installation Exit (a door that opens onto a blank wall) and mixed-media paintings of figures behind prison bars. The video and the door installation were later included in the group exhibition Entry Prohibited to Foreigners in Sweden.

## In their words

> I started paying close attention to what people were telling me, I was more conscious of what they were feeling, having good conversations where friends shared their feelings. Some were stuck in their jobs, others depressed. The people who were feeling this way seemed to multiply. There are so many people going through a tough time or trapped by circumstances.
>
> — Jackie Karuti, [Zihan Kassam, 'Jackie Karuti', Africanah.org (2015)](https://africanah.org/jackie-karuti/)
