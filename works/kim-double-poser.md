# Double Poser

**Heecheon Kim**, 2023 · Screen / monitor

Double Poser is a 38-minute auto-played game made with the Unity game engine (4k, colour, stereo sound), commissioned by the Hayward Gallery, London, and Art Sonje Center, Seoul, and shown in the Hayward's HENI Project Space from 1 December 2023 to 7 January 2024. Reinterpreting components of Kim's Cutter III (2023), it takes the Hayward's brutalist architecture as a starting point and combines computer-generated imagery, video game aesthetics and skateboarding culture. Visitors watch it as a video in the gallery.

## In their words

> I’m not as interested in the specific software or applications as much as the society that is obsessed with them
>
> — Heecheon Kim, [ArtReview, “Heecheon Kim: Keeping it Real”, Harry C. H. Choi, 25 March 2024](https://artreview.com/heecheon-kim-keeping-it-real/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> A large part of my work deals with the fact that these latest technological developments generate real-time experiences in the first person. From this point of departure, an array of questions follows: ‘Who am I? What is time? How do we make sense of the world?’
>
> — Heecheon Kim, [ArtReview, “Heecheon Kim: Keeping it Real”, Harry C. H. Choi, 25 March 2024](https://artreview.com/heecheon-kim-keeping-it-real/)

<sub>asks · about their practice</sub>

> In the imaginary worlds that are created in games, the task of the players is to create their own narratives as they navigate the ups and downs of their surroundings. But this is no different in the real world: what we are asked to do is make our own stories given the inherent restrictions that are set forth in the beginning of our lives.
>
> — Heecheon Kim, [ArtReview, “Heecheon Kim: Keeping it Real”, Harry C. H. Choi, 25 March 2024](https://artreview.com/heecheon-kim-keeping-it-real/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> It was only by the tail end that I realised that these mishaps from the game engine could all be a part of the work.
>
> — Heecheon Kim, [ArtReview, “Heecheon Kim: Keeping it Real”, Harry C. H. Choi, 25 March 2024 (on Cutter III, 2023)](https://artreview.com/heecheon-kim-keeping-it-real/)
