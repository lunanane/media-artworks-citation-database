# Hinat

**Daniah Al-Saleh**, 2022 · Projection / AI / generative / Material / sculpture

A multi-panel installation made during an AlUla residency in 2022, named after Hinat, a Nabataean woman whose tomb (Tomb 117) is at Hegra. Painted and photo-transferred collages of AlUla landscapes have rectangles cut out, into which videos are projected; the videos, generated with machine learning from footage of three women from AlUla wearing coloured fabrics, show ghostly figures moving across the landscapes. It was shown in 'Art of the Kingdom' at the Saudi Arabian Museum of Contemporary Art.
