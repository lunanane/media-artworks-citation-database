# Angles Mirror

**Daniel Rozin**, 2013 · Robotics / kinetics / Camera / computer vision / Material / sculpture

Angles Mirror is a wall-mounted triangular steel sculpture holding 465 yellow plastic spokes, each turned by a motor and driven by a video camera, control electronics and custom software. When a viewer stands in front of it, the spokes rotate to angles that trace the contours and movement of the viewer's figure rather than light and dark; when the viewer moves away, the surface runs through predefined images and transitions. It was shown in Rozin's solo exhibition Angles at bitforms gallery, New York, in 2013 and received an Honorary Mention in Interactive Art at the Prix Ars Electronica 2013.

## In their words

> My main interests in my art are image creation and perception, and viewer participation. Interactive mirrors offer an ideal platform to experiment with both these ideas.
>
> — Daniel Rozin, ['Daniel Rozin: The art of interaction', interview by Arnau Salvadó, Metal Magazine, September 2015](https://metalmagazine.eu/post/daniel-rozin-the-art-of-interaction-arnau-salvado)

> Some works are about the material and use the intrinsic qualities of that material such as wood, rust, and fur. In other cases, the material is merely a necessity to fabricate the piece and in those cases the materials are more neutral such as plastic and steel.
>
> — Daniel Rozin, ['Daniel Rozin: The art of interaction', interview by Arnau Salvadó, Metal Magazine, September 2015](https://metalmagazine.eu/post/daniel-rozin-the-art-of-interaction-arnau-salvado)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> Giving people an active role in the creation of the piece means that certain aspects of the piece remain undefined and unfinished until the moment of viewing. That is a great price to pay for an artist, but for me the reward is worth it, as the participation of people is the reason I make the art.
>
> — Daniel Rozin, ['Daniel Rozin: The art of interaction', interview by Arnau Salvadó, Metal Magazine, September 2015](https://metalmagazine.eu/post/daniel-rozin-the-art-of-interaction-arnau-salvado)

>
