# Home

**Tayeba Begum Lipi**, 2014 · Screen / monitor / Performance / body

Home is a two-channel (diptych) video installation drawn from the artist's extended conversations with Anonya (also spelled Anonnya), a transgender woman from Dhaka. It was made in Dhaka in 2014 for the group exhibition Cross Casting, curated by Mahbubur Rahman at Britto Arts Trust, an artist-run space Lipi co-founded. Her meeting with Anonya while making the video led to the 2015 exhibition Reversal Reality (Shrine Empire, New Delhi), which compared the two women's childhoods through video, photographs and objects.
