# Anxious Body

**Yoriko Mizushiri**, 2022 · Screen / monitor

Anxious Body is a 5'47 2D digital animated short without dialogue, commissioned by Towada Art Center and produced by Miyu Productions and New Deer (2021), with sound by Yuka C. Honda. It shows fingers, cellophane tape and a dispenser, a mechanical pencil, tennis racket strings, a sponge and a snake-like creature touching, pulling and pressing against one another. It received an Award of Distinction in Computer Animation at Prix Ars Electronica 2022.

## In their words

> It's a tactile animation work, so I'm happy if you can feel something in your body being evoked, like ‘I understand this movement,’ or ‘I've felt this before.’
>
> — Yoriko Mizushiri, [quoted in Landmarks (University of Texas at Austin) video-art text by Kanitra Fletcher](https://landmarks.utexas.edu/video-art/anxious-body)
