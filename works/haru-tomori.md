# TOMORI

**Misa Haru**, 2026 · VR / AR / XR / Performance / body

A 20-minute mixed reality installation in which the visitor, seated on a physical chair in a small room, takes the place of Shizuku, a young worker undergoing hypnosis with the therapist Ayano, and looks through virtual windows and gaps in the room into scenes of a teenage family life and Tomori's past. The room's windows and crevices require visitors to change position and posture to follow the story. Made as a master's thesis project at Tokyo University of the Arts (GEIDAI GAMES 07, March 2026), it was selected for the Venice Immersive competition at the 83rd Venice International Film Festival (2026).
