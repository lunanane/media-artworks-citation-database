# Le Miroir

**Camille Sauer**, 2025 · Projection / Performance / body

Le Miroir is an experimental film produced at Le Fresnoy – Studio national des arts contemporains, in which the artist interacts with her own virtual double, a MetaHuman generated from her appearance, in mental landscapes structured around figures inspired by psychoanalytic archetypes (Ego, Superego, Id, Self). It combines real-time 3D animation, motion capture, photogrammetry, miniature sets and filmed sequences, with a soundtrack of synthesizers and electroacoustic composition. It was shown in Le Fresnoy's Panorama 27 and received an Honorary Mention in New Animation Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2026.
