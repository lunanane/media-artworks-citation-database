# fugue notes

**Selina Ershadi**, 2025 · Projection / Sound / Text / print

fugue notes is a film (HD digital video and 16mm, with sound) in which the artist's original plan for a film is replaced by its notes: in deep shadow and darkness, a voiceover links eroding autobiographical memory with the failed image. It was commissioned by CIRCUIT for Leap to the Place of Two Pools at Te Pātaka Toi Adam Art Gallery, Wellington (2025), and was later screened at Artspace Aotearoa in 2026 with the artist reading the script live.
