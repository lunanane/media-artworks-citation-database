# Difficult Love

**Zanele Muholi**, 2010 · Screen / monitor

Difficult Love is a 48-minute documentary, commissioned by the South African Broadcasting Corporation (SABC) and co-directed by Zanele Muholi and Peter Goldsmid, about Muholi's life and work and the lives of Black lesbians in South Africa. It is built from interviews with Muholi, their family, partner and friends, and with people who appear in Muholi's photographs, who speak about their lives, relationships and experiences of violence. It was shot in 2010.
