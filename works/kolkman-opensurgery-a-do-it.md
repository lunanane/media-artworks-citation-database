# OpenSurgery - A do-it-yourself surgery robot for domestic laparoscopy

**Frank Kolkman**, 2015 · Robotics / kinetics / Material / sculpture

OpenSurgery is a do-it-yourself robot-assisted surgery system for keyhole (laparoscopic) surgery at home, built from 3D-printed and laser-cut parts, hacked surgical instruments and components bought online, with electronics taken from 3D-printer designs, for about $5,000. The prototype moves but is not stable enough for surgery; at the exhibition it ran through a randomised series of movements. It was developed as a graduation project at the Design Interactions department of the Royal College of Art, London, in 2015.
