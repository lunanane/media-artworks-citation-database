# Believe me

**Addie Wagenknecht**, 2017 · Online / net art / Screen / monitor

A web-based work commissioned by the Whitney Museum for artport, made in collaboration with Leander Herzog. In the browser, the visitor sees a series of images (political scenes, interface elements, internet folklore, among them the American flag) overlaid with the visual effect of a cracked, glitching screen; clicking shifts the fractured image and refreshing the page loads a new one. The title takes the two words Donald Trump used most often, according to a 2017 analysis by sociolinguist Jennifer Sclafani for CNN.

>
> — Addie Wagenknecht, [⌘F: Addie Wagenknecht, bitforms gallery (email interview, April–May 2020)](https://www.bitforms.art/news/cmd-f-addie-wagenknecht)
