# Quando o segundo Sol chegar / Um cometa nos teus olhos

**Janaina Wagner**, 2025 · Projection

A two-channel video installation, the third film of a trilogy made on the Transamazônica highway (BR-230), shown in MASP's Video Room. It shows scenes of the highway under a fleeting glow that suggests a 'second Sun' approaching Earth, set against a text that tells a physical and existential journey. The project began during a residency at the Centro Cultural do Cariri awarded at the 22nd Bienal Sesc\_Videobrasil.

## In their words

> In today's reality, where the climate crisis of the Capitalocene and the Solastalgia (a kind of existential suffering caused by environmental changes) derived from it operate as imminent announcements of a catastrophe, the arrival of a Second Sun touches on the anguish of the future that is so present in the conversations and imaginations of contemporary people: fear and delirium
>
> — Janaina Wagner, [Videobrasil, Residency Prize Cariri Cultural Center \| 22ª Bienal (news)](https://site.videobrasil.org.br/en/news/2247012)
