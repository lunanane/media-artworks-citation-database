# Skeleton of the Glorious

**Khalid Zahid**, 2025 · Light / Material / sculpture

Skeleton of the Glorious is a light installation by Khalid Zahid in the gardens of the King Abdulaziz Historical Center for Noor Riyadh 2025. It is a hollow, wireframe-like structure based on Al-Masmak Fort, the adobe and clay citadel of old Riyadh. It is lit from within, so the inner form is visible while the exterior falls into shadow.
