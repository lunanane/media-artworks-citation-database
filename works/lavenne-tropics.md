# TROPICS

**Mathilde Lavenne**, 2018 · Screen / monitor / Camera / computer vision / Sound

TROPICS is a black-and-white animated film about a farm in the Jicaltepec area of Veracruz, Mexico, settled by French farmers in the 19th century; Lavenne made it from point clouds captured with a FARO 3D laser scanner during a months-long residency at the Casa Proal foundation, with the FabLab team of Anáhuac Norte University. The viewer moves through scanned landscapes and scanned local people while recorded voices tell their stories and memories. It won the 2018 Prix Ars Electronica Golden Nica for Computer Animation.

## In their words

> So I had a kind of dream, that of Mexico whose secret force is the one of its landscapes, its volcanoes, its deserts, and its natural and sacred magic. It has confronted the West, which still today exercises its economic and cultural domination over the world.
>
> — Mathilde Lavenne, [Interview by Martin Hieslmair, Ars Electronica Blog, 31 August 2018](https://ars.electronica.art/aeblog/en/2018/08/31/tropics/)

> I chose to let the people in the area speak and not speak for them.
>
> — Mathilde Lavenne, [Interview by Martin Hieslmair, Ars Electronica Blog, 31 August 2018](https://ars.electronica.art/aeblog/en/2018/08/31/tropics/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Personally, I had the feeling that, by showing these clouds of points, we could make visible the invisible structures that constitute us. And at the same time make perceptible the strata of memory: the ones that remain in us in our cells and those outside us that haunt the territories.
>
> — Mathilde Lavenne, [Interview by Martin Hieslmair, Ars Electronica Blog, 31 August 2018](https://ars.electronica.art/aeblog/en/2018/08/31/tropics/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Black and white was also imposed to reinforce the ambiguity of space time and this ghostly presence that the film transmits.
>
> — Mathilde Lavenne, [Interview by Martin Hieslmair, Ars Electronica Blog, 31 August 2018](https://ars.electronica.art/aeblog/en/2018/08/31/tropics/)

<sub>says</sub>

> TROPICS draws an orbit around a Mexican farm. Scattered voices seem to revive and disturb the memory of the place. Crossing the matter, the film attempts to stop time and men, and reveals the ghost of a lost paradise.
>
> — Mathilde Lavenne, [Work description, mathildelavenne.com](https://www.mathildelavenne.com/t-r-o-p-i-c-s/)
