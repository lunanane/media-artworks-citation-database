# SMOG SEASON

**Witaya Junma**, 2023 · Sensor / interaction / Robotics / kinetics / Network / data

SMOG SEASON is an installation of 80 cigarette packs mounted on 5 motor-driven circular bases, using Bangkok PM 2.5 air-pollution data and the cigarette-equivalence figure from Berkeley Earth (one cigarette per 22 micrograms per cubic meter per day). Visitors scan a QR code, choose a smoke level on their smartphone and hold it to a light sensor; the bases spin and lights blink at 18 FPS so the cigarette-label pictures appear animated, while an LED display shows the date, time and PM 2.5 value. It received an Honorary Mention in Prix Ars Electronica 2023 (New Animation Art).

## In their words

> SMOG SEASON invites audiences to realize the bad effect of PM 2.5 they are living in and to start questioning about the problem. Nowadays people start to misunderstand and try to adapt themselves to stay with the problem just like it is a normal weather condition. If we count the problem of PM 2.5 as a season, it would be a very bad monsoon that people must suffer and endure to pass.
>
> — Witaya Junma, [Witaya Junma, artist website, 'Smog Season'](http://www.witayajunma.com/smog-season1.html)

<sub>says</sub>

> "What do we breathe in?" The work wishes to expand on the point of PM 2.5 in Thailand. It is expanding into a chronic problem that is not solved. The work wants to stimulate the thoughts so that everyone returns to look at this problem seriously and question on the right to receive clean air for breathing.
>
> — Witaya Junma, [Witaya Junma, artist website, 'Smog Season'](http://www.witayajunma.com/smog-season1.html)

<sub>asks</sub>

> The interesting thing is that this problem does not occur only in Thailand. Many countries around the world are facing air pollution problems, and people must directly take on the burden of the consequences.
>
> — Witaya Junma, [Witaya Junma, artist website, 'Smog Season'](http://www.witayajunma.com/smog-season1.html)

<sub>says</sub>

>
