# Thank you for your souvenir, UK!

**Oushi Lin (Los)**, 2024 · Screen / monitor

A three-minute animated short film about a Chinese international student's year of study in the UK, in which identical closed-eyed ragdoll figures are pushed through the system. It received an Honorary Mention in the New Animation Art category of Prix Ars Electronica 2024.
