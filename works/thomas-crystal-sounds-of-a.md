# Crystal Sounds of a Synchrotron

**Jo Thomas**, 2012 · Sound

A 38-minute surround-sound (5.1) work composed from frequencies generated by the electron storage ring of Diamond Light Source, the UK's synchrotron, mostly the sounds of electron injections into the accelerator, combined with binaural recordings made in its experimental hall, storage ring and beamlines. It was commissioned and published on DVD by Diamond Light Source and is designed both for concentrated listening and for installation, where listeners can enter at any point. It received the Golden Nica for Digital Musics & Sound Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2012.

> Crystal is designed both for concentrated listening and also for installation. One can walk in at any time and capture phrases, or simply sit and listen to the whole work.
