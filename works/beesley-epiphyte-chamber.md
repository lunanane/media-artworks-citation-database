# Epiphyte Chamber

**Philip Beesley**, 2014 · Material / sculpture / Robotics / kinetics / Sensor / interaction

Epiphyte Chamber is an installation of linked masses, described as 'floating islands', hung from above. Each is built from thousands of lightweight, digitally fabricated components that move together in subtle, coordinated, nearly synchronised 'breathing and whispers'. Visitors walk into enclosed sculptural spaces among them, which connect to larger gathering areas. Philip Beesley Architect Inc. (PBAI) made it for the inaugural Aleph Exhibition at the Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Seoul (12 November 2013 - 16 March 2014). It received an Honorary Mention for Interactive Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2014.
