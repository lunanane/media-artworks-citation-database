# Evaporating Symphony

**Wing Po So**, 2025 · Sound / Material / sculpture / Robotics / kinetics

An installation of 3D-printed metal stones on elevated stainless steel panels at varying heights, heated through heat conductors, thermal sensors, relays and a thermal controller, with roller pumps and water pipes delivering droplets of water. Each droplet that touches a stone evaporates in under a second with a soft percussive sound, which is amplified in the space. It was shown at the 13th Seoul Mediacity Biennale, Séance: Technology of the Spirit, Seoul Museum of Art (2025).
