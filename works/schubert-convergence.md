# Convergence

**Alexander Schubert**, 2020/21 · Performance / body / AI / generative / Sound

Convergence is a 35-minute piece for string ensemble and AI system, made with Ensemble Resonanz, that exists as a staged version (premiered in Bonn, October 2020; revised in Dresden, 2023) and a digital-only streamed version (premiered February 2021, commissioned by Eclat Festival). Deep-learning models (auto-encoders and GANs, with audio programming by IRCAM researchers) learn features of the musicians' faces, bodies and voices, and the performers, guided by a computer voice, interact with the generated counterparts of themselves. It won the 2021 Prix Ars Electronica Golden Nica for Digital Musics & Sound Art.
