# The Flower Field

**Imhathai Suwatthanasilp**, 2012 · Material / sculpture / Light

An installation of thousands of small flowers hand-crocheted from human hair – hair of cancer patients, survivors and donors in the Hair for Hope charity programme – arranged on a lightbox made from a repurposed bed frame, under glass sheets and lit by LEDs. It was conceived for the Hair for Hope exhibitions in Bangkok in 2012; The Nation's report on 'Hair for Hope: The New Beginning' at the Ardel Gallery of Modern Art describes 2,000 hair flowers resting in an enclosed light bed. The work is in the Singapore Art Museum collection.
