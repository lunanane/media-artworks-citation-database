# Micro-Nano Landscape

**Suxuan Jiang**, 2026 · Projection / Screen / monitor / Robotics / kinetics

A video work built from high-magnification microscope recordings of magnetically controlled micro-nano robotic collectives: magnetic microparticles that, under a dual-axis oscillating magnetic field, self-assemble into clusters, grow, tilt and move together. The footage, which the catalogue states is not animated or simulated, is enlarged to projection scale and presented as landscape- and calligraphy-like imagery. Made by Suxuan Jiang with research collaboration from Jiangfan Yu and support from the Shenzhen Institute of Artificial Intelligence and Robotics for Society (AIRS) SciArt Co-lab; Honorary Mention, New Animation Art, Prix Ars Electronica 2026.
