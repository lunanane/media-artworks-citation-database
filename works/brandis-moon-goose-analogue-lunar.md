# MOON GOOSE ANALOGUE: Lunar Migration Bird Facility

**Agnes Meyer-Brandis**, 2012 · Performance / body / Screen / monitor / Network / data

The Moon Goose Analogue: Lunar Migration Bird Facility builds on Francis Godwin's 17th-century story The Man in the Moone, in which a man flies to the Moon in a chariot towed by 'moon geese'. The artist raised eleven geese from birth at Pollinaria in Italy, gave them astronauts' names, imprinted them on herself as goose-mother, trained them to fly and housed them in a remote Moon analogue habitat. In the gallery, visitors meet a control-room installation with consoles, a live stream from the habitat, instructional videos, photographs and vitrines of the geese's egg shells and footprints.
