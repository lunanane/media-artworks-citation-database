# Nothing to See Here

**Celine Daemen**, 2025 · Camera / computer vision / Sound / Sensor / interaction

Nothing to See Here is an interactive viewing box by Celine Daemen and Studio Nergens, installed in public spaces such as train stations. A visitor looks through stereoscopic spyholes in a mirrored cube and sees a live 3D image of themselves from above, peering into the box, while fictional figures (a confused man, a wolf, a plastic bag) and a spatial soundscape act around this double. A prototype was shown at Cultura Nova in Heerlen station in 2025 and the world premiere was at IDFA DocLab in November 2025.
