# Screen Melancholy

**Li Yi-Fan**, 2026 · Screen / monitor / Material / sculpture / AI / generative

Screen Melancholy (2026) is a video installation by Li Yi-Fan, presented by the Taipei Fine Arts Museum as Taiwan's collateral event at the 61st Venice Biennale in the Palazzo delle Prigioni, curated by Raphael Fonseca. In the video, Li performs a monologue through a game-engine digital character in the tone of an online tutorial, addressing subscription-based image software and AI-generated images. Large sculptures of fragmented limbs are placed throughout the space; visitors can sit on them and charge their phones from chargers built into the sculptures.

## In their words

> Within the counterbalance of exhaustion, tension and collision, a narrative slowly emerges. In contemporary image production, artists inevitably rely on lots of different software tools. Some are open-source and free, while others are highly commercial. Each choice represents a different possibility for expression, but also has its own limitations and controls. I move among these systems, trying to grasp the subtle relationship of dependence and resistance between artists and contemporary tools. Through this process, using these image production techniques, I contemplate the state of humankind in relation to technological media.
>
> — Li Yi-Fan, [Taipei Fine Arts Museum announcement, via photography-now.com, 'Screen Melancholy'](https://photography-now.com/exhibition/176751)
