# I.AM.ISIGO Digital Mystery System

**Bubu Ogisi**, 2024 · Online / net art / Network / data / Material / sculpture

A digital tool by Bubu Ogisi's label I.AM.ISIGO that archives knowledge of African weaving techniques gathered through research with artisan communities, and lets users create new weaving patterns. It was shown in the S+T+ARTS exhibition at Ars Electronica Festival 2024 in the context of the STARTS4Africa project.

## In their words

> A digital environment is a natural reflection of a physical environment. I think of a Sahelian network of wares on camels as an embodied network of hardware connected to each other through movement. When scaled up, this network is an internet of sorts, with growth through connectivity and the build up of organic social networks.
>
> — Bubu Ogisi / I.AM.ISIGO (project statement), [Ars Electronica Festival 2024, project page](https://ars.electronica.art/hope/?p=13421)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> We aim to document previously inaccessible practices surrounding weaving traditions, gain critical understanding of how these materials are processed and used, archive these for future use, make weaving more approachable, bring the knowledge of these techniques to a global audience, unearth ancient systems of governance, challenge the concept of a “finished” good, give a voice to traditional fabrics of Africa, and research the entire supply chain for each of these material knowledge systems.
>
> — Bubu Ogisi / I.AM.ISIGO (project statement), [Ars Electronica Festival 2024, project page](https://ars.electronica.art/hope/?p=13421)
