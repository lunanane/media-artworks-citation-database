# Chijikinkutsu

**Nelo Akamatsu**, 2015 · Sound / Material / sculpture

A sound installation made of water, sewing needles, glass tumblers and coils of copper wire. Magnetized needles float on the water in the tumblers and align north-south with the earth's magnetic field; when current is switched to a coil on the outside of a tumbler, the temporary magnetic field draws the needle against the glass, producing a faint sound, with the switching sequenced by MIDI from an iPad app through custom controllers. The title combines "chijiki" (geomagnetism) and "suikinkutsu", an Edo-period sound device for Japanese gardens; the work received the Golden Nica for Digital Musics & Sound Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2015.

## In their words

> While utilizing the action of geomagnetism normally treated as a subject of science, this sound installation expands the subtle sounds of "Suikinkutsu" in the context of Japanese perspective on Nature.
>
> — Nelo Akamatsu, [Chijikinkutsu, work text on the artist's portfolio site](http://www.neloakamatsu.jp/chijikinkutsu-eng.html)

<sub>says</sub>

> The concept of the work "Chijikinkutsu" does not derive from experimentalism of science and technology on which media arts rely, nor from architectural theory of western music upon which some sound arts lay their foundation.
>
> — Nelo Akamatsu, [Chijikinkutsu, work text on the artist's portfolio site](http://www.neloakamatsu.jp/chijikinkutsu-eng.html)

<sub>says</sub>

> I did not include many elements into the work, but aimed to create a minimal expression like a painting with a large unpainted margin.
>
> — Nelo Akamatsu, [Chijikinkutsu, work text on the artist's portfolio site](http://www.neloakamatsu.jp/chijikinkutsu-eng.html)

<sub>says</sub>

> The smaller the sounds of the glasses will be, the more keenly viewers' sensibility will be sharpened. In the meantime, they realize that the sounds don't come from outside of their bodies, but already exist inside of their mind.
>
> — Nelo Akamatsu, [Chijikinkutsu, work text on the artist's portfolio site](http://www.neloakamatsu.jp/chijikinkutsu-eng.html)

<sub>says</sub>

> I did not add unnecessary elements such as many colors, LED lights, amplified sound etc., so that the acoustic sounds would stand out and viewers could concentrate on listening to them.
>
> — Nelo Akamatsu, [Catalogue text, Prix Ars Electronica 2015 (Golden Nica, Digital Musics & Sound Art)](https://archive.aec.at/prix/212289/)
