# Grosse Fatigue

**Camille Henrot**, 2013 · Screen / monitor / Sound / Text / print

Grosse Fatigue is a 13-minute colour video with sound in which overlapping computer windows show footage filmed in the collections of the Smithsonian Institution in Washington, D.C., mixed with images found on the internet and scenes filmed in places such as a pet store and a domestic interior. Over the images a spoken-word poem, written with Jacob Bromberg and voiced by Akwetey Orraca-Tetteh, combines scientific history with creation stories from religious, hermetic and oral traditions. It was made during a Smithsonian Artist Research Fellowship and presented at the 55th Venice Biennale, Il Palazzo Enciclopedico, in 2013.
