# Behind Shirley

**Ibiye Camp**, 2022 · Screen / monitor / VR / AR / XR / Material / sculpture

An installation made in 2020 consisting of a video-essay, two 3D-printed busts and an augmented reality application. It addresses the 'Shirley cards' used to calibrate skin tones in colour photography and the failure of current facial recognition software to register darker skin. It was shown at the 5th Istanbul Design Biennial (2020) and in the exhibition Digital Shadows at OBA Oosterdok, Amsterdam (until 26 Feb 2023), and received an Honorary Mention in Interactive Art + at Prix Ars Electronica 2022.
