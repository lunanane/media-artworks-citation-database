# Añoranzas (Yira Yira)

**Joaquín Aras**, 2023 · Projection / Material / sculpture

Añoranzas (Yira Yira) is an installation with 16mm colour film made by recycling celluloid combs, screened with a 16mm film projector. It refers to Argentine film pioneer Federico Valle, whose surviving films, after a 1926 fire, were sold to a comb factory that used the celluloid as raw material. The work was realised with a 2023 CIFO-Ars Electronica Award and shown at the LENTOS Kunstmuseum during Ars Electronica Festival 2023.

## In their words

> The project attempts to reverse this cultural loss through experimentation and the recycling of plastic combs, transforming them into film for projection.
>
> — Joaquín Aras (project text on the artist's website), [joaquinaras.com — Añoranzas (Yira Yira) (translated from Spanish)](https://joaquinaras.com/proyectos/anoranzas-yira-yira/)

> to reverse this cultural loss of Valle's by doing the inverse process: instead of the films becoming combs, turning the combs into film
>
> — Joaquín Aras (to EFE, Linz), [Forbes México / EFE, 'Museo en Austria dedica muestra al arte experimental de América Latina' (translated from Spanish)](https://forbes.com.mx/forbes-life/arte-y-cultura-museo-en-austria-dedica-muestra-al-arte-experimental-de-america-latina/)

> For many years in Argentina people combed their hair with what had been Valle's films, one of our great cinematic pioneers. It is a story that is endearing and sad at the same time
