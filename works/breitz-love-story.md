# Love Story

**Candice Breitz**, 2016 · Screen / monitor / Performance / body

Love Story is a seven-channel video installation based on day-long interviews with six people who fled their countries and were seeking or had been granted asylum in Berlin, New York or Cape Town. In a first room, actors Alec Baldwin and Julianne Moore re-perform fragments of the interviews on a green-screen set in a fast-cut montage; a second room, reachable only through the first, shows the six original interviews in full.

## In their words

> I wanted Alec and Julianne to almost bait you into a relationship with the stories
>
> — Candice Breitz, [Six Stories, Twice Retold, interview by Haley Weiss, Interview Magazine, 2017](https://www.interviewmagazine.com/art/candice-breitz)

<sub>says</sub>

> Why it is that identification or empathy is afforded only to those who have access to the same kinds of individuality that we already value?
>
> — Candice Breitz, [Six Stories, Twice Retold, interview by Haley Weiss, Interview Magazine, 2017](https://www.interviewmagazine.com/art/candice-breitz)

<sub>asks</sub>

> So one of the early impetuses to the work was a desire to individuate and to get at or to give a platform to the particularity or the richness of individual stories, and that meant at the beginning speaking to lots and lots of different people and spending long stretches of time getting to know people.
>
> — Candice Breitz, [Six Stories, Twice Retold, interview by Haley Weiss, Interview Magazine, 2017](https://www.interviewmagazine.com/art/candice-breitz)

<sub>says</sub>

> One of the things that interested me very much when I started to speak to and consider different people for the work was the way in which as someone is arriving, and this is not just for refugees but for any migrant, you're constantly expected to rehearse your narrative, and narrate your story in order to narrate yourself into legitimacy.
>
> — Candice Breitz, [Six Stories, Twice Retold, interview by Haley Weiss, Interview Magazine, 2017](https://www.interviewmagazine.com/art/candice-breitz)
