# Honorata

**Mercedes Nasta**, 2025 · Sound

Honorata is a 15-minute multichannel song conceived as a lullaby, commissioned by MUAC for its Espacio de Experimentación Sonora in Mexico City, where it was shown from 11 October 2025 to April 2026. Visitors listen to Nasta's voice with bass, viola, guitar, Rhodes piano, Hammond organ and synthesisers, spatialised across the room's 22 channels by Tobías Álvarez. The piece was composed during Nasta's pregnancy and finished a few months after the birth.

> When they invited me to the project I was pregnant, and I had my baby in January. I explained that I was in a very introspective process, taking great care of myself, and that this was going to directly inform the song
>
> — Mercedes Nasta, [Roberto Frías, "Una canción hipnótica que arrulla el tiempo, un pop onírico", Gaceta UNAM, 16 Oct 2025 (translated from Spanish)](https://www.gaceta.unam.mx/una-cancion-hipnotica-que-arrulla-el-tiempo-un-pop-onirico/)
