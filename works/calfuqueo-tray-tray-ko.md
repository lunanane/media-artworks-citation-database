# TRAY TRAY KO

**Seba Calfuqueo**, 2022 · Screen / monitor / Performance / body

A six-minute HD video performance in which the artist, wearing or pulling a long electric-blue fabric, moves through a forest towards a river and enters it, dragging the soaked fabric upstream until disappearing beneath a waterfall. The title refers to the trayenko (waterfall) in Mapuche cosmology, linked to lawen, medicinal herbs that grow near water; the video ends with a poem in Mapuzungun and Spanish. It was shown at the 2024 Whitney Biennial and is in the collection of the Block Museum, Northwestern University.

## In their words

> the trayenko is a vital and sacred space to many of the Mapuche people’s practices; the flow of the water, especially of the trayenko, is of utmost importance and is linked to the lawen—medicinal herbs that grow near water bodies.
>
> — Seba Calfuqueo, [Quoted in "Collection Spotlight: Seba Calfuqueo, Tray Tray Ko", Stories From The Block (Block Museum, Northwestern University), 2025](https://nublockmuseum.blog/2025/03/11/collection-spotlight-seba-calfuqueo-tray-tray-ko/)
