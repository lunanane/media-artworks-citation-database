# We Are All Made of Light

**Maja Petrić**, 2019 · Light / Camera / computer vision / Sound

An immersive installation of projectors, spatial sound, AI recognition software and over 63,000 reflective woven threads, first shown at MadArt Studio, Seattle (6 Oct – 1 Dec 2018). The system detects visitors' bodies, records their silhouettes and projects them back into the space as star-like light trails, which remain after they leave and accumulate with the trails of earlier visitors. The technical system was developed with Microsoft researcher Mihai Jalobeanu; it received the Lumen Prize 3D/Interactive Award in 2019.

> — Maja Petrić, [Artist's statement as quoted in "We Are All Made of Light", Art & Electronic Media (artelectronicmedia.com)](https://artelectronicmedia.com/en/?p=2414)
