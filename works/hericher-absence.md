# Absence

**Marc Hericher**, 2022 · Screen / monitor

Absence is a 3D-animated short film (10 min per the artist, 12 min per the producer Autour de Minuit), in French with English subtitles. An old homeless man collapses alone in a square; passers-by avoid, ignore or help him, until journalists take an interest and he becomes the centre of an escalating media event. It received an Award of Distinction in Computer Animation at Prix Ars Electronica 2022.
