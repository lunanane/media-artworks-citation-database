# Mourning Class: Nollywood

**Zina Saro-Wiwa**, 2010 · Screen / monitor / Performance / body

Mourning Class: Nollywood is a video installation, the first in Saro-Wiwa's Mourning Class series of video performances about mourning rituals. Nigerian actresses sit in close-up before the camera, shoulders bare and heads covered, and cry real tears when prompted by the artist; Wikipedia adds that each breaks the performance with a smile. It was first presented in 2010 in Sharon Stone in Abuja, an exhibition on Nollywood that Saro-Wiwa co-curated at Location One gallery in New York, and has been shown as both a single-channel and a multi-channel work.
