# but that is not the important part of the story

**Dineo Seshee Bopape**, 2013 · Material / sculpture / Screen / monitor / Sound

An installation made for the 12th Biennale de Lyon (2013) and shown at the Musée d'art contemporain de Lyon; according to the Biennale guide it was first created as a performance in South Africa before being sent to Lyon. Wikipedia describes it as made up of wooden beams draped in white fabric, electrical cables, screens, rear-view mirrors, a small fan and sound recordings. It was shown again in 2014 in Ruffneck Constructivists, curated by Kara Walker, at the Institute of Contemporary Art, Philadelphia.
