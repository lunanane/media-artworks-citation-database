# VFRAME: Visual Forensics and Metadata Extraction

**Adam Harvey**, 2017 · Camera / computer vision / AI / generative / Network / data

VFRAME (Visual Forensics and Metadata Extraction) is a research project and open-source computer-vision toolkit by Adam Harvey for human rights researchers and investigative journalists who work with large archives of conflict-zone video. It began in 2017 as a prototype using object detection to locate illegal munitions in footage of the Syrian conflict, was developed with the Syrian Archive and Mnemonic.org into a visual search engine for million-scale video datasets, and from 2019 used 3D-modelled synthetic training images, for example of the AO-2.5RT cluster munition, to train detectors for objects with few real examples. It received an Award of Distinction in Artificial Intelligence & Life Art at the Prix Ars Electronica 2019.

## In their words

> The idea for VFRAME grew out of discussions at a 2017 Data Investigation Camp organized by Tactical Technology Collective in Montenegro. Through meeting with investigative journalists, human rights researchers, and digital activists from around the world it became clear that computer vision was a much needed tool in this community yet the solutions were nowhere in sight.
>
> — VFRAME (project website, by Adam Harvey), [About, vframe.io](https://vframe.io/about/)
