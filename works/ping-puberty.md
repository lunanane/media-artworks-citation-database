# puberty

**Wong Ping**, 2022 · Screen / monitor / Material / sculpture

puberty is an installation commissioned for the Simon Lee Foundation Institute of Contemporary Asian Art at the Art Gallery of Western Australia, shown in the gallery foyer from 22 July to 11 December 2022. It combines animation on an iPad with cast acrylic, chain, fans and synthetic turf, forming a face whose eyes, nose, tongue and mouth are described by the venue as moving elements.
