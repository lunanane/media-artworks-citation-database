# The Innocence of Unknowing

**Ryat Yezbick, Milo Talwani**, 2025 · AI / generative / Camera / computer vision / Performance / body

The Innocence of Unknowing is an AI archival project, two-channel essay film and live performance examining U.S. news media coverage of mass shootings since the 1960s, studying footage of people retreating. In the performance the artists question an AI model, trained on humanities texts, about its readings of the archive footage, shown alongside computer-vision analyses such as object and pose detection. It had its world premiere at Tribeca Immersive 2025 ('In Search of Us').
