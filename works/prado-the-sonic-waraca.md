# The Sonic Waraca

**Paola Torres Núñez del Prado**, 2023 · Sound / Sensor / interaction / Material / sculpture

The Sonic Waraca is a textile controller based on the waraca, an Andean sling, made for the Textile Museum of Canada's Gathering project with the curatorial collective Mending the Museum, in response to Peruvian textiles in the museum's collection. It works as part of a quadraphonic sound system: its position shifts sound among the speakers and modifies recorded sounds from protests in Peru. Online it is presented as an audio piece.
