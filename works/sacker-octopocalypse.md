# Octopocalypse

**Max Sacker, Arite Szadkowski**, 2026 · Sensor / interaction / AI / generative / Sound

Octopocalypse is an immersive installation for three participants, who enter an interrogation room after a fictional meteor storm and face an alien octopus that communicates through music, touch, rhythm and visions. With questions, button presses and rhythm the group shapes the outcome and builds a music track from impulses sent by the alien, choosing whether to interrogate it or earn its trust. It premiered in the Venice Immersive competition in 2026; the makers say they used generative AI as a production tool.
