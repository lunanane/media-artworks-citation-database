# Solwata

**Felix Gaedtke, Gayatri Parameswaran**, 2026 · VR / AR / XR / Sensor / interaction

Solwata is a 25-minute mixed reality installation, narrated by Mark Ruffalo, about rising seas and planned relocation in the Solomon Islands, co-produced by Human Rights Watch, Agog: The Immersive Media Institute and NowHere Media and shown in Venice Immersive 2026. Participants interact with water and artifacts and, through 360° video, Gaussian splats and 3D animation, join community members Martha, Francis and Lawrence. It was made with residents of Walande, Ngongosila and Kwai.

## In their words

> The ocean that’s consuming their world is the same ocean that connects us all. We created this mixed reality installation to collapse that distance.
>
> — Felix Gaedtke, Gayatri Parameswaran (NowHere Media), [La Biennale di Venezia, Venice Immersive 2026 — Main Creators' Statement (NowHere Media - Felix Gaedtke, Gayatri Parameswaran)](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2026/venice-immersive/solwata)

<sub>asks</sub>

> One moment from our trip stays with us – diving underwater to see the remains of the submerged island of Walande, which was once home to a thriving community, now entirely displaced due to rising sea levels. It was sobering, and struck us deeply: this could be all our futures.
>
> — Felix Gaedtke, Gayatri Parameswaran (NowHere Media), [La Biennale di Venezia, Venice Immersive 2026 — Main Creators' Statement (NowHere Media - Felix Gaedtke, Gayatri Parameswaran)](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2026/venice-immersive/solwata)
