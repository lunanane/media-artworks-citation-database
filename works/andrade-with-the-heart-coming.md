# With the Heart Coming Out of the Mouth (Com o coração saindo pela boca)

**Jonathas de Andrade**, 2022 · Material / sculpture / Robotics / kinetics / Screen / monitor

With the Heart Coming Out of the Mouth was Brazil's presentation at the 59th Venice Biennale (2022), curated by Jacopo Crivelli Visconti. Built on an index of more than 200 Brazilian idiomatic expressions that name body parts, it combines sculptures, photographs and a video; visitors enter through an outsized ear and leave through another, and at intervals a large fabric heart drops from a mouth in the ceiling and inflates to fill the second room. The expressions are captioned in Portuguese, English and Italian.

> My project, With the heart coming out of the mouth, is anchored in an extensive collection of popular expressions that are metaphors of the body.
>
> — Jonathas de Andrade, [ArtReview — 'An Anatomy Fair': Jonathas de Andrade on Representing Brazil at the 59th Venice Biennale](https://artreview.com/an-anatomy-fair-jonathas-de-andrade-on-representing-brazil-at-the-59th-venice-biennale/)

>
> — Jonathas de Andrade, [ArtReview — 'An Anatomy Fair'](https://artreview.com/an-anatomy-fair-jonathas-de-andrade-on-representing-brazil-at-the-59th-venice-biennale/)
