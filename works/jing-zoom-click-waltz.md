# Zoom, Click, Waltz

**Sarah Choo Jing**, 2020 · Screen / monitor / Performance / body

Zoom, Click, Waltz is a multimedia installation of 13 LED screens, each showing a person or people in a separate apartment window, dancing or not. It began during Singapore's 2020 Covid-19 'Circuit Breaker' lockdown, when the artist mailed letters to neighbours in her building asking them to dance by their living-room windows at a set time; the recordings are stitched together with staged, found and borrowed footage. It was shown at Gillman Barracks, Singapore (21 January - 7 February 2021) and won the 2021 Lumen Prize 3D/Interactive Award.
