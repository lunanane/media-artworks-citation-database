# Live Study

**Michael Mandiberg**, 2019–2024 · Online / net art / Performance / body / Screen / monitor

Live Study is an online durational performance commissioned by the Whitney Museum for its artport platform, in which a live video stream (2019–2024, now an archive) showed Michael Mandiberg painting portraits of the people who had worked for the artist over the years. Each canvas is sized at one square inch per hour the person worked; many portraits were painted twice, one priced at the total wages Mandiberg paid the subject and the other given to the subject. Visitors to the artport site browse the archived livestream videos of the painting sessions alongside the 100 resulting paintings and studies.

## In their words

> I'm making this labor visible, this hidden labor that no one wants to talk about
>
> — Michael Mandiberg, [Casey Lesser, "Watch an Artist Paint Every Assistant They've Ever Had", Artsy, 30 July 2019 (reposted by Denny Gallery)](https://dennygallery.com/news/michael-mandiberg-in-artsy/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Those big, grand scenes that showed the work itself being made—but are ultimately about cultivating the myth of genius. I'm trying to find a way of deconstructing that, in the process of representing process.
>
> — Michael Mandiberg, [Casey Lesser, "Watch an Artist Paint Every Assistant They've Ever Had", Artsy, 30 July 2019 (reposted by Denny Gallery)](https://dennygallery.com/news/michael-mandiberg-in-artsy/)

<sub>says</sub>

> I find that this kind of work, I can't do for more than five hours at a time, because it requires such physical and mental concentration—and I am learning, right?
>
> — Michael Mandiberg, [Casey Lesser, "Watch an Artist Paint Every Assistant They've Ever Had", Artsy, 30 July 2019 (reposted by Denny Gallery)](https://dennygallery.com/news/michael-mandiberg-in-artsy/)

<sub>asks</sub>

> I was doing client design work at the time to fund projects, so I took these semi-obligations and turned them into opportunities for my students to build portfolio material. A lot of this work is represented in Live Study and the portraits depict my work and the amount of time I spent with them.
>
> — Michael Mandiberg, [CSI Today (College of Staten Island), "CSI Professor Michael Mandiberg Showcases Groundbreaking Work at the Whitney", 15 April 2026](https://csitoday.com/2026/04/csi-professor-michael-mandiberg-showcases-groundbreaking-work-at-the-whitney/)
