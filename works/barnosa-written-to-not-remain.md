# Written To Not Remain

**Tewa Barnosa**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / VR / AR / XR / Text / print

Written To Not Remain is a 12'30" video work about writing on walls across post-revolution Libya since 2011. It combines archival imagery from a photo archive of over 200 images of wall writings that Barnosa began collecting in 2019 with acts made inside a VR simulation and 3D spatial scans, alongside a text recitation. It is listed among the S+T+ARTS Prize 2025 winners (Ars Electronica).

## In their words

> Some of the works that I’ve made in the last few years – such as Written to not remain and Teneri Ara Assensu / The sahara is not a hotel – were instantaneous. It was a matter of, “I can’t let go of this”. There’s an urgency around it.
>
> — Tewa Barnosa, [Canvas (Canvas 117: The Maghreb Issue), interview with Tewa Barnosa](https://canvasonline.com/tewa-baronsa/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> Written To Not Remain (12’30) is based on a visual investigation looking into the acts of writing on the walls across post-revolution Libya. positioning collective statements as ephemeral evidence of contemporary and historical dialogue, social commentary, war correspondences, or often silent protests.
