# Justin

**Ang Song Ming**, 2012 · Text / print / Projection

Over three months Ang Song Ming practised copying the signature of pop musician Justin Bieber, then signed Bieber's autograph on a poster of the musician. The installation consists of 34 practice sheets, a slide projector showing 80 35mm slides, and the signed, framed poster, documenting the change from the artist's own handwriting to Bieber's. The work is in the Singapore Art Museum collection.
