# Nuestra Victoria (Our Victory)

**Julieta Gil**, 2019–2020 · Camera / computer vision / Screen / monitor / Online / net art

A series of works built on a 3D digital archive of Mexico City's Angel of Independence monument. Gil documented the monument with drone footage and photogrammetry after feminist protests against violence towards women in August 2019, before it was boarded up and restored. The record is shown as video (in one installation on two 55-inch screens, commissioned for Storefront TV) and as photographic prints, and the 3D files can be downloaded.

## In their words

> The work consists of a 3D digital archive that intends to preserve the data of the monument after its occupation by civil society.
>
> — Julieta Gil, [Nuestra Victoria, project text on the artist's website](https://julietagil.com/Nuestra-Victoria)

<sub>says</sub>

> The intention was not to make a perfect replica, but to also allow the materiality of this digital version to show through and reveal new layers of information about its process of preservation and restoration, and allow the mistakes and imperfections of the scan to serve as a metaphor for the fragility of patriarchal institutions.
>
> — Julieta Gil, [Nuestra Victoria, project text on the artist's website](https://julietagil.com/Nuestra-Victoria)

<sub>says</sub>

> Hours after the protest, government censorship prevented people from seeing the Angel of Independence by boarding it up and immediately working on its restoration, thus erasing all the claims against violence that were posted by women all over the monument. This work allows the words and actions of protest to remain in our collective memory.
>
> — Julieta Gil, [Lumen Prize Gold Award winner 2020, work description](https://lumenprize.com/2020-winners/julieta-gil)
