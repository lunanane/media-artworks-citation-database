# Compose

**Yuko Mohri**, 2024 · Sound / Material / sculpture / Robotics / kinetics

Yuko Mohri's exhibition for the Japan Pavilion at the 60th Venice Biennale (20 April to 24 November 2024), curated by Sook-Kyung Lee, made of two site-specific installations built mostly from materials found locally. In Moré Moré (Leaky), the artist creates water leaks in the pavilion and catches them with household goods, with pumps circulating the water through a large kinetic sculpture; rain can enter through the open ceiling. In Decomposition, electrodes in local fruit turn their changing moisture into signals that modulate drone sounds and lights as the fruit ripens and decays, after which it goes to compost.

> — Yuko Mohri, [Interview by Ryo Sawayama, Tokyo Art Beat, 18 Jun 2024](https://www.tokyoartbeat.com/en/articles/-/sook-kyung-lee-yuko-mohri-venice-biennale-interview-en-202406)

> For me, creating a circuit is very important. It is about using water and electricity, communicating with people, and using invisible elements.
