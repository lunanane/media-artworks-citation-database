# Paper Tears

**Claudia Pagès Rabal**, 2026 · Light / Screen / monitor / Performance / body

An installation of light, sound, choreography and image for Catalonia in Venice, a collateral event of the 61st Venice Biennale (Docks Cantieri Cucchini, 9 May–22 November 2026), curated by Elise Lammer for the Institut Ramon Llull. Four lasers project 15th-century paper watermarks from the archive of the Museu Molí Paperer de Capellades, a sculptural LED screen shows drone footage of four aquifer springs, and performers dressed as jesters deliver monologues in a script structured like a waltz, with a soundtrack. Visitors walk around and onto platforms to change their point of view.

## In their words

> I didn’t want to address these issues in a moralistic or reactionary way, which is why I chose the watermark as a medium: a margin on the page, a space open enough to play, struggle, and even bring joy.
>
> — Claudia Pagès Rabal, [Fakewhale Log, 'Fakewhale in Dialogue with Claudia Pagès Rabal', 15 May 2026](https://log.fakewhale.xyz/fakewhale-in-dialogue-with-claudia-pages-rabal/)

<sub>says</sub>

> The watermarks I’ve chosen date back to the 15th century, a century marked by the expulsion of Jews and Arabs from the Iberian Peninsula and the colonization of the Americas. I probably chose this period to help me cope with the violence we see every day in today’s world.
>
> — Claudia Pagès Rabal, [Fakewhale Log, 'Fakewhale in Dialogue with Claudia Pagès Rabal', 15 May 2026](https://log.fakewhale.xyz/fakewhale-in-dialogue-with-claudia-pages-rabal/)

<sub>says</sub>

> The figure of the jester and the use of humor seemed to me the only starting point from which we could approach this material without viewing it through a moral lens, and instead work with it from a place of joy.
>
> — Claudia Pagès Rabal, [Fakewhale Log, 'Fakewhale in Dialogue with Claudia Pagès Rabal', 15 May 2026](https://log.fakewhale.xyz/fakewhale-in-dialogue-with-claudia-pages-rabal/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Just as the watermarks navigate in the negative and are only visible against the light, the water in the aquifers is also latent and only becomes visible when it is exploited, extracted.
>
> — Claudia Pagès Rabal, [Fakewhale Log, 'Fakewhale in Dialogue with Claudia Pagès Rabal', 15 May 2026](https://log.fakewhale.xyz/fakewhale-in-dialogue-with-claudia-pages-rabal/)

<sub>says</sub>

> My works are time machines because they travel from the past to the present and back again.
>
> — Claudia Pagès Rabal, [Fakewhale Log, 'Fakewhale in Dialogue with Claudia Pagès Rabal', 15 May 2026](https://log.fakewhale.xyz/fakewhale-in-dialogue-with-claudia-pages-rabal/)
