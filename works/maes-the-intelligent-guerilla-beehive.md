# The Intelligent Guerilla Beehive

**AnneMarie Maes**, 2017 · Material / sculpture / Sensor / interaction / Network / data

A bio-art installation and research project: a beehive designed for urban environments to shelter swarming bee colonies, whose outer membrane, the 'Sensorial Skin', is a fabric combining organic and electronic elements in which bacteria change colour in response to pollution. It grew out of Maes's Brussels rooftop Bee Laboratory, where observation beehives with monitoring technology produced data on bee behaviour, and her Laboratory for Form and Matter, where she works with propolis, chitin, fungi and bacteria. It was shown at Ars Electronica in 2017, where it received an Honorary Mention in Hybrid Art.

## In their words

> The project is a collaboration with the non-human other. Honeybees and bacteria become interfaces to be informed about the health status of our environment. Bees and bacteria become living monitoring technology.
>
> — AnneMarie Maes (artist's website), [The Intelligent Guerrilla Beehive, project page, annemariemaes.net](http://annemariemaes.net/projects/the-intelligent-guerilla-beehive/)
