# Species series

**Yang Wonbin**, 2012 · Robotics / kinetics / Material / sculpture

Species series is a project of autonomous robots conceived as new species that are born, live and die in the city, built from mass-produced everyday objects and waste combined with electronics and mechanics. The robots are released into urban spaces, where - as the Japan Media Arts Festival jury describes - they are run over by cars, stepped on or knocked from their moorings, and the project records their histories. It was shown as an installation of mixed media and video at the Sullivan Galleries, Chicago, in 2012 and at SIGGRAPH Asia 2012, and received the New Face Award in the 16th Japan Media Arts Festival Art Division.

## In their words

> In my work, a series of artificial life forms such as autonomous kinetic objects and robotic creatures are created to have birth, life, and death in varying environments.
>
> — Yang Wonbin, [Species Series, Simultan festival archive (2014)](https://archive.simultan.org/works/species-series/)
