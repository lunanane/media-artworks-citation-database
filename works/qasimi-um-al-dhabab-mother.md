# Um Al Dhabab (Mother of Fog)

**Farah Al Qasimi**, 2023 · Screen / monitor / Online / net art

A two-channel video (29 minutes 45 seconds) shot across locations in Ras Al Khaimah and shown at Sharjah Biennial 15 in the Khorfakkan Art Centre. Through the perspectives of the ghost of an Al Qasimi pirate and a British naval officer, the film recounts the 1819 siege of Al Dhayah fort and the subsequent treaty, using TikTok-style graphics and internet aesthetics. Visitors watch from floral-patterned bean bags.
