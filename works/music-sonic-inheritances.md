# Sonic Inheritances

**Singing Wells (Ketebul Music and Abubilla Music)**, 2025 · Sound / Screen / monitor

A listening space commissioned by Sharjah Art Foundation and Bergen Assembly, shown at Sharjah Biennial 16 (Bait Al Serkal, 6 February – 15 June 2025) and then at the fifth Bergen Assembly. It presents recordings, interviews and performance videos from Singing Wells' archive of East African music. Singing Wells was founded in 2011 by Nairobi's Ketebul Music and London's Abubilla Music, and travels to villages with a mobile recording studio.

## In their words

> Our goal is to record, archive and share the traditional music of East Africa for two important reasons – to sustain and celebrate the extraordinary cultural music heritage of the region and to help make this legacy relevant and fresh to today’s audiences.
>
> — Singing Wells, [Singing Wells, 'Mission'](https://www.singingwells.org/mission/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> As a group of sound and video engineers, producers and musicians, we set out on this mission not to become ‘fossil collectors’ and store the recordings in inaccessible archives. We work with musicians to make sure their music traditions continue to be practiced, can be shared amongst the widest audiences and become a source of inspiration for new musicians.
>
> — Singing Wells, [Singing Wells, 'Mission'](https://www.singingwells.org/mission/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> Singing Wells can’t be just about archiving. We can’t be fossil collectors. We also have to make sure we can bring these traditions into modern music.
>
> — Steve Kivutia, Ketebul Music, [Singing Wells, 'What We Do'](https://www.singingwells.org/what-we-do/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> We are committed to supporting traditional music performance in rural East Africa as we believe that this not only serves to promote and preserve the cultural heritage of a community but is also a key indicator of the sustainability of that community.
>
> — Singing Wells, [Singing Wells, 'What We Do'](https://www.singingwells.org/what-we-do/)
