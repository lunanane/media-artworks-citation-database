# tele-present water

**David Bowen**, 2011 · Robotics / kinetics / Network / data / Material / sculpture

tele-present water is a kinetic installation in which a lattice of wooden rods hangs from nylon threads and is moved by seventeen servo motors controlled by a computer. The motors are driven by wave intensity and frequency data from a National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration data buoy in the Pacific (station 51003, or station 46246 in the 2011 WRO version), so that visitors see the grid reproduce the movement of water at that distant location. A version driven by wave data recorded on Lake Superior was also made in 2011.
