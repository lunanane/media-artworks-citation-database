# Raster und Spectrum

**Kim Asendorf**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative / Network / data

Raster und Spectrum is an onchain abstract real-time animation that can play on any device, shown by Nguyen Wahed in the Zero 10 section of Art Basel Miami Beach 2025. Two brushes — a raster of white lines on black and a spectrum that continuously cycles through hues — cross the screen in one of eight directions and return when they meet an edge.
