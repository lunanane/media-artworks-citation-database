# SKY/WORLD DEATH/WORLD

**Amelia Winger-Bearskin**, 2022 · Online / net art / Text / print / Screen / monitor

A two-part Whitney artport commission for the Sunrise/Sunset series (Dec 2022–Apr 2023) that played on whitney.org at sunrise and sunset in New York. SKY/WORLD pairs game-engine animations of dawn, drawing on the Haudenosaunee story of Sky Woman, with the question "Who benefits from your burnout?"; DEATH/WORLD pairs abstract visuals and video of the artist recovered from the Internet Archive with "What is made bright by the loss of your light?". Viewers can click a switch to change the animation while answering text populates the screen.
