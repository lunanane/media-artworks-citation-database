# biko.fanon

**Nolan Oswald Dennis**, 2018 · Text / print / Network / data

biko.fanon is an installation of receipt printers and a micro-controller that print texts from the writings of Steve Biko and Frantz Fanon, with the printers mounted on the gallery wall. It was listed as Biko.Fanon, 2018 in Dennis's solo exhibition Options at Goodman Gallery, Cape Town (2019), and was shown again in the survey Understudies at Zeitz MOCAA, Cape Town (2024–25). Goodman Gallery describes a later work, biko.nomzamo, as extending the artist's 'Biko dialogues' series (2018-ongoing).
