# Stranger Visions

**Heather Dewey-Hagborg**, 2012 · Material / sculpture / AI / generative / Network / data

For Stranger Visions, Heather Dewey-Hagborg collected hairs, chewed gum and cigarette butts from streets, public bathrooms and waiting rooms in New York City, extracted DNA from them and had selected genetic regions sequenced. From the results she used custom software to computationally generate life-size, full-colour 3D-printed portraits of what the anonymous individuals might look like. Exhibitions show the portraits alongside the collected samples, their collection sites and the inferred traits.

## In their words

> Working with the traces strangers unwittingly left behind, the project was meant to call attention to the developing technology of forensic DNA phenotyping, the potential for a culture of biological surveillance, and the impulse towards genetic determinism.
>
> — Heather Dewey-Hagborg (artist's website), [Stranger Visions, project page, deweyhagborg.com](https://deweyhagborg.com/projects/stranger-visions)
