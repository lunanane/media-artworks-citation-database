# Hero

**Navid Khonsari, Vassiliki Khonsari**, 2018 · VR / AR / XR / Material / sculpture / Sound

Hero is a large-scale, multi-sensory 'vérité' virtual-reality installation by iNK Stories (Navid and Vassiliki Khonsari), produced with Starbreeze, set in a Syrian town during and after a barrel-bomb air raid. Participants wear a VR headset and haptic backpack and walk through a built set whose props (a tire, crates, bricks) mirror the virtual space, with smell, heat, vibration and surround sound; the session lasts about seven minutes and is followed by a recovery room. Syrian refugees placed in the U.S. by ICNA Relief were interviewed and photographed for the piece; it premiered in Sundance New Frontier in January 2018 and was shown at Tribeca Immersive in April 2018.
