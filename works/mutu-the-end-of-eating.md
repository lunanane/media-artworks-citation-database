# The End of Eating Everything

**Wangechi Mutu**, 2013 · Screen / monitor / Sound

The End of eating Everything is Wangechi Mutu's first animated video, about eight minutes long, made with the recording artist Santigold and commissioned by the Nasher Museum of Art at Duke University for the 2013 survey Wangechi Mutu: A Fantastic Journey. Santigold appears as a planet-like creature whose body is covered in human limbs and machine parts; it drifts through a polluted, darkened sky and devours a flock of birds. The soundtrack, also by Mutu, combines industrial and organic sounds.
