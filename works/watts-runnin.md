# Runnin'

**Reggie Watts, Kiira Benzing**, 2019 · VR / AR / XR / Performance / body / Sound

Runnin' is an interactive VR music experience set to the track 'Runnin'' by Reggie Watts and John Tejada (Wajatta), made with volumetric capture of a cast of dancers at Intel Studios. The player starts in a neighbourhood record store and is transported to a retro-future dance party where dancers appear on the floor, walls and ceiling, and the player's actions trigger changes of perspective. It premiered at the 2019 Sundance Film Festival New Frontier.
