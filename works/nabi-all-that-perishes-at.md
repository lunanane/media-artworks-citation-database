# All That Perishes at the Edge of Land

**Hira Nabi**, 2018 · Screen / monitor / Sound

All That Perishes at the Edge of Land is a docu-fictional HD video by Hira Nabi set at the Gadani shipbreaking yards in Balochistan, Pakistan, in which a decommissioned container vessel, 'Ocean Master', is given a voice and converses with the migrant workers who dismantle it. It was developed through a residency at Vasl, Karachi, and commissioned by the Lahore Biennale Foundation, where it was shown looped at LB01 in 2018; a roughly 30-minute film version circulated at festivals from 2019.

## In their words

> My film, All That Perishes at the Edge of Land, is set at the Gadani shipbreaking yards in Pakistan, and is a conversation between a decommissioned container vessel and the shipyard workers. I chose to have a ship in conversation with the workers to more fully explore the impact of this industry on the environment.
>
> — Hira Nabi, [Meet New Day: Hira Nabi, New Day Films](https://www.newday.com/blog/meet-new-day-hira-nabi)

<sub>says</sub>

> When I arrived at the yards, I realized that the industry is not just dangerous for its workers, but also contributes to a complete ecological breakdown of the surrounding land and marine environment.
>
> — Hira Nabi, [Meet New Day: Hira Nabi, New Day Films](https://www.newday.com/blog/meet-new-day-hira-nabi)

> at some point I would like to believe that the container vessel is speaking on behalf of the fish, the waters, the air, the shoreline, and all the elements of that region that should be seen as important stakeholders in this industry.
>
> — Hira Nabi, [Dilpreet Bhullar, 'Hira Nabi on the technique of slow cinema to narrate the human cost of development', STIRworld](https://www.stirworld.com/inspire-people-hira-nabi-on-the-technique-of-slow-cinema-to-narrate-the-human-cost-of-development)

> The way that I think about it, my film has two roles: one, as a film, a work of art, and two, as a form of activism, as a means to achieve some kind of palpable change on the ground, at Gadani.
