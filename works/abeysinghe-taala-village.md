# Taala Village

**Chamindika Abeysinghe**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / Sound / Sensor / interaction

Taala Village ('Village of Rhythms') is a pixel-art video game newly commissioned for Colomboscope 2026 (Rhythm Alliances). Players move through a virtual island where actions are accompanied by sounds of Sri Lankan instruments such as the thalampata, drums and conch shells, and meet a speculative community that worships fire and practises rituals, legends and folklore, with settings drawn from kovils, Buddhist temples and village habitats.

## In their words

> From the immersive world of gaming at hand, the artist retreats to proto-design ideas of early game building with minimal pixels. His characters are mobilized through Sri Lankan music instruments and sonic layering. Visitors are invited to venture into a virtual island where echoes, whispers, chimes, and vibrations accompany each action sequence.
