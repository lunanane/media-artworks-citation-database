# I'm Humanity

**Etsuko Yakushimaru**, 2017 · Sound / Material / sculpture

I'm Humanity is a pop song by Etsuko Yakushimaru released in 2016 as a digital download, on CD and as a genetically modified microorganism: the song's main chord progression was converted through a codon-based cipher into a 276-nucleotide DNA sequence and inserted into the genome of the cyanobacterium Synechococcus elongatus PCC 7942, which copies it with each cell division. The microorganism was exhibited at KENPOKU ART 2016 in Ibaraki and at YCAM in Yamaguchi, where the song was also performed live by her band Soutaiseiriron with a section arranged to resemble mutation. It received the STARTS Prize 2017 Grand Prize for Artistic Exploration.

## In their words

> “I’m Humanity” is Yakushimaru Etsuko’s project based on the concept of “post-humanity music.” It seeks to find the shape of new music ―how it is transmitted, recorded, mutated, and diffused.
>
> — Etsuko Yakushimaru (artist's statement), [Artist's website, I'm Humanity page (English text given there)](https://yakushimaruetsuko.com/archives/2602)
