# I'm Feeling Lucky

**Timothy Thomasson**, 2024 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative / Network / data

A real-time generative installation of infinite runtime in which a historically and geographically ambiguous 3D landscape, generated with game engine technology, is populated with thousands of figures taken from Google Street View and turned into 3D models by a deep neural network. A virtual camera pans endlessly across the landscape, a format the artist relates to 19th-century panorama paintings; a 360° fulldome version (Panorama: I'm Feeling Lucky) was shown at the SAT in Montréal. Sound design and composition by Tatum Wilson.

## In their words

> I'm Feeling Lucky is a real-time, computer-generated installation that questions relationships to image, geography, virtual space, historical media technology, and mass data collection systems.
>
> — Timothy Thomasson, [Timothy Thomasson — I'm Feeling Lucky (artist site)](https://timothythomasson.com/i'm-feeling-lucky)

> With their blurred faces from 'Street View' these figures become semi-anonymised entities pulled from the approximately 115 thousand terabytes of 360-degree image data collected by Google–many of whom would not have known their photo was ever taken in the first place–never mind ending up in this new strange setting.
>
> — Timothy Thomasson, [Timothy Thomasson — I'm Feeling Lucky (artist site)](https://timothythomasson.com/i'm-feeling-lucky)
