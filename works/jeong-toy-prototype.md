# Toy Prototype

**Geumhyung Jeong**, 2021 · Robotics / kinetics / Screen / monitor / Performance / body

Toy Prototype is an installation of DIY robotic sculptures built by the artist from materials including aluminium profiles and DC motors, operated with remote controllers made of medical simulators and joysticks. The robots are shown alongside videos documenting Jeong's own interactions with the machines and their production process. It was commissioned by the National Museum of Modern and Contemporary Art, Korea in 2021 and shown with 9 added videos at the 59th Venice Biennale in 2022.

## In their words

> I was initially interested in controlling an object from a distance and in the remote controller itself. Specifically, the triangular relationship between a remote controller, the controlled object, and the person controlling the object.
>
> — Geumhyung Jeong, [Interview with ICA Exhibitions Curator Andrea Nitsche-Krupp, August 2024 (Under Construction)](https://www.ica.art/media/10164.pdf)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> Yes, I usually start by imagining the potential movements I am hoping to achieve. Often, it doesn’t work like I imagined, and I must change the plan. But then it becomes much more interesting to figure out what they can do from their own physical reality than the hypothetical reality I had imagined.
>
> — Geumhyung Jeong, [Interview with ICA Exhibitions Curator Andrea Nitsche-Krupp, August 2024 (Under Construction)](https://www.ica.art/media/10164.pdf)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> The idea of building the body function of the sculptures started with copying human movements, but their movements show how obviously machine they are. I find it cute when they try to move like humans but in their own way.
>
> — Geumhyung Jeong, [Interview with ICA Exhibitions Curator Andrea Nitsche-Krupp, August 2024 (Under Construction)](https://www.ica.art/media/10164.pdf)
