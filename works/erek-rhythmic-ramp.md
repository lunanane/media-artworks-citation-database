# Rhythmic Ramp

**Cevdet Erek**, 2025 · Sound / Material / sculpture

Rhythmic Ramp (Rampa rítmica) is a site-specific work by Cevdet Erek on the external ramp of Oscar Niemeyer's Ciccillo Matarazzo Pavilion at the 36th Bienal de São Paulo, the ramp that connects the exhibition's floors. It combines sonic and geometric elements placed along the ramp. Its sound includes rhythmic patterns drawn from music undergrounds in Istanbul and from São Paulo's vernacular rhythms, and visitors and passersby meet it as they go up and down the ramp.

## In their words

> Most of my spatial work invites the audience and occasionally other artists to perform to get more from it.
>
> — Cevdet Erek, [Ibraaz, 'Unfolding the Structures of Sound', Cevdet Erek in conversation with Başak Şenova (2017)](https://archive.ibraaz.org/interviews/220)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> From my side, experimentation is at the heart of this setting. All spatial installations are new experiments. They include repetition but don't want to repeat themselves. Then there is experimentation done by a portion of the visitors / viewers / participators et cetera; how to move or stand in the space, how to hear, how to loop a route.
>
> — Cevdet Erek, [Ibraaz, 'Unfolding the Structures of Sound', Cevdet Erek in conversation with Başak Şenova (2017)](https://archive.ibraaz.org/interviews/220)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> The work is done for everybody, but the diligent visitor gets in deep. Attention, moving, synchronization, looping, re-routing in the space is the visitor's part in the play, and the work happens when some of these are done.
>
> — Cevdet Erek, [Ibraaz, 'Unfolding the Structures of Sound', Cevdet Erek in conversation with Başak Şenova (2017)](https://archive.ibraaz.org/interviews/220)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> The moment a body gets moving in the space – the work starts. I don't mean there's an interaction with a sensor et cetera.
>
> — Cevdet Erek, [Ibraaz, 'Unfolding the Structures of Sound', Cevdet Erek in conversation with Başak Şenova (2017)](https://archive.ibraaz.org/interviews/220)
