# Without Us

**Daniel Brandt**, 2026 · Sound / Projection / Performance / body

Without Us is a project by composer and percussionist Daniel Brandt made up of an album (Erased Tapes, March 2025), a 20-minute short film of the same name directed with Anthony Dickenson and shot in Athens in 2023, and a live performance in which the concert is interwoven with the film. The film follows Gaia, an Uber driver who decides humanity is the world's problem; the album uses field recordings including the hum of Europe's largest mall. The live A/V version premiered at the Barbican in London in April 2025 and was presented at CTM Festival 2026.
