# Codex Virtualis\_Genesis

**Interspecifics**, 2023 · AI / generative / Projection

An AI-art-science project that generates an open-ended taxonomic collection of speculative life forms by training generative networks on microscopy footage of bacteria and archaea combined with cellular-automata models. The installation, distributed across three projections, shows the process from the simplest organisms through 'virtual genesis' to a final rendering with an AI-generated taxonomic name. It was made during the SETI x AI residency (SETI Institute and Ars Electronica Futurelab, European ARTificial Intelligence Lab) in 2021 and received an Honorary Mention at Prix Ars Electronica 2023.

## In their words

> Artificial intelligence, in this case, acts as a meta-researcher, helping us find patterns and identify emergencies at different levels of phenomena. Over time, we have positioned it as a creative agent, capable of producing subjectivity.
>
> — Interspecifics, ['Artificial intelligence as a meta-researcher', Ars Electronica Blog interview (2021)](https://ars.electronica.art/aeblog/?p=26245)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> Like with any other technology, it is our responsibility to understand the effects every particular use of AI is specifically exerting on our cultures and societies. That is why it is crucial to propose experimental scenarios to seek meaning beyond the pragmatic aspects of technology.
>
> — Interspecifics, ['Artificial intelligence as a meta-researcher', Ars Electronica Blog interview (2021)](https://ars.electronica.art/aeblog/?p=26245)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> For us, it is an opportunity for learning and generating knowledge to continue exploring questions together, in this case, about what we define as life and about how symbiosis could also be an emergent process at the computational level.
>
> — Interspecifics, ['Artificial intelligence as a meta-researcher', Ars Electronica Blog interview (2021)](https://ars.electronica.art/aeblog/?p=26245)

> The project stands on the idea of cooperation to expand on the concept of intelligence by including machine and non-human agents into its configuration. And intends to articulate new schemes for the social imaginary to picture life outside the planet, and to better appreciate life on earth.
>
