# Iyeza

**Kudzanai Chiurai**, 2012 · Screen / monitor

Iyeza is an 11-minute film, written and directed by Kudzanai Chiurai, described in the Sundance programme as an allegory of the Last Supper depicting the establishment of a new nation-state, setting the past and the present of a continent in civil wars side by side. It is in Xhosa with English subtitles, with music by Thandiswa Mazwai. Chiurai says it is one of three films (Iyeza, Creation and Moyo) about birth, sacrifice and death. It screened at the 2013 Sundance Film Festival and at MIFF 2013 in Melbourne.
