# Hidirtina / Sisters

**Ezra Wube**, 2018 · Screen / monitor / Sound

Hidirtina / Sisters is a 9'14" animated video based on a myth from Eritrea and northern Ethiopia about seven immortal sisters living in nature and a hunter who kills a deer, is cursed, and is protected by a piece of a tree until he loses it in a river. The story was told to the artist by a volunteer who answered his open call for folklore to a Habesha diaspora community in New York, and the animation also includes the process of its making, such as email exchanges, café meetings and a brief portrait of the storyteller. It was shown in the 21st Contemporary Art Biennial Sesc\_Videobrasil, 'Imagined Communities' (São Paulo, 2019–20).
