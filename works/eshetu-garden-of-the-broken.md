# Garden of the Broken Hearted

**Theo Eshetu**, 2026 · Projection / Material / sculpture / Robotics / kinetics

An installation in the Arsenale at the 61st Venice Biennale (In Minor Keys): a large olive tree placed on a rotating stage, onto which a video is projected. The projected footage shows the same tree, filmed before its transport to Venice.

## In their words

> I am bringing a tree. It is also an absence of mine, if you like: perhaps the theme of the work is precisely absence. It is a tree placed on a rotating platform, and perhaps this is the element that is still cinematic, if you like.
>
> — Theo Eshetu, [Artribune, 'Intervista per scoprire Theo Eshetu, unico artista italiano alla Biennale di Venezia 2026' (Christian Caliandro), 6 May 2026 (translated from Italian)](https://www.artribune.com/?p=1230851)

<sub>says</sub>

> The work is closely linked to the death of Koyo Kouoh. Not presenting a large multiscreen video mirrors the ‘violence’ of this loss, and the general violence in which we live immersed, and in which our reference points have shifted.
>
> — Theo Eshetu, [Artribune, 'Intervista per scoprire Theo Eshetu, unico artista italiano alla Biennale di Venezia 2026' (Christian Caliandro), 6 May 2026 (translated from Italian)](https://www.artribune.com/?p=1230851)

<sub>says</sub>

> This is what I feel: reality has made us shift those fixed points on which we could build discourses.
>
> — Theo Eshetu, [Artribune, 'Intervista per scoprire Theo Eshetu, unico artista italiano alla Biennale di Venezia 2026' (Christian Caliandro), 6 May 2026 (translated from Italian)](https://www.artribune.com/?p=1230851)

> — Theo Eshetu, [Artribune, 'Intervista per scoprire Theo Eshetu, unico artista italiano alla Biennale di Venezia 2026' (Christian Caliandro), 6 May 2026 (translated from Italian)](https://www.artribune.com/?p=1230851)

> In my view, everything is ruined by screens: that is, by the fact that the intimacy of our relationship with things is always filtered and mediated. This digital mediation is sabotaging our imagination; video art should try to be, in this sense, a lens that reveals, that shows the artifice.

> — Theo Eshetu, [Artribune, 'Intervista per scoprire Theo Eshetu, unico artista italiano alla Biennale di Venezia 2026' (Christian Caliandro), 6 May 2026 (translated from Italian)](https://www.artribune.com/?p=1230851)
