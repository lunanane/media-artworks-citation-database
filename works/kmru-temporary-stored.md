# Temporary Stored

**Joseph Kamaru (KMRU)**, 2023 · Sound

A sound work built from recordings in the Sound Archive of the Royal Museum for Central Africa (Tervuren), field recordings and synthesizers, presented as shorter fixed-media pieces and a longform radiophonic piece. It received an Honorary Mention in Digital Musics & Sound Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2023 and was exhibited at the Ars Electronica Festival 2023; it also received an Honorary Mention at the 2023 Prix Palma Ars Acustica.

## In their words

> Archives have always been framed in a guise that promotes a Western perspective of representations of the Other, a modernity in which the institutions and museums frame these archives and collections.
>
> — Joseph Kamaru (project statement), [Prix Ars Electronica 2023, project page](https://ars.electronica.art/who-owns-the-truth/?p=9470)
