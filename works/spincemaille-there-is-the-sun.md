# There is the sun

**Ief Spincemaille**, 2014 · Light / Robotics / kinetics / Network / data

There is the sun is an installation of three motorized mirrors placed on the lawn in front of block 1 of Sint-Maartensdal, a 1960s residential complex in Leuven, Belgium, whose north-south orientation leaves many apartments without direct sunlight. Through a website, residents entered their house number and booked a date and time, and a mirror turned to reflect sunlight through their window. It received an Honorary Mention in Interactive Art at the Prix Ars Electronica 2014.

## In their words

> I placed three motorized mirrors to reflect sunlight towards one specific point. Using a user-friendly website, occupants could click on the date and time they wished to book sunlight for their apartment. One of the mirrors then turned to reflect the sunlight through their window.
>
> — Ief Spincemaille, [Prix Ars Electronica 2014, Interactive Art, Honorary Mention - catalog text, Ars Electronica Archive](https://archive.aec.at/prix/209335)
