# Chuly? Chuly

**Letta Shtohryn**, 2024 · Performance / body / Sensor / interaction / Projection

An XR video game and live motion-capture performance (Ukrainian title Чули? Чули, 'Have you heard? We've heard' / 'Have you felt it? We've felt it'), first developed as a demo in Deep Space 8K at the Ars Electronica Center. In the first act a player crosses a game world guided by an AI-processed narration of a woman's story of seeing giants; in the second a dancer in an inertial motion-capture suit, whose calibration drifts, enters, and player and dancer exchange suit parts and controller, so the player becomes a non-player character and the dancer controls the camera. It received an Honorary Mention in New Animation Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2024.

## In their words

> This work delves into questions of who serves as the protagonist and antagonist in online manipulations as it dynamically shifts perspectives between the player and the dancer, both virtually and metaphorically, challenging the traditional divide between virtuality and physicality.
>
> — Letta Shtohryn, [Project page, FOUNDING LAB Fall Term Projects (IT:U x Ars Electronica)](https://ars.electronica.art/university/en/%d1%87%d1%83%d0%bb%d0%b8-%d1%87%d1%83%d0%bb%d0%b8/)

> The project is driven by several interconnected themes – a reflection on truth, belief, propaganda, and online interaction, exploring their consequences in the physical world; and an exploration of our extended sensorium, enhanced and triggered by technologies, leading to speculative experiences that blur the boundaries between fact and fiction.
>
> — Letta Shtohryn, [Project page, FOUNDING LAB Fall Term Projects (IT:U x Ars Electronica)](https://ars.electronica.art/university/en/%d1%87%d1%83%d0%bb%d0%b8-%d1%87%d1%83%d0%bb%d0%b8/)
