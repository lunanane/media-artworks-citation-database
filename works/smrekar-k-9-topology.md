# K-9\_topology

**Maja Smrekar**, 2017 · Performance / body / Material / sculpture

K-9\_topology is a series of four projects (2014–2017) about the relationship between humans, dogs and wolves: Ecce Canis (a fragrance made from serotonin of the artist and her dog), I Hunt Nature and Culture Hunts Me (a performance made during a residency at JACANA Wildlife Studios in France, on the relationship between wolf, dog and human), Hybrid Family (a three-month seclusion in which the artist induced lactation and lived with her dogs, ending in a public performance with a puppy) and ARTE\_mis (a hybrid cell made by fusing the artist's enucleated egg cell with a somatic cell of her dog, shown frozen under a microscope). For the 2017 CyberArts exhibition in Linz the series was shown together as an installation in the form of a dog stacker, a training tool for show dogs.
