# System's Void

**Sung Tieu**, 2026 · Sound / Projection / Network / data

System's Void is a sound installation that translates danger signals from Hazardous Gas System (HGS) monitoring at four fracking wells in the United States into sound, accompanied by a video projection of chemical codes used to classify chemicals. At the 2026 Whitney Biennial the sound was amplified through the large pipe in the museum's central staircase; an earlier version, commissioned by the 15th Gwangju Biennale in 2024, was a five-channel sound installation with sand, steel pipes, speakers and amplifiers.

## In their words

> It was more of a conceptual idea to record the Earth and shift the perspective away from the human. I wanted to explore what the Earth “hears” when a pipe goes through it or when a well is constantly pumping and extracting gas.
>
> — Sung Tieu, [The Brooklyn Rail, 'Sung Tieu with Amelia Saul', In Conversation, July/August 2023](https://brooklynrail.org/2023/07/art/Sung-Tieu-with-Amelia-Saul/)
