# Mid Tide #3

**Ryu Furusawa**, 2024 · Screen / monitor

A silent three-channel video installation (48:49 seamless loop) made from three and a half hours of fixed-camera 4K 120fps footage of waves breaking against rocks as the light and tide change. Furusawa's own software turns the footage into a 3D object with time as its depth and cuts it along freely moving planes instead of the usual front-to-back playback, so different moments appear side by side within one frame. Viewers watch the waves keep their form while the rocks appear to metamorphose.

## In their words

> In surfing, one must sharpen one's physical senses to catch the waves. Thus, the body becomes one with the wave. The purpose of this work is to convey this sense of unity.
>
> — Ryu Furusawa (artist statement), [Prix Ars Electronica archive, catalog text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/290636/)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> Expressing the feeling of floating, specifically the experience where the perception of time and space becomes ambiguous, is challenging.
>
> — Ryu Furusawa (artist statement), [Prix Ars Electronica archive, catalog text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/290636/)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> Therefore, I developed a software to create a 3D object from video data, using time as the depth dimension, and to freely cut out arbitrary planes within that object.
>
> — Ryu Furusawa (artist statement), [Prix Ars Electronica archive, catalog text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/290636/)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> Just as the changes in the tides can quietly transform the landscape, this work gradually erodes the viewer's perception of time and space.
>
> — Ryu Furusawa (artist statement), [Prix Ars Electronica archive, catalog text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/290636/)
