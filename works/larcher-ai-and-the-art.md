# AI and the Art of Historical Reinterpretation – Filling Gender Bias Gaps

**Claudia Larcher**, 2024 · AI / generative / Projection / Network / data

An ongoing project that uses AI image generation to build a growing fictional archive of 'historical images' in which male figures in known historical photographs are replaced by female or nonbinary figures; the collection is spread across digital platforms so it can be scraped as training data for future AI models. In the exhibition installation, a selection of images related to technology is shown with a slide projector on a vintage projection screen.
