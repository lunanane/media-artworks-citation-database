# Transmission Loss

**Magdi Mostafa**, 2016 · Sound / Material / sculpture / Sensor / interaction

Transmission Loss is a vibration-based sound sculpture and installation of variable size by Magdi Mostafa, first shown in GLOBALE: New Sensorium at ZKM Karlsruhe in 2016, extending his earlier work Sound Cells (Analog frequencies) (Cairo, 2009) built from hand-made circuits and simple vintage sound devices. Visitors turn the dials of tone generators and handle vibrating devices on a table, altering a field of noise and the feedback between devices. It was also shown at Dak'Art, the Dakar Biennale, in 2018.
