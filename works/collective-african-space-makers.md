# African Space Makers

**The Nrb Bus Collective**, 2020 · VR / AR / XR

A 50-minute interactive 360-degree VR experience made in Nairobi, shown in Venice VR Expanded 2020. The viewer chooses a gender and the order in which to visit five creative spaces run by Nairobi collectives, and in each space chooses what to do and where to go next.

## In their words

> Promoting these autentic Afro-narratives and these urban heroes means more than just having fun by exploring contemporary Africa, it’s Afro-Punk in action! AfroPunk promotes the doing it yourself philosophy, which is an anti-capitalist and anti imperialist, a powerful weapon against neo-colonialism.
>
> — The Nrb Bus Collective (Director’s Statement), [La Biennale di Venezia, Venice VR Expanded 2020](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2020/venice-vr-expanded/african-space-makers)

<sub>says</sub>

> VR is an ideal medium to explore space, which becomes the real protagonist of the story. The characters are more a link between you and the space to help you navigate it through their stories, their actions, their movement,
>
> — Vincenzo Cavallo Faras (Dr. Faras), co-founder of The Nrb Bus Collective, [Immerse, “Field Notes: Space-Making at Venice VR Expanded” (interview by Andrea Kim)](https://immerse-news.github.io/field-notes-space-making-at-venice-vr-expanded-20ce4b9e924d.html)

<sub>says</sub>

> We have a Western idea of what a creative city should be and what it looks like — for instance, Amsterdam or San Francisco. But we want to show creative cities in African contexts and the collectives doing amazing things despite the fact there are limited funds or little effort from their governments to promote the creative industry.
>
> — Vincenzo Cavallo Faras (Dr. Faras), [Immerse, “Field Notes: Space-Making at Venice VR Expanded” (interview by Andrea Kim)](https://immerse-news.github.io/field-notes-space-making-at-venice-vr-expanded-20ce4b9e924d.html)

<sub>says</sub>

> The topics are many, but they are linked to one major issue, the importance and the right of having non-commercial spaces where people can develop new projects and ideas together, for the good of the emerging creative economy.
>
> — Vincenzo Cavallo Faras (Dr. Faras), [Hivos, “Meet Dr. Faras who is exploring a new era of storytelling” (interview by Sarah Nyakio)](https://hivos.org/meet-dr-faras-who-is-exploring-a-new-era-of-storytelling/)
