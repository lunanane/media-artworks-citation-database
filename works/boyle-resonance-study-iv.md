# Resonance Study IV

**Asvajit Boyle**, 2018 · Sound / Projection / AI / generative

Resonance Study IV is a multimedia digital sound installation (7 minutes 10 seconds, looped) by Colombo-based audio/visual artist Asvajit Boyle, shown at the first Lahore Biennale (LB01) in 2018. According to the Biennale, Boyle used an image-based sound editor, synthesizer and processor together with Google's Deep Dream computer-vision program to turn sounds into images and images into sound. Visitors meet the resulting images and soundscape as an audio-visual installation.
