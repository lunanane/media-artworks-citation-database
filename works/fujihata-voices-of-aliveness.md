# Voices of Aliveness

**Masaki Fujihata**, 2013 · Sound / Network / data / Performance / body

Voices of Aliveness invites participants to ride bicycles equipped with a GPS recorder and a video camera along a 'shouting circuit' at La Martinière near Nantes, France, and to shout as loud as they can during the ride. Each ride is recorded and put online as a ring-shaped trace of path and voice, and the rings are compiled into a tower-like 'time tunnel'; the composer Yasuaki Shimizu arranged the recorded voices for the soundtrack. Produced in 2012 by the École supérieure des beaux-arts de Nantes Métropole within Estuaire, it received an award in Interactive Art at the Prix Ars Electronica 2013.

>
> — Masaki Fujihata, [Masaki Fujihata, 'Concept - Voices of Aliveness 2012', voicesofaliveness.net](http://voicesofaliveness.net/concept/)
