# Guanaquerx

**Paula Gaetano Adi**, 2024–2025 · Robotics / kinetics / Performance / body

Guanaquerx is a four-legged robot modelled on the guanaco, partly made from caña colihue bamboo and dressed in Andean textiles. In January 2024, about 30 people with 58 mules and horses took it across the Andes from Argentina to the Chilean border over seven days, re-enacting the 1817 crossing by San Martín's army; mules carried the robot where the ground was too rough for it. The robot records sound and images, emits a synthetic whinny, beats a drum, waves a flag and carries a plaque with 'Pluriversal Laws of Robotics'. The work won the 2025 Prix Ars Electronica Golden Nica and was shown at the Ars Electronica Festival 2025.
