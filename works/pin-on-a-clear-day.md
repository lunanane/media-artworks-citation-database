# On a Clear Day You Can See Forever

**Tan Pin Pin**, 2025 · Projection / Sound

A two-channel video installation (synchronised rear HD projections, colour, four-channel sound, 39 min) on translucent voile screens held in a parallel, trapezoid frame, shown in a shop unit at Lucky Plaza, Orchard Road, for Singapore Biennale 2025. One screen shows Inuka, Singapore's first polar bear born in captivity, circling in his zoo enclosure; the other shows 80km/h (2004), a single-take drive along the Pan Island Expressway filmed from the passenger seat at a constant 80 km/h. Visitors view the two projections overlapping through the frame.

## In their words

> In one take, without a single cut, I document every inch of the island from one edge to the other.
>
> — Tan Pin Pin, [Tan Pin Pin, 'A filming manifesto for 80km/h', artist website](https://tanpinpin.com/art/80km-per-hour)

<sub>says · about another work</sub>

> I maintain a consistent speed of 80 km/h so the document retains cartographic value. If the same route along the Pan Island Expressway were recorded every year, Singapore’s topographical changes could be mapped by comparing recordings over time. It is my intention to film this journey regularly.
>
> — Tan Pin Pin, [Tan Pin Pin, 'A filming manifesto for 80km/h', artist website](https://tanpinpin.com/art/80km-per-hour)
