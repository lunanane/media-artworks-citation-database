# Wantee

**Laure Prouvost**, 2013 · Screen / monitor / Material / sculpture

Wantee is a film installation, commissioned by Tate and Grizedale Arts for the exhibition Schwitters in Britain at Tate Britain in 2013, in which a film tells a fictional story of Prouvost's grandfather, a conceptual artist and friend of Kurt Schwitters, who disappeared while digging a tunnel from his home towards Africa. Visitors watch the film in a room furnished like a tea party, with objects including some made by potters and community groups; the title is the nickname of Schwitters's partner Edith Thomas, from her habit of asking 'want tea?'. Shown again at the Turner Prize exhibition in Derry-Londonderry, where tea and cakes were served, it won the 2013 Turner Prize.

## In their words

> Schwitters had a girlfriend who asked visitors if they wanted tea so much that he ended up calling her Wantee.
>
> — Laure Prouvost, [Interview with The Guardian, as quoted on purple TELEVISION, 'An excerpt from Wantee (2013)'](https://purple.fr/television/an-excerpt-from-wantee-2013/)

<sub>says</sub>

> He would have done anything for Wantee. She pushed him to go deeper in history and dig the tunnel and question how far society can control traces of a person.
>
> — Laure Prouvost, ['Interview with Laure Prouvost', The White Review (email interview)](https://www.thewhitereview.org/feature/interview-with-laure-prouvost/)

<sub>says</sub>

> I love collaborating with people from the past who are not dead yet.
>
> — Laure Prouvost, ['Interview with Laure Prouvost', The White Review (email interview)](https://www.thewhitereview.org/feature/interview-with-laure-prouvost/)
