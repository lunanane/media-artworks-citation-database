# A Tale of Two Seeds: Sound and Silence in Latin America's Andean Plain

**Atractor Estudio + Semántica Productions**, 2022 · Sound / Network / data / Screen / monitor

A sound installation built from field recordings of soils and plants in Colombia's Eastern Plains, recorded above and below ground, together with recordings of the electrical conductivity of soy and amaranth plants, which are sonified. FACT describes the recordings as contrasting soy monocultures with soy-amaranth polycultures, where amaranth grows despite herbicide use. Accompanying video works (On Vegetal Politics, Botánica Transgénica) and a blockchain application address the stock-market valuation and patenting of genetically modified plants; the work received the 2023 Prix Ars Electronica Golden Nica in Digital Musics & Sound Art.
