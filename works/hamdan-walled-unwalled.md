# Walled Unwalled

**Lawrence Abu Hamdan**, 2018 · Projection / Sound / Performance / body

Walled Unwalled is a 20-minute single-channel video installation in which Lawrence Abu Hamdan, filmed behind the windows of recording booths in the Funkhaus studios in former East Berlin (the GDR state radio headquarters), delivers a monologue on legal cases in which evidence was heard or experienced through walls, doors or floors. These include the US thermal-imaging case Kyllo v. United States, the Oscar Pistorius murder trial and testimonies of survivors of Saydnaya prison in Syria, with projected images, moving walls and sound. At Tate Modern's Tanks (2018) the video was projected onto a wall of semi-transparent panels that visitors first saw from behind.
