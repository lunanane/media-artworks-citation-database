# Pixel Dust

**Leo Villareal**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / Light

Pixel Dust is a video of pulsating particles made with the artist's custom software, premiered as Times Square Arts' Midnight Moment for November 2025. It played synchronized on over 90 electronic billboards in Times Square nightly from 11:57pm to midnight, November 1-30, 2025.
