# Chosho Hakkei in Rittor Base - HPL ver

**evala**, 2021 · Sound

A five-track, roughly 42-minute 3D audio album for headphone listening, made with the HPL binaural process developed with engineer Jiro Kubo (ACOUSTIC FIELD). It reconstructs evala's 2019 sound installations 'Chosho Hakkei' in the Nakazu Banshou-en daimyo garden in Marugame, Kagawa, placed on a bridge, on a small island and in the oldest extant tea house there, first restaged as a 3D sound piece at Rittor Base. Listeners follow, on headphones, a route through the garden's recorded and composed sounds.

## In their words

> People who reach Kanchoro hear the various sounds of the garden they have walked through until then, like auditory hallucinations, while, because the windows are left open, they also hear the sounds sounding outside right now, and these resonate with one another. It is a work in which the boundary between reality and fantasy becomes ambiguous.
>
> — evala, [Interview by Susumu Kunisaki, OTOTOY, 25 Jan 2021 (translated from Japanese)](https://ototoy.jp/feature/2021012501)
