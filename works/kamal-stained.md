# Stained

**Jeremy Kamal**, 2024 · Screen / monitor / Sound / AI / generative

A CGI short film set in Mojo, a fictional future in which Black culture transforms American landscapes. It follows Demetrius, a tea master in the Crimson Needles gang, which uses colored flora to mark territory, as he relives being reprimanded for his fascination with a forbidden blue plant. Credits list music by Nathan Buttel and Jeremy Kamal and AI creative direction by Case Miller; it received a Prix Ars Electronica 2024 Award of Distinction in New Animation Art.

## In their words

> The film takes place in the narrative world of Mojo, a fictional future I created as a way of bridging the disparities between the contemporary Black experience and landscape discourse. It is a response to my experience in landscape architecture where we were taught to see, study, and interpret landscapes from a limited point of view.
>
> — Jeremy Kamal, [Ars Electronica Archive — Stained (Prix Ars Electronica 2024)](https://archive.aec.at/prix/290621)
