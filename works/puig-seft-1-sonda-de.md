# SEFT-1 (Sonda de Exploración Ferroviaria Tripulada)

**Ivan Puig & Andrés Padilla Domene**, 2010 · Robotics / kinetics / Network / data / Online / net art

SEFT-1 (Manned Railway Exploration Probe) is a two-person vehicle built on the chassis of a convertible railway pickup truck, with an aluminium fuselage, that can travel on land or on rails; its makers, the 'ferronautas', drove it along Mexico's disused railway lines, guided by modern and 19th-century maps. On the way it records photographs, video, audio, text and interviews with people it meets, and transmits them to the project website, where its location, routes and recordings can be followed; in some towns the crew presents material gathered on earlier trips.
