# Neo Geo City

**Rafaël Rozendaal**, 2014 · Online / net art / Sensor / interaction

Neo Geo City is a website at its own domain (neogeocity.com), supported by ZKM Karlsruhe and coded by Reinier Feijen, showing an abstract city of large, variously coloured cubes. According to ZKM, visitors fly over the city, can grab and move the cubes in any direction with the mouse, and clicking one turns the scene black so that only wire-frame models remain; the city extends endlessly in whichever direction is chosen.

## In their words

> What you try to do when you find a medium is find its strengths and its weaknesses. I found out that if you want to go really 3D realistic, the browser can’t handle it, so I thought, "Let's go the opposite way. Let's make it simple as possible. What can a browser do that a DVD player cannot do?"
>
> — Rafaël Rozendaal, [Interview by Leigh Silver, Complex, February 2014](https://www.complex.com/style/a/leigh-silver/rafael-rozendaal-interview)

<sub>asks</sub>

> A website is a work that exists in a specific place, in its domain name. It’s a place that you can go to wherever you are, by typing the title of the work into your browser. It’s a public place that is non-local.
>
> — Rafaël Rozendaal, [Interview by A Will Brown, Studio International, 2015](https://www.studiointernational.com/rafael-rozendaal-interview-dutch-brazilian-visual-artist-websites-internet-haiku-new-york/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Because a website is a new format, we look for analogies to understand what it is. It’s not a movie, it’s not a game, it’s not a painting: it’s something between those three, I think.
>
> — Rafaël Rozendaal, [Interview by A Will Brown, Studio International, 2015](https://www.studiointernational.com/rafael-rozendaal-interview-dutch-brazilian-visual-artist-websites-internet-haiku-new-york/)

<sub>says</sub>

> That’s why I make moving images that have no beginning or end. You can look at them for a few seconds, or leave them on your screen for a year.
>
> — Rafaël Rozendaal, [Interview by A Will Brown, Studio International, 2015](https://www.studiointernational.com/rafael-rozendaal-interview-dutch-brazilian-visual-artist-websites-internet-haiku-new-york/)

<sub>says</sub>

> The idea is that the website is like liquid, or like a piece of gas,
>
> — Rafaël Rozendaal, [Rhizome, "Rafaël Rozendaal's Liquid Websites" (quoting Rhizome's video interviews), 2013](https://old.rhizome.org/editorial/2013/oct/09/rafael-rozendaals-liquid-websites/)
