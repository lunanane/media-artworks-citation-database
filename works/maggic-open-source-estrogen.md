# Open Source Estrogen

**Mary Maggic**, 2015 · Material / sculpture / Performance / body / Screen / monitor

Open Source Estrogen is a collaborative research project by Mary Maggic, begun with Byron Rich, that combines biohacking and speculative design around the hormone estrogen, aiming to develop do-it-yourself / do-it-with-others protocols for detecting and extracting it. Its protocols include transgenic yeast sensors for estrogenic compounds, DIY column chromatography with cigarette filters, silica gel and methanol to extract hormones from urine, and solid-phase extraction of estrogenic compounds from water; it is carried out through public workshops, kitchen performance and videos such as Housewives Making Drugs. It received an Honorary Mention in Hybrid Art at the Prix Ars Electronica 2017.
