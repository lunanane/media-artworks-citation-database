# FuneralPlay

**Ruini Shi**, 2023 · Online / net art / Network / data

A website presented as a fictitious online funeral platform where mourners attend simulated funerals for lost loved ones in themed virtual mausoleums, such as a mahjong room, a gallery of cat memes or a feng shui master's orchard. Users can choose to delete a deceased person's digital footprint, keep it as an inheritance, or donate it to a collective Memorial Hall, and can give NFTs as memorial gifts; exploring a scene reveals hidden clues and rewards. The platform states it has no actual financial function and does not share data with other platforms.
