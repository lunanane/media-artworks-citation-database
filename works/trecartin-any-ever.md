# Any Ever

**Ryan Trecartin**, 2010 · Screen / monitor / Sound / Material / sculpture

Any Ever is a series of seven movies by Ryan Trecartin, made in collaboration with Lizzie Fitch and largely filmed over two years in a house in Miami that served as both their home and the set. In 2011 it was shown at MoMA PS1 and MOCA Miami at the same time, after opening at The Power Plant in Toronto. At PS1 the movies played inside 'sculptural theaters' made with Fitch, which visitors sit in to watch; the movies are also online.
