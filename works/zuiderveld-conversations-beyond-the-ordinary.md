# Conversations Beyond the Ordinary

**Jan Zuiderveld**, 2024 · AI / generative / Sensor / interaction / Sound

Conversations Beyond the Ordinary is an ensemble of three interactive installations (a coffee vending machine, a photocopier and a microwave) fitted with sensors, custom software and generative AI so that they converse with visitors. Visitors have to persuade the coffee machine to brew them a coffee, draw doodles that the photocopier turns into rendered prints they can take home, and use the microwave to give a voice to objects placed inside it. It received an Honorary Mention in Interactive Art+ at Prix Ars Electronica 2024 and was shown at the Ars Electronica Festival 2024; the coffee machine and microwave were developed during a residency at iii (instrument inventors initiative).

## In their words

> Motivated by curiosity and fascination for the fast developing field of artificial intelligence, I try to understand and shed light on the profound processes underlying AI methods that are transforming our world by approaching the topic from both a creative- and scientific perspective.
>
> — Jan Zuiderveld, [KABK graduation catalogue 2023, artist statement (Dream Machine)](https://graduation.kabk.nl/2023/jan-zuiderveld)
