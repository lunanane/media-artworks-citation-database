# Occupy George

**Ivan Cash, Andy Dao**, 2012 · Text / print / Network / data

Occupy George is a set of rubber stamps that print infographics on US one-dollar bills, showing statistics on wealth and income disparity in America, such as how much wealth the richest 400 Americans hold or the ratio of CEO to worker pay. Ivan Cash and Andy Dao stamped bills and exchanged them for unmarked ones at Occupy Wall Street protests, so the marked bills circulated as ordinary money; templates were offered online for people to print their own. The project was later shown at Ars Electronica (Honorary Mention, Interactive Art, 2012) and entered the V&A collection, which commissioned a version for the UK £5 note.
