# Mnemosyne

**WUER**, 2025 · VR / AR / XR / Performance / body

A 35-minute immersive work that combines VR film (360° animation and 180° live-action footage) with live theatre performance and an installation environment of meditation booths, inspired by Tang Xianzu's Ming Dynasty play The Peony Pavilion. Audience members wear headsets inside the installation and, near the end, meet a live actor with mixed reality elements. It was shown in the Future Cinema section of the Shanghai International Film Festival (2025) and out of competition in the Biennale College Cinema – Immersive section of Venice Immersive 2025.
