# In a Small Room

**KyungJin Jeong**, 2021 · AI / generative / VR / AR / XR / Screen / monitor

A project titled in full 'In a Small Room: The Gap between Advertising and Reality', made as a Royal College of Art (Information Experience Design) degree project, about cramped housing in London and Seoul and the advertising images used to rent it. It has four parts: an interactive web application (CycleGAN) into which visitors put a photo of a real room and receive an advertising-style image, a VR installation of an 'advertising' room entered from a bed in a built room, two films shot in the same Seoul goshiwon room (a documentary and an advertisement), and a research dissertation. It received an Honorary Mention in Digital Humanity at Prix Ars Electronica 2021.

## In their words

> My aim is to explore the gap between advertising and reality and produce works that would impact our real world. The main goal is to show how advertisements can distort normal photos.
>
> — KyungJin Jeong, [Artist profile and project page, RCA2020 graduate show (Royal College of Art)](https://2020.rca.ac.uk/students/kyungjin-jeong)

<sub>says</sub>

> In South Korea, a goshiwon is a residential space in the form of a building divided into many rooms on one floor. I wanted to show two views of a goshiwon room.
>
> — KyungJin Jeong, [Artist profile and project page, RCA2020 graduate show (Royal College of Art)](https://2020.rca.ac.uk/students/kyungjin-jeong)

<sub>says</sub>

> To explore my work deeply, while collecting housing advertising images and determining how they distorted the photos, I found common strategies like stretching, object removal, and special lights. Because online photos are virtual, it is easy for people to trick audiences.
>
> — KyungJin Jeong, [Artist profile and project page, RCA2020 graduate show (Royal College of Art)](https://2020.rca.ac.uk/students/kyungjin-jeong)

<sub>says</sub>

> I believe that designers have the power to reveal social issues to the public through their works. Moreover, I think that designers can find feasible solutions to solve those issues.
>
> — KyungJin Jeong, [Artist profile and project page, RCA2020 graduate show (Royal College of Art)](https://2020.rca.ac.uk/students/kyungjin-jeong)
