# The Butcher's Son

**Mario Klingemann**, 2018 · AI / generative / Text / print

A single figurative image generated by a chain of generative adversarial networks (GANs): a randomly generated stick figure is fed to a first GAN that produces a low-resolution painterly image, which further GANs upscale and add detail to. The artist selects one output from thousands of variations. It is shown as a print on paper (giclée/ink on cotton rag); the V&A dates the work 2017 and lists it in Klingemann's Imposture Series, and it received the Lumen Prize Gold Award in 2018.

## In their words

> This image has been generated entirely by a machine using a chain of GANs (generative adversarial neural networks).
>
> — Mario Klingemann, [Artist statement, Lumen Prize Gold Award Winner 2018 page](https://lumenprize.com/2018-winners/mario-klingemann)

> I control this process indirectly by training the model on selected data sets, the model’s hyperparameters and eventually by making a curatorial choice, by picking among the thousands of variations produced by the models the one that speaks to me most.
>
> — Mario Klingemann, [Artist statement, Lumen Prize Gold Award Winner 2018 page](https://lumenprize.com/2018-winners/mario-klingemann)

>
