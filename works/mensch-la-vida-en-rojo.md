# La vida en rojo

**Julia Mensch**, 2018 · Screen / monitor / Sound

La vida en rojo is a 21'50" video essay (2016-2018) by Julia Mensch, part of a long-term project begun in 2008 about the private and political life of her grandparents, both members of the Argentinian Communist Party. A narrating voice contextualises and questions images of the grandparents' house, slides from her grandfather's 1970s trip through the Soviet bloc, letters, postcards and photographs from the family archive. It was shown at the 21st Bienal Sesc\_Videobrasil, Imagined Communities (São Paulo, 2019-2020).
