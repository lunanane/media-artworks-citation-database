# Cangjie's Poetry

**Weidi Zhang, Donghao Ren**, 2022 · AI / generative / Camera / computer vision / Projection

An installation in which an AI system named Cangjie, a neural network trained on the constructions of over 9,000 Chinese characters, watches its surroundings through a camera and turns the live feed into new symbols built from Chinese strokes, shown as a moving pixelated landscape on a first projection. A second projection shows descriptive sentences the system generates about the scene, rendered as flowing ink 'poetry' assembled with fragments of the captured imagery. Visitors meet both projections changing in real time with what the camera sees.
