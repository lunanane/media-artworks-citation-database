# Estate (July 10, 2022)

**Cooper Jacoby**, 2026 · AI / generative / Camera / computer vision / Material / sculpture

Estate (July 10, 2022) is a sculpture in the form of a door intercom mounted on a folding screen, shown at the Whitney Biennial 2026. A camera in the work monitors its surroundings, and an AI model trained on the social media posts of a deceased person from a creative field speaks in a cloned voice when visitors come near, responding with fragments of recollection (for example a recipe when someone passes holding a drink). An LED screen counts the years, days, hours and minutes since that person's death.
