# Study for Strings

**Susan Philipsz**, 2012 · Sound

Study for Strings is an eight-channel sound installation made for dOCUMENTA (13) in 2012 and installed at the end of a platform at Kassel Hauptbahnhof. It reworks the 1943 orchestral piece of the same name by Pavel Haas, composed while he was imprisoned in the Theresienstadt concentration camp; Philipsz recorded only the viola and cello parts, so listeners hear those two lines with silences where the other instruments would play. It was later shown at MoMA's Soundings: A Contemporary Score (2013) through eight wall-mounted speakers.

## In their words

> Like in Kassel, for instance, it was the atmosphere of the train station that drew me. “Study for Strings” started from standing at the platform's end and thinking about sound coming from a distance.
>
> — Susan Philipsz, [Art21, 'Susan Philipsz in "Berlin"', Art in the Twenty-First Century, Season 9 (2018), English subtitles (Amara)](https://amara.org/api/videos/LWNxEBkt9jXB/languages/en/subtitles/?format=txt)

>
> — Susan Philipsz, [Art21, 'Susan Philipsz in "Berlin"', Art in the Twenty-First Century, Season 9 (2018), English subtitles (Amara)](https://amara.org/api/videos/LWNxEBkt9jXB/languages/en/subtitles/?format=txt)
