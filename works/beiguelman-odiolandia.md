# Odiolândia (Hateland)

**Giselle Beiguelman**, 2017 · Screen / monitor / Text / print / Network / data

A video without images that shows comments posted on social media about the São Paulo city and state governments' operations in Cracolândia between 21 May and 9 June 2017. The audio comes from videos that participants in those operations posted online. The comments are shown as posted, without grammatical or stylistic corrections; the artist removed only electoral content. It was made for the exhibition São Paulo não é uma cidade: as invenções do centro at Sesc 24 de Maio, São Paulo.
