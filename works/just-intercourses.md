# Intercourses

**Jesper Just**, 2013 · Projection / Sound / Material / sculpture

Intercourses is a five-channel film installation made for the Danish Pavilion at the 55th Venice Biennale (1 June - 24 November 2013). The film was shot in Tianducheng, a replica of Paris in a suburb of Hangzhou, China, and follows three men moving through the city, with the city itself treated as the main character. Projections of different sizes were placed through the pavilion, and architectural interventions began outside the building before visitors entered.

## In their words

> Intercourses is an installation that deals with architecture, cultural dislocation and memory – real and imagined. I was thinking about connection and disconnection, ways to communicate with and through a location or space.
>
> — Jesper Just, ['The Venice Questionnaire #1: Jesper Just', ArtReview, 1 May 2013](https://artreview.com/1-venice-jesper-just/)

<sub>says</sub>

> I wanted to find a way to integrate the architectural space of the pavilion with the filmic space.
>
> — Jesper Just, ['The Venice Questionnaire #1: Jesper Just', ArtReview, 1 May 2013](https://artreview.com/1-venice-jesper-just/)

<sub>says</sub>

> There is something very homeless about the area: the way it is left deserted for a greater part of the year, the way it is one nation on another nation’s soil. Or maybe what characterizes that area is the exact opposite of homelessness? Perhaps what you witness there are little architectural acts of ownership? Thinking about this was what lead me to look into replica cities in the first place actually.
>
> — Jesper Just, ['The Venice Questionnaire #1: Jesper Just', ArtReview, 1 May 2013](https://artreview.com/1-venice-jesper-just/)

<sub>asks</sub>

> I was thinking about ways to make the city the protagonist or mediator between these characters, making them connect via the architecture. I wanted to explore how you could take something as superficial as this architecture and then turn it into something connecting humans.
>
> — Jesper Just, [Danish Arts Council press announcement 'Jesper Just’s Intercourses', e-flux, 2013](https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/32711/jesper-just-s-intercourses)
