# Still Life: Icon and Fetish (manifestation 1)

**Harshit Agrawal**, 2021 · AI / generative / Text / print

A grid of archival prints on canvas (54 x 54 inches) made with AI models trained on a dataset of European floral still-life paintings. The central image comes from a model trained slowly on whole paintings; the surrounding images come from a model trained quickly on randomly cropped details of the same paintings. It was shown in custom frames in Agrawal's solo exhibition EXO-stential: AI Musings on the Posthuman at Emami Art, Kolkata, in September 2021; a related video work is titled (Un)Still Life.

## In their words

> Positing the question, can machines be taught aesthetics, here the artist trains a machine to look at thousands of still-life paintings, some in their entirety, and some in their details, to try and guide a machine to learn both composition and the painterly nuances of aesthetic.
>
> — Harshit Agrawal, [(Un)Still Life- Icon and Fetish, artist's website](https://harshitagrawal.com/unstill-life-icon-and-fetish/)

<sub>says</sub>

> In any painting what is the relation of the part (as fetish) to the whole (as icon)? How can one teach a computer compositional structure and painterly texture? This work tries to start poking into these set of questions.
>
> — Harshit Agrawal, [(Un)Still Life- Icon and Fetish, artist's website (archival print on canvas)](https://harshitagrawal.com/unstill-life-icon-and-fetish/)

<sub>asks</sub>

> For the second AI, the learning rate is kept fast since the attempt is for the machine to learn abstracted visuals from the dataset - the painterly as opposed to the compositional of the first. Thus, experimenting with both treatments of the dataset and the AI learning process, a new study in aesthetics of icon and fetish emerges.
>
> — Harshit Agrawal, [STIR, 'Harshit Agrawal's Exo-stential is India's first solo artificial intelligence art show' (interview by Rahul Kumar), 5 October 2021](https://www.stirworld.com/see-features-harshit-agrawals-exo-stential-is-india-s-first-solo-artificial-intelligence-art-show)

<sub>says</sub>

> Beyond the conceptual, I am interested in exploring the evolution of aesthetics too enabled by the process of teaching machines a visual language from given examples and varying the training process to produce different visual outcomes, like in Still Life: Icon and Fetish .
>
> — Harshit Agrawal, [STIR, 'Harshit Agrawal's Exo-stential is India's first solo artificial intelligence art show' (interview by Rahul Kumar), 5 October 2021](https://www.stirworld.com/see-features-harshit-agrawals-exo-stential-is-india-s-first-solo-artificial-intelligence-art-show)

>
> — Harshit Agrawal, [STIR, 'Harshit Agrawal's Exo-stential is India's first solo artificial intelligence art show' (interview by Rahul Kumar), 5 October 2021](https://www.stirworld.com/see-features-harshit-agrawals-exo-stential-is-india-s-first-solo-artificial-intelligence-art-show)
