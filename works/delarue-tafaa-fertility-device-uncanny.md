# TAFAA – Fertility Device (Uncanny Valley)

**Chloé Delarue**, 2022 · Material / sculpture / Screen / monitor / Light

TAFAA – Fertility Device (Uncanny Valley) is a sculpture from Chloé Delarue's FERTILITY DEVICE group, part of her ongoing TAFAA (Toward A Fully Automated Appearance) cycle, and is in the collection of HeK (House of Electronic Arts), Basel. Works in the FERTILITY DEVICE group, as documented by her gallery and other collections, combine metal frames, LCD screens showing video, latex, glass, resin and neon or LED light into free-standing or wall-leaning objects that visitors walk around.
