# If You Have Starry Skies in Your Eyes

**Rib**, 2024 · Performance / body / Sensor / interaction / Light

A prosthetic eye designed, made and worn by the artist, who lost the sight of her right eye as a child; it contains a magnetic sensor, an LED and a battery encapsulated in medical-grade acrylic resin, and lights up when a magnet is brought near. The project, presented with a video, follows an earlier starry-sky-patterned prosthetic eye she wears daily, and received an Award of Distinction in Interactive Art + at Prix Ars Electronica 2024.
