# Real-time AR visual show for Nosaj Thing

**Daito Manabe**, 2016 · Camera / computer vision / Performance / body / Sound

A touring live audiovisual show by Daito Manabe (Rhizomatiks) and the musician Nosaj Thing (Jason Chung), developed from a 2015 prototype for Taico Club in Japan and presented at Coachella 2016 and at Sónar festivals. Kinect depth cameras around the stage, a motion-capture suit and head camera worn by Nosaj Thing, and a camera operator moving on stage feed a system that renders the performers as 3-D figures in real time, synchronised with MIDI and audio from his laptop. The audience sees the two performers on stage and, on the screens, a computer-generated version of the same scene from changing camera viewpoints.

>
> — Daito Manabe, [Interview I Daito Manabe, by Elena Giulia Rossi, Arshake, 2017 (answer to a question about the show with Nosaj Thing at Sónar)](https://www.arshake.com/en/interview-i-daito-manabe/)

<sub>says</sub>

> It’s called No Reality because on stage you're able to see everything that’s happening live with us right in front of you, but also an alternate reality through the camera screens,
>
> — Nosaj Thing (Jason Chung), collaborator, [Nosaj Thing: A Visual and Musical History, Ableton blog](https://www.ableton.com/en/blog/nosaj-thing-visual-and-musical-history/)

<sub>says</sub>

> That was all happening live in parallel, so it was like being able to generate a parallel reality live, where you’re able to change the camera’s perspective like in a video game.
>
> — Nosaj Thing (Jason Chung), collaborator, [Nosaj Thing: A Visual and Musical History, Ableton blog](https://www.ableton.com/en/blog/nosaj-thing-visual-and-musical-history/)

<sub>says</sub>
