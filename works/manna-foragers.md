# Foragers

**Jumana Manna**, 2022 · Projection / Sound

A film of about an hour combining documentary, scripted fiction and archival footage, shot mainly in the Galilee, the Golan Heights and around Jerusalem. It follows Palestinian foragers gathering za'atar and 'akkoub, plants whose wild harvesting is restricted under Israeli nature-protection law, and stages court hearings co-scripted with lawyer Rabea Eghbariah. Viewers watch it as a film in cinemas, festivals and exhibitions.

## In their words

> Aside from all of that background research of the past ten years, this film actually really began with my parents, who are avid foragers. In the springtime, they are out every weekend collecting and filling the refrigerator with green leaves.
>
> — Jumana Manna, [Jumana Manna and Rabea Eghbariah in Conversation, MoMA PS1 (edited excerpt of the talkback after the screening)](https://www.momaps1.org/en/post/213-jumana-manna-and-rabea-eghbariah-in-conversation)

<sub>says</sub>

> I wanted joy to be felt as much if not stronger than the anger towards the criminalization of the practice.
>
> — Jumana Manna, [Jumana Manna and Rabea Eghbariah in Conversation, MoMA PS1](https://www.momaps1.org/en/post/213-jumana-manna-and-rabea-eghbariah-in-conversation)

<sub>says</sub>

> Marte Vold, the DP I worked with most closely, and I thought a lot about how to develop a “foraging camera.” A movement that kind of mimics the act of foraging. A handheld movement, with close up shots focused on the ground. It’s not an eye that’s looking at the landscape as such.
>
> — Jumana Manna, [Jumana Manna and Rabea Eghbariah in Conversation, MoMA PS1](https://www.momaps1.org/en/post/213-jumana-manna-and-rabea-eghbariah-in-conversation)

<sub>says</sub>

> But actually, the main characters are the plants through which we understand the colonial dynamics of the law, the village relations around the collecting or buying of these foods, and the extractive economies of the Israeli industrialized plantations.
>
> — Jumana Manna, [Jumana Manna and Rabea Eghbariah in Conversation, MoMA PS1](https://www.momaps1.org/en/post/213-jumana-manna-and-rabea-eghbariah-in-conversation)

<sub>says</sub>

> Foraging is an ancient means for survival. It never died out with the rise of agriculture, but rather continued as an adjacent practice.
>
> — Jumana Manna, [Jumana Manna and Rabea Eghbariah in Conversation, MoMA PS1](https://www.momaps1.org/en/post/213-jumana-manna-and-rabea-eghbariah-in-conversation)
