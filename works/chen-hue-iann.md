# huě iànn

**Yu-Han Chen, Ching-Wen Hsu, Ruei-Sin Jhang, Li-Hua Zhan, Tung-En Chien, Pei-Chi Wu**, 2023 · VR / AR / XR

huě iànn is a VR animated video made by a six-person team from the Department of Visual Communication Design at Jinwen University of Science and Technology, Taiwan. It addresses events in Taiwan's history connected to freedom of speech, starting from the Taiyuan incident. It received an Honorary Mention in New Animation Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2023.
