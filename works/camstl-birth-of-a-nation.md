# Birth (of a Nation)

**Centro Audiovisual Max Stahl Timor-Leste (CAMSTL)**, 2019 · Screen / monitor / Sound / Network / data

Birth (of a Nation) is a seven-channel video installation with soundscape and computer terminal (about one hour) by Centro Audiovisual Max Stahl Timor-Leste (CAMSTL), commissioned for the Singapore Biennale 2019. It presents material drawn from the CAMSTL archive, founded in 2003 and holding more than 6,000 hours of audiovisual material on Timor-Leste, through which, according to the Singapore Art Museum, visitors encounter the country's history and culture and the Timorese struggle for democracy and nationhood. The archive's independence-struggle collection, 'On the Birth of a Nation: Turning Points', was inscribed in UNESCO's Memory of the World Register.

## In their words

> In the CAMSTL audiovisual archive, we document many different aspects of the birth of the nation Timor-Leste through many stories: we have 4000 hours of material at present. We don’t know entirely what the story will be; we don’t do what one would normally do.
>
> — Max Stahl (founder and director of CAMSTL), [Max Stahl, 'How skilful communication won the real story' (keynote extract), Pacific Journalism Review 21(1), 2015](https://ojs.aut.ac.nz/pacific-journalism-review/article/download/123/90)
