# GR-AI-N

**Marielena Papandreou**, 2024 · Robotics / kinetics / Camera / computer vision / Material / sculpture

GR-AI-N is a digital-craft workflow that uses discarded offcuts from the wood industry: pieces are scanned, AI-powered tools extract their grain patterns, texture and imperfections, and robotic assembly and milling build objects (mirrors, a headboard) as 3D mosaics of irregular pieces that keep the grain continuous. It was developed in the VOJEXT S+T+ARTS residency 'Robotics in Arts and Crafts' in Barcelona and was nominated for the 2024 S+T+ARTS Prize.

## In their words

> We often view wood as a raw material for shaping our world. Yet our creations come from the sacrifice of a living organism. A tree is a living record of Earth’s climate, capturing every environmental variation within its growth rings.

> — Marielena Papandreou, [Project text, S+T+ARTS Prize 2024 entry, Ars Electronica archive](https://archive.aec.at/startsprize/288853/)

> Regrettably, our production processes are designed not only to strip away their anatomy, their life’s tale, but also to discard nearly half of their potential. Is there a way to adapt our methods in order to waste less but also work with the tree’s story, weave it into the creations we make?
>
> — Marielena Papandreou, [Project text, S+T+ARTS Prize 2024 entry, Ars Electronica archive](https://archive.aec.at/startsprize/288853/)

<sub>asks · unsigned project text</sub>

> Could we bridge the gap between image or point-cloud generators and the realities of making? Instead of starting with a rigid design and attempting to fit the material to it, this approach begins with the material itself and uses it as input for an AI design assistant.
>
> — Marielena Papandreou, [Winning proposal text, VOJEXT S+T+ARTS residency](https://vojext.eu/challenge-driven-robotics-in-arts-crafts/)
