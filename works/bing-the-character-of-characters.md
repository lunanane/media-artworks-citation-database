# The Character of Characters: An Animation by Xu Bing

**Xu Bing**, 2012 · Projection / Screen / monitor / Text / print

The Character of Characters is a five-channel animated video installation of about 17 minutes, commissioned by the Asian Art Museum of San Francisco for the 2012 exhibition Out of Character: Decoding Chinese Calligraphy. Shown as one wide landscape across five projections, it begins with the single stroke of the character for 'one' and moves through calligraphy, painting (including Zhao Mengfu's Lotus Sutra calligraphy and Autumn Colors on the Qiao and Hua Mountains), diagrams and text, touching on the genesis of writing, copying in art and society, and symbols and brand names in contemporary China.
