# Makȟóčhe Kiŋ Oíč'iwa (The Land Paints Itself)

**Kite**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative / Sound

A digital video with sound commissioned by CIRCUIT Artist Moving Image and shown in the group exhibition Leap to the Place of Two Pools at Te Pātaka Toi Adam Art Gallery, Wellington (1 February – 6 April 2025). According to the gallery, the work pairs technologies of dreaming and AI to imagine Indigenous futures patterned across unceded land, marked by stones whose own language emerges as a conduit to multivocal storytelling.
