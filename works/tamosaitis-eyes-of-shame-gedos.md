# Eyes of Shame (Gėdos akys)

**Tomas Tamošaitis**, 2026 · VR / AR / XR

Eyes of Shame is a 40-minute animated virtual reality film (Lithuania, France, Croatia). It follows 14-year-old Eyeless in a hospital for troubled teenagers: when a nurse forces her to eat, unfinished memories begin to interrupt the present, and the viewer is placed in her point of view. It premiered in the Venice Immersive competition in 2026 and draws on the director's volunteering at a hospital for teenagers in Vilnius, where he has run animation workshops since 2014.

## In their words

> I didn’t enter a hospital for troubled teenagers to research a film. I simply wanted to volunteer. I watched children hesitate before drawing a single line, convinced they could not create anything. I heard adults speak about them in front of them, as if the children were deaf.
>
> — Tomas Tamošaitis, [Main Creator's Statement, Eyes of Shame (Gėdos akys), Venice Immersive 2026, La Biennale di Venezia](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2026/venice-immersive/eyes-shame-g%C4%97dos-akys)

<sub>says</sub>

> Judgements about what children can or cannot do. Repeated often enough to become part of how a child begins to describe themselves. I asked myself: at what point does someone else’s voice become our own? EYES OF SHAME grew from that question. The story begins in a hospital, but it is not about a hospital.
>
> — Tomas Tamošaitis, [Main Creator's Statement, Eyes of Shame (Gėdos akys), Venice Immersive 2026, La Biennale di Venezia](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2026/venice-immersive/eyes-shame-g%C4%97dos-akys)

<sub>asks</sub>

> We Aim With Eyes Of Shame To Give A View On Social Exclusion And Alienation Of Troubled Teenagers And Evoke Reconsideration Of Social Values And Social Phobias, Resulting In Systematic Discrimination.
>
> — Project team (Tomas Tamosaitis, Joni Art; Emmanuel Rouglan, Insomniak; Sara Bozanic, ITD), [Project listing, XR Development Market, NewImages Festival 2022 (Swapcard)](https://app.swapcard.com/event/newimages-festival-2022-1/exhibitor/RXhoaWJpdG9yXzc3ODQ3MQ==)
