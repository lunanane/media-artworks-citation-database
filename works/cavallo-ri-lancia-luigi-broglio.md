# Ri-lancia Luigi Broglio (Re-launching Luigi Broglio)

**Vincenzo Cavallo**, 2025 · VR / AR / XR / Performance / body

Ri-lancia Luigi Broglio is a 33-minute 360° VR film directed by Vincenzo Cavallo, staged like a theatre piece, about Luigi Broglio, who launched Italy into space from the San Marco sea platform off Kenya in the 1960s. The viewer watches a confrontation between Broglio and a journalist in the abandoned command centre, with flashbacks to the African expedition, an encounter with Enrico Mattei and a near-diplomatic crisis with President Nasser. It was supported by Biennale College Cinema Immersive and shown out of competition at Venice Immersive 2025.

## In their words

> As Italians who are living in Kenya we relentlessly hunt for untold stories woven between Africa and Europe. Italians who ended up in Africa during and after colonialism, Africans and Afro- Italians who helped forge Italy, before, during and after World War II — a vast cultural heritage overlooked for various reasons.
>
> — Vincenzo Cavallo, [Main Creator's Statement, La Biennale di Venezia, Venice Immersive 2025](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2025/venice-immersive/ri-lancia-luigi-broglio-re-launching-luigi-broglio)
