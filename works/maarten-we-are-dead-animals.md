# We Are Dead Animals

**Tote Tiere Maarten**, 2025 · VR / AR / XR / Sensor / interaction / Material / sculpture

We Are Dead Animals is a VR game, premiered at IDFA DocLab 2025, built from 3D scans (photograms) of dead animals the artist found in his surroundings, placed in a landscape that is his interpretation of paradise. Players fly through it with hand-tracking, revive animals, read about how they died, collect them, combine their body parts into new creatures, release them or eat them to extend a limited lifespan. At IDFA it was installed inside a nest built of organic material.
