# The Great Orator

**Daniel Ernst**, 2025 · VR / AR / XR / AI / generative / Network / data

The Great Orator is a 40-minute nonlinear VR story world by Daniel Ernst (The Shoebox Diorama) about a TV preacher whose consciousness lives on as an AI after her death. The visitor explores spaces such as a preserved house, a mainframe holding her followers' shared consciousness and an infinite parking lot, and points a hand at objects to receive her memories, which are generated from the visitor's interaction, random Internet input and live news feeds and differ on each visit. It premiered at Venice Immersive 2025.

## In their words

> The Great Orator feeds on your interaction, mixed with random Internet input and live news feeds. She mixes truths and fabulations in her storytelling, thus reflecting the misinformation and normalisation of falsehoods used to fit our narratives in today’s world.
>
> — Daniel Ernst, [Main creator's statement, La Biennale di Venezia, Venice Immersive 2025](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2025/venice-immersive/great-orator)
