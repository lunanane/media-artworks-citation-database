# F\*ckai? (Famous)

**Jordan Clarke**, 2024 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative

F\*ckai? (Famous) is a five-minute 3D-animated short film by Toronto director and animator Jordan Clarke. Its story, about an AI that wanted to be famous, was generated by OpenAI in response to his prompt and voiced by an AI, then paraphrased and edited by Clarke, who animated, composited, sound-designed and edited the film himself. It closes his 'f\*ckai?' series of short films and received an Honorary Mention in New Animation Art at the 2024 Prix Ars Electronica.

## In their words

> This is a story about an A.I. that wanted to be famous, also written and performed by an A.I. (paraphrased and edited by a human) and visualized by a human (me), flipping the norm of what we see in the AI-driven art world today.
>
> — Jordan Clarke, [Stash Magazine, 'Jordan Clarke Meets an AI who Wants to be Famous' (23 November 2022)](https://www.stashmedia.tv/jordan-clarke-meets-an-ai-who-wants-to-be-famous/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> Lately a lot of people have been generating images by prompting A.I. (Midjourney, Dall-e, etc.). In this video, I wanted to flip this idea and have the A.I. generate the story and the human (me) would create the images based on the story it wrote.
>
> — Jordan Clarke, [Stash Magazine, 'Jordan Clarke Flips the AI Script With His New Short Film F\*ckai? (sexy)' (28 September 2022)](https://www.stashmedia.tv/?p=91089)
