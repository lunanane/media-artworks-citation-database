# Diary of a Madman

**Cheng Ran**, 2016 · Screen / monitor / Sound

A multi-video installation of fifteen videos made during a three-month residency at the New Museum, New York, in partnership with K11 Art Foundation, and shown in the museum's Lobby Gallery from October 2016 to January 2017. The diary-like vignettes, played on screens hung at varying heights, include footage from early-morning walks in New York, a trip to Staten Island Bay and a visit to an abandoned psychiatric hospital on Long Island; videos with dialogue carry Mandarin and English subtitles. The title is taken from Lu Xun's 1918 short story.
