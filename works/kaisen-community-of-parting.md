# Community of Parting

**Jane Jin Kaisen**, 2019 · Projection / Sound

Community of Parting is a 72-minute film presented as a double-channel video installation in the Korean Pavilion of the 58th Venice Biennale (2019). It invokes the Korean shamanic myth of the abandoned princess Bari and combines shamanic ritual performances and chants by shaman Koh Sunahn of Jeju Island, nature and cityscapes, archival material, aerial imagery, poetry, voiceover and soundscapes. It was filmed in locations including Jeju Island, the DMZ, South Korea, North Korea, Kazakhstan, Japan, China, the United States and Germany, with narratives by South Korean, North Korean and diasporic women.

## In their words

> “Community of Parting” was compelled by a need to engage borders otherwise, after interfacing with the mechanism of borders as they manifest as gender bias, and in the geopolitical enforcements of borders, including the division of Korea and its Cold War polarities.
>
> — Jane Jin Kaisen, [Alice Ko, 'The Translator and the Medium: A Conversation with Jane Jin Kaisen', No Man's Land (heath.tw)](https://www.heath.tw/nml-article/the-translator-and-the-medium-a-conversation-with-jane-jin-kaisen/?lang=en)

>
> — Jane Jin Kaisen, [Alice Ko, 'The Translator and the Medium: A Conversation with Jane Jin Kaisen', No Man's Land (heath.tw)](https://www.heath.tw/nml-article/the-translator-and-the-medium-a-conversation-with-jane-jin-kaisen/?lang=en)

> One of the great forces of the myth of Bari, to me, lies in how it reconfigures the notion of abandonment from one of victimization to one of agency and mediating capacity.
