# Transition sequence for a dream or fight

**Sonya Lacey**, 2025 · Projection / Material / sculpture

Transition sequence for a dream or fight is a silent stop-frame animation made from acid-etched copper plates, which borrows the cloud-like scene-change formations used in historical animated productions. It was commissioned by CIRCUIT Artist Moving Image for the group exhibition Leap to the Place of Two Pools, curated by Erin Robideaux Gleeson, at Te Pātaka Toi Adam Art Gallery, Wellington (1 February – 6 April 2025).
