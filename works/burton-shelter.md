# Shelter

**Carl Burton**, 2013 · Screen / monitor

Shelter is a six-minute computer-animated short film with no dialogue. It begins in a fairly ordinary attic during a rainstorm, which becomes more and more surreal as the film goes on. Carl Burton made it with Cinema 4D and Adobe software, mostly during the month he takes off each year for personal work. It received an Honorary Mention in Computer Animation/Film/VFX at Prix Ars Electronica 2013 and was screened at SIGGRAPH Asia 2012 and Slamdance 2013.
