# Pollinator Pathmaker

**Alexandra Daisy Ginsberg**, 2021– · AI / generative / Online / net art / Material / sculpture

An algorithmic garden-design tool and website (pollinator.art) that generates planting designs computed to support the greatest number of pollinator species, choosing from curated regional plant palettes. Users select their garden conditions, receive a unique design they can explore in 3D or with a 'pollinator vision' view, and download planting instructions with an edition certificate; each planted garden is an edition of the work. Commissioned Editions have been planted at the Eden Project, Cornwall (2022), for the Serpentine in Kensington Gardens, London, and outside the Museum für Naturkunde Berlin (LAS Art Foundation); it won the S+T+ARTS Grand Prize 2023.

## In their words

> I’m not offering a technofix. It’s a problem so vast, caused by human destruction of habitats, pollution of ecosystems, and creation of climate change. Pollinator Pathmaker is about transforming our perception of the natural world and giving participants the agency to care for it, rather than consume it.
>
> — Alexandra Daisy Ginsberg, [Alex Estorick, 'Living Artwork', interview with Alexandra Daisy Ginsberg, Right Click Save, 25 November 2024](https://www.rightclicksave.com/article/living-artwork-alexandra-daisy-ginsberg-interview)
