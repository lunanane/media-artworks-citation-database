# Electrical Walks

**Christina Kubisch**, 2004– · Sound / Network / data / Performance / body

Visitors borrow custom wireless headphones with built-in induction coils and a map of a city district on which the artist has marked routes and strong electromagnetic fields. Walking alone, in a group or on a guided tour, they hear the hums, pulses and drones of shop security gates, ATMs, trams, Wi-Fi, lighting and other infrastructure that normally cannot be heard. The series has run in cities worldwide since 2004, with walk no. 100 announced for Budapest in October 2026.
