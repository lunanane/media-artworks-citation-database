# Ausente

**Paolo Almario**, 2026 · Camera / computer vision / AI / generative / Projection

An interactive installation (Dyscrimina / Ausente) presented at the 44th International Symposium of Contemporary Art of Baie-Saint-Paul: a surveillance-style projection shows visitors in real time, and people are erased from the frame. Across four stations using AI and computer vision, the public collectively teaches the machine which characteristics to recognize; those outside the resulting vocabulary are designated « non-choisi.e » and progressively removed from the image.

## In their words

> Through my work, I question how we negotiate our existence and our agency within the systems we inhabit. I am interested in the relationship between individuals and the natural, social, political, technological, and digital structures that surround us. Systems identify, classify, and represent us. They organize bodies, territories, information, and memory, and can also produce exclusion or disappearance.
>
> — Paolo Almario, [Paolo Almario, artist statement (paoloalmario.com)](https://paoloalmario.com/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> I construct machines, algorithms, protocols, and situations and allow them to act: to classify, accumulate, transform, reveal, deteriorate, erase. Through these operations, processes that might remain abstract become physical, temporal, perceptible. A system can be experienced through what it does.
>
> — Paolo Almario, [Paolo Almario, artist statement (paoloalmario.com)](https://paoloalmario.com/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> I do not try to resolve these tensions. I try to create conditions in which they become perceptible and can be experienced. What returns throughout the practice is this negotiation: between individual and system, agency and constraint, presence and disappearance.
>
> — Paolo Almario, [Paolo Almario, artist statement (paoloalmario.com)](https://paoloalmario.com/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> The work asks how the collective gestures of a society align into a system that decides who is to be excluded, targeted, eliminated, and how today's technological tools extend that capacity for segregation: documenting, locating and targeting individuals under cover of notions such as sovereignty.
>
> — Paolo Almario, [Paolo Almario, Dyscrimina / Ausente project page (paoloalmario.com)](https://paoloalmario.com/portfolio/dyscrimina-ausente/)

> i have no oil or marble: my brushes are machines, and my creation happens in zeros and ones. the subject of my work, the one i cannot stop looking straight in the face: the ease with which a few appropriate the institutions and bend them to the measure of their interest.
>
> — Paolo Almario, [Paolo Almario, manifesto (paoloalmario.com/manifesto), Baie-Saint-Paul, 2026](https://paoloalmario.com/manifesto)
