# Wellenwald mit Bunker

**David Ebner**, 2017 · Sound / Material / sculpture / Network / data

Wellenwald mit Bunker is an installation of a spruce trunk covered in aluminium foil, leaning on a concrete object shaped like a bunker; antennae mounted on the trunk are wired into the concrete object. Inside, a speaker plays a composed drone sound file together with signals the antennae pick up from electromagnetic waves of nearby devices such as mobile phones, so visitors' devices alter the sound in real time. It is a reinterpretation of Joseph Beuys's 1972 work Fichtenstamm mit Munitionskiste (Munitionskiste mit Fichtenstamm).

## In their words

> The original work is transformed into a contemporary interpretation through the use of material and sound referring to topics like the digital age and the impact of technology.
>
> — David Ebner (Prix Ars Electronica catalogue text), [Wellenwald mit Bunker, Prix Ars Electronica 2017 archive, catalog text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/226549/)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> The sound stands in contrast to the visual part of the installation: while the spruce trunk and bunker are placed on the ground, the sound is a hovering counterpart that spreads out, is moving.
>
> — David Ebner (Prix Ars Electronica catalogue text), [Wellenwald mit Bunker, Prix Ars Electronica 2017 archive, catalog text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/226549/)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> Furthermore, signals that are not communicating become audible. There is no dialog between signal and listener, the content appears closed, drawing the attention of the spectator and provoking he/him to listen carefully. The present digital world appears to be connected yet remains without content and intransparent at the same time.
>
> — David Ebner (Prix Ars Electronica catalogue text), [Wellenwald mit Bunker, Prix Ars Electronica 2017 archive, catalog text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/226549/)
