# Loophole for All

**Paolo Cirio**, 2014 · Online / net art / Network / data / Text / print

Loophole for All is an online intervention in which the artist exposed over 200,000 companies registered in the Cayman Islands, leaking their names from the government registry, and offered the identities of these anonymous offshore companies for low-cost sale on the website Loophole4All.com, issuing forged Certificates of Incorporation under his own name and signature. He also set up his own company in the City of London and produced a video documentary of interviews with experts on offshore centres. In exhibitions the work is shown as two video channels and digital prints of the certificates and legal documents, with a flowchart of the operation.
