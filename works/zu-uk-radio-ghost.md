# RADIO GHOST

**ZU-UK**, 2022 · Sound / Performance / body

Radio Ghost is an audio-driven, app-based walking game for three players set in a shopping centre, part game, part interactive radio show and part ghost hunt. Players act as ghost hunters, following audio prompts on phones and blending in as shoppers to unlock stories of the mall connected to the supply chains of the goods on sale. It was commissioned by LIFT Festival 2022 and won the Lumen Prize Interactive Award 2022.

## In their words

> Shopping malls epitomise the top of the supply chain. These mammoth, glorious, mega-palaces of consumption are filled to the brim with stuff, objects, products, goods, material artefacts for sale. They make me think about the fact that every single item there has, in the making of it, been touched by the bare hands of someone in impoverished conditions, somewhere, very far away.
>
> — Persis Jadé Maravala (ZU-UK), [LIFT Festival, 'LIFTing the curtain on Radio Ghost' (interview, 4 Aug 2022; archived copy)](http://web.archive.org/web/20221128224314/https://www.liftfestival.com/lifting-the-curtain-radio-ghost/)

<sub>says</sub>

> It’s a Shinto belief that everything has a spirit, and I was interested in unlocking these ‘ghosts’ that betoken the hidden stories of the supply chain.
>
> — Persis Jadé Maravala (ZU-UK), [LIFT Festival, 'LIFTing the curtain on Radio Ghost' (interview, 4 Aug 2022; archived copy)](http://web.archive.org/web/20221128224314/https://www.liftfestival.com/lifting-the-curtain-radio-ghost/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Ultimately, we wanted to prompt responses to questions about society, consumerism and loneliness. To make a game that would be played out against the ordinary backdrop of a shopping mall but in which the familiar would turn hauntingly strange.
>
> — Persis Jadé Maravala (ZU-UK), [LIFT Festival, 'LIFTing the curtain on Radio Ghost' (interview, 4 Aug 2022; archived copy)](http://web.archive.org/web/20221128224314/https://www.liftfestival.com/lifting-the-curtain-radio-ghost/)

<sub>says</sub>

> But the question remains – what do we do with the knowledge of the suffering of others? A simple thing that the game suggests is that all we are asking for is informed consumer decision-making – the right to know.
>
> — Persis Jadé Maravala (ZU-UK), [LIFT Festival, 'LIFTing the curtain on Radio Ghost' (interview, 4 Aug 2022; archived copy)](http://web.archive.org/web/20221128224314/https://www.liftfestival.com/lifting-the-curtain-radio-ghost/)

<sub>asks</sub>

> It is about learning to put the blame where it belongs and how it is that certain things came to be in our hands, delivered to us by the hands of ghosts… and that no matter how hard you dust, you’ll not find their fingerprints.
>
> — ZU-UK, [ZU-UK project page, Radio Ghost](https://zu-uk.com/project/radio-ghost/)
