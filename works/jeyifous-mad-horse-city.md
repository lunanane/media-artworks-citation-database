# Mad Horse City

**Olalekan Jeyifous & Wale Lawal**, 2018 · Screen / monitor / VR / AR / XR / Text / print

Mad Horse City is a collaboration between architect-artist Olalekan Jeyifous and writer Wale Lawal that imagines Lagos in the year 2115 through three fictional 'moments' – Offline, Òmìníra and Dreamscape – presented as experimental animations, a digital slideshow, a 360 VR experience and a graphic novella. In Offline a woman visits an illegal botanical garden where people pay to disconnect; in Òmìníra two scavengers from a fishing community inspired by Makoko test the limits of their society; in Dreamscape a young man illegally downloads the dreams of people in other jurisdictions. It was shown in Africa Is Not a Refugee Camp at the Architekturmuseum der TU München and in Digital Imaginaries at ZKM Karlsruhe (2018–19).

## In their words

> This project imagines the city of Lagos in 2115, and explores the interactions between bodies and spaces both virtual and physical across three fictional narratives or “moments”.
>
> — Olalekan Jeyifous & Wale Lawal (project text on Jeyifous's site), [jeyifo.us, 'African Mobilities I, II – Mad Horse City'](https://jeyifo.us/African)
