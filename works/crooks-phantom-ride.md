# Phantom Ride

**Daniel Crooks**, 2016 · Projection / Screen / monitor

Phantom Ride is a two-channel video installation by Daniel Crooks, made as the second Ian Potter Moving Image Commission and shown at ACMI, Melbourne (16 February – 29 May 2016); it is in the ACMI collection. Footage of tracks in urban and country settings (tram tracks, tram repair depots, bridges and tunnels along disused railways), shot with a computerised dolly, is stitched into what appears to be a single continuous journey referencing the 'phantom rides' of early cinema. It is projected on a double-sided screen: one side shows the journey travelling forward, the other the view travelling backward.

## In their words

> Phantom Ride comes out of my long-held fascination with the convergence of trains, the birth of cinema and modern ideas and representations of time.
>
> — Daniel Crooks, [Phantom Ride, ACMI collection page](https://www.acmi.net.au/works/119--phantom-ride)

<sub>says</sub>

> I’ve presented it as a two-sided video; the forward-facing journey on one side and the rear looking journey on the reverse. The screen becomes a meniscus of the present, separating the past and the future.
>
> — Daniel Crooks, [Phantom Ride, ACMI collection page](https://www.acmi.net.au/works/119--phantom-ride)

<sub>says</sub>

> You have one side looking into the future, the other side looking into the past and then the screen itself is this moment of now—a very thin meniscus where the present exists.
>
> — Daniel Crooks, [Gail Priest, 'Screen as time lens' (interview, Daniel Crooks, Phantom Ride), RealTime, March 2016](https://www.realtime.org.au/?p=16213)

<sub>says</sub>

> I’ve always been interested in the ‘multiple worlds’ interpretation—that there are parallel universes spiralling off from every possible causal event. It was a way to start talking about that a little bit more explicitly.
>
> — Daniel Crooks, [Gail Priest, 'Screen as time lens' (interview, Daniel Crooks, Phantom Ride), RealTime, March 2016](https://www.realtime.org.au/?p=16213)
