# From Noise

**Tyler Hobbs**, 2025 · AI / generative / Network / data / Text / print

From Noise is a series of 12 NFTs from one algorithm by Tyler Hobbs that translates the spontaneous gestural marks of painters such as Cy Twombly and Joan Mitchell into code; each work contains more than 4,000 marks. At Zero 10, Art Basel Miami Beach 2025 (SOLOS stand), it was shown as four large-format prints on wood varnished by the artist, with boxed sets of screen prints, Translated Gestures, taken from details of the outputs.

## In their words

> From Noise is an algorithmic response to the notion of the gesture, and gestural abstraction as practiced in the 20th century. When our hands no longer manually control the tools of artistic creation, what becomes of the gesture? Is there still a way in which the gesture evolves, continuing to represent the vitality of the creator?
>
> — Tyler Hobbs, ['The Translated Gesture', text signed 'by Tyler Hobbs', SOLOS exhibition page From Noise](https://solos.gallery/exhibitions/from-noise)
