# The Visitors

**Ragnar Kjartansson**, 2012 · Screen / monitor / Sound / Performance / body

The Visitors is a nine-channel video installation with sound, 64 minutes long, in which musicians, each filmed alone in a different room of the 43-room Rokeby mansion in upstate New York, play together a song by Kjartansson and Davíð Þór Jónsson with lyrics by Ásdís Sif Gunnarsdóttir. Visitors walk among the nine screens; residents of the house appear on the terrace, and the performers finally leave the house together. It was commissioned by the Migros Museum für Gegenwartskunst, Zurich, and named after ABBA's final album.
