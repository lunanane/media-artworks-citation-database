# Avatar Robot Cafe DAWN ver.β

**Ory Yoshifuji / Ory Lab**, 2022 · Robotics / kinetics / Network / data

Avatar Robot Cafe DAWN ver.β is a permanent café in Nihonbashi, Tokyo, run by Ory Lab, where people who have difficulty going out because of illness, disability, caregiving or distance work as “pilots”, remotely operating the avatar robots OriHime and OriHime-D from home or hospital through the robots' cameras, microphones and speakers, in some cases by eye-gaze input. Visitors are seated, order, are served drinks and talk with the pilots through the robots; the project also matches pilots with employers through Ory Lab's Avatar Guild staffing agency. It received the Golden Nica in Digital Communities at Prix Ars Electronica 2022.

## In their words

> We'd like to give people lifestyle choices that can lead to friendships and fulfilling roles in society regardless of any physical barriers. For myself as well, one day when I'm no longer able to move around freely, I'd like to be prepared. That's our goal with OriHime—our vision of how we can eliminate people's loneliness.
>
> — Ory Yoshifuji, [Catalogue text, Prix Ars Electronica Archive, Golden Nica Digital Communities 2022](https://archive.aec.at/prix/268808)
