# Of Men and Gods and Mud

**Ali Cherri**, 2022 · Screen / monitor / Material / sculpture

A three-channel video installation shown at the 59th Venice Biennale (The Milk of Dreams), where Cherri received the Silver Lion for a Promising Young Participant. Shot mainly at a brickyard near the Merowe Dam on the Nile in northern Sudan, it follows a seasonal brickmaker who shapes mud into bricks by day and at night secretly builds a structure of mud and scrap that becomes a creature. It was shown alongside drawings of mud creatures and three sculptural figures, Titans.

## In their words

> At Venice Biennale, a new three channel video installation entitled 'Of Men and Gods and Mud' was made from images that I shot mainly on a brickyard in Sudan.
>
> — Ali Cherri, [Artbreath interview](https://artbreath.org/interviews/ali-cherri)

<sub>says</sub>

> It speaks of the mythologies around mud and the diverse religious beliefs of creation through it. Whether we refer to the biblical figure Adam, Enkidu, Golem, or so many other religious or mythological figures, humanity saw clay as the material of creation, where life came through mud.
>
> — Ali Cherri, [Artbreath interview](https://artbreath.org/interviews/ali-cherri)

<sub>says</sub>

> Mud has a dual aspect; destruction or deluge happens a lot along the Nile, where muddy waters rise up, destroy and can even kill people, but mud is also the fertiliser that permits for the earth to prosper.
>
> — Ali Cherri, [Artbreath interview](https://artbreath.org/interviews/ali-cherri)

<sub>says</sub>

> I work with imagination as a political strategy and tool for change,
>
> — Ali Cherri, [Canvas, profile (Canvas 109)](https://canvasonline.com/ali-cherri/)

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> Mud has the ability to hold or break. It is the material manifestation of violence, of trauma, of loss, but also of sedimentation and prosperity. Mud can be an entry point for reconstruction histories, especially histories of violence.
>
> — Ali Cherri, [BOMB, interview with Dina A. Ramadan (2023), reposted by Swiss Institute](https://wp.swissinstitute.net/press/ali-cherri-humble-and-quiet-and-soothing-as-mud-3/)
