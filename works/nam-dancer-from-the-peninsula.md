# Dancer from the Peninsula

**Hwayeon Nam**, 2019 · Screen / monitor / Performance / body / Material / sculpture

Dancer from the Peninsula is a multi-channel video installation made for the Korean Pavilion of the 58th Venice Biennale (History Has Failed Us, but No Matter, 2019). It traces the dancer Choi Seung-hee (1911–1969) through her fragmented archive, focusing on the period after 1941 and her aim to create a modern East Asian dance, using found footage and images from her archives. The presentation in Venice also included a large sculptural structure and a small garden installed behind the pavilion.

> — Hwayeon Nam, ['Hwayeon Nam on alternative archives and the modern dance of Seunghee Choi', Artforum (2020)](https://www.artforum.com/columns/hwayeon-nam-on-alternative-archives-and-the-modern-dance-of-seunghee-choi-247427/)

> I looked at the period following Choi’s return to Japan after her 1937–40 world tour, when she declared her intention to establish the art of East Asian dance by incorporating traditional Korean, Japanese, and Chinese dance and theater.
>
> — Hwayeon Nam, ['Hwayeon Nam on alternative archives and the modern dance of Seunghee Choi', Artforum (2020)](https://www.artforum.com/columns/hwayeon-nam-on-alternative-archives-and-the-modern-dance-of-seunghee-choi-247427/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Over the course of my making Dancer from the Peninsula, Choi’s presence appeared to me slowly. She felt both familiar and distanced at the same time. Near the end of the working process, I observed that she had become split into both the subject and object of research.
>
> — Hwayeon Nam, ['Hwayeon Nam on alternative archives and the modern dance of Seunghee Choi', Artforum (2020)](https://www.artforum.com/columns/hwayeon-nam-on-alternative-archives-and-the-modern-dance-of-seunghee-choi-247427/)

<sub>says</sub>

> These encounters and collisions enabled me to surface the gaps, erasures, and contradictions in Choi’s life and work. These are what interest me the most.
>
> — Hwayeon Nam, ['Hwayeon Nam on alternative archives and the modern dance of Seunghee Choi', Artforum (2020)](https://www.artforum.com/columns/hwayeon-nam-on-alternative-archives-and-the-modern-dance-of-seunghee-choi-247427/)
