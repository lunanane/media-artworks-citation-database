# I DIDNT REALISE YOU THOUGHT LIKE THAT

**Danielle Brathwaite-Shirley**, 2026 · Online / net art / Screen / monitor

An online game for web and mobile, released by Serpentine on 21 May 2026, in which players decide whether to open a door to fictional characters, conspiracy agents or 'Influencers' who ask to enter on the basis of their stated beliefs. The game simulates a real-time feed, and players meet a cast of characters and reach different endings depending on their choices. It is described as the next chapter of THE DELUSION, the multiplayer game exhibition at Serpentine North (September 2025 to January 2026), where a gallery version with a transparent door and a screen was the first work visitors encountered.

> I DIDNT REALISE YOU THOUGHT LIKE THAT is a reflection of our world, allowing players to reflect on their political opinions and beliefs to understand how their judgements shape and constrict the lives of others.

> — Danielle Brathwaite-Shirley, [Serpentine press release, 21 May 2026 (PDF)](https://d37zoqglehb9o7.cloudfront.net/uploads/2026/05/Danielle-Brathwaite-Shirley_online-game-release-FINAL-3.pdf)

> The choices that you make in \[“The Delusion”\] are a reflection of you rather than an entering or a teaching of someone else. Because I find it a lot more interesting if the audience comes out with an “I” statement, for example, “I made the wrong decision” or “I hated this” rather than, “I didn’t know this about X, Y, Z” \[...\]. Especially in a world where we are evermore polarized and evermore not having spaces to think about what you want and what you think about a particular subject.
>
> — Danielle Brathwaite-Shirley, [Louis Jebb, "The Interview \| Danielle Brathwaite-Shirley", Right Click Save, 22 October 2025 (on THE DELUSION)](https://www.rightclicksave.com/article/the-interview-danielle-brathwaite-shirley-the-delusion-serpentine-galleries-gaming)
