# Pandemonium

**Michelle Lopez**, 2025 · Projection / VR / AR / XR / Robotics / kinetics

A multimedia project that premiered in October 2025 at Moore College of Art & Design and the Fels Planetarium at The Franklin Institute, Philadelphia. It combines mechatronics, animation and sound with a 360-degree film shown with 8K projection in the planetarium dome, and the opening performance used a robot. The film shows a tornado of debris, including newspaper clippings swirling out of a tornado machine built by the artist; at the 2026 Whitney Biennial it was shown on a 20-ft circular screen overhead (approx. 20 min.).
