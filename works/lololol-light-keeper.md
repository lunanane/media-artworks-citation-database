# Light Keeper

**lololol**, 2025 · Sound / Light / Sensor / interaction

A GPS-enabled sound walk and light installation commissioned for Singapore Biennale 2025 at Fort Canning Park. Visitors use a GPS-enabled app that guides them to five sites in the park, where audio vignettes based on the artists' encounters with lighthouses, lighthouse keepers and crypto-linguists play; at the Fort Canning Lighthouse, a programmed, looped light cycle runs after dusk. A companion three-channel video, Light Keeper: Signal (2025, 7 min 58 sec), is shown at Fort Canning Centre.
