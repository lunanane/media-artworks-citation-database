# Build Up the House II

**Brendan Fernandes**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / Performance / body

Build Up the House II is an animated video shown on more than 92 electronic billboards in Times Square nightly from 11:57pm to midnight throughout March 2025 as part of Midnight Moment, presented with ART on THE MART. Silhouettes of dancers leap, spin and vogue across colored patterns that reference the artist's East African heritage and textiles, as an homage to house music and its queer and POC roots. It follows Build Up the House, a 2024 projection on the facade of THE MART in Chicago marking the 40th anniversary of house music; a public DJ dance party accompanied the Times Square presentation on 14 March 2025.

<sub>says</sub>

> Having the opportunity to take over and intervene within this space has allowed me to create a transformative public experience—one where people gather and respond to the flashes, actions and gestures of my animation. My goal is to create a space of celebration and joy, where movement and communal gathering become acts of unity and resilience. This work is a homage to my cultural identity and to queer and POC communities, affirming that through dance and shared space we can foster connection and collective presence.
>
> — Brendan Fernandes, [Build Up the House II project page, brendanfernandes.ca (Times Square Arts & ART on THE MART announcement)](https://brendanfernandes.ca/build-up-the-house-ii)
