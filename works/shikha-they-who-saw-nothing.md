# They Who Saw Nothing but Beauty

**Hussein Shikha**, 2025 · Material / sculpture

They Who Saw Nothing but Beauty is a series of seven jacquard-woven wool tapestries made during the artist's residency at the Jan van Eyck Academie and woven at the TextielLab of the TextielMuseum Tilburg. The compositions are drawn as pixel art in the software Aseprite and show scenes connected to the Battle of Karbala, including a two-faced figure based on Sayyida Zaynab, in dense black-and-white patterns drawn from southern Iraqi carpet-making and Shia iconography. The title refers to Zaynab's reply 'I saw nothing but beauty'; the work received an Honorary Mention in Interactive Art + at Prix Ars Electronica 2026.

## In their words

> This project is part of my ongoing research into the visual culture of southern Mesopotamia, focusing on carpet-making traditions of southern Iraq alongside (Shia) Islamic iconography. The southern Iraqi carpet-making craft is hardly documented.
>
> — Hussein Shikha, [Prix Ars Electronica 2026 archive, catalogue text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/317102/)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> The work also emerges from the experience of forced displacement—a condition shared, in different but connected ways, with the communities of southern Iraq, who have endured a long history of political oppression, environmental devastation, and displacement, conditions that have disrupted the transmission and continuity of their material and visual culture. Rather than attempting to reconstruct a lost tradition, the project seeks to keep these visual languages and symbologies present by carrying them forward and recontextualizing their legacy through contemporary artistic transformation.
>
> — Hussein Shikha, [Prix Ars Electronica 2026 archive, catalogue text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/317102/)
