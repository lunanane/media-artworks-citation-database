# Sunshine Monument

**Peter Burr**, 2023 · Online / net art / AI / generative / Screen / monitor

A Whitney artport commission of seven animated black-and-white architectures, one for each weekday, that translate the layout of blocks of text and images on whitney.org into an abstract building populated by wandering figures, centred on a round sun icon and interspersed with text generated by the artist using AI. It first played on the museum website at sunrise and sunset in New York (Sunrise/Sunset series, from July 2023) and from September 2024 ran hourly, opening the On the Hour series.
