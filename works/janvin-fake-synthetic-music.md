# fake synthetic music

**Stine Janvin**, 2019 · Sound / Light / Performance / body

Fake Synthetic Music is a live performance for voice, echo, lights and spatial distribution, and a double LP released on PAN in 2018. Janvin sings sustained and sequenced tones through an amplified microphone and a digital echo, imitating synthetic sounds, while programmed lights accompany the performance. It received an Honorary Mention in Digital Musics & Sound Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2019.

## In their words

> The project is about de-glorifying the voice as a supposedly fragile instrument and playing with the perception of the difference between fake and real.
>
> — Stine Janvin, [The Attic, 'AUDIO: Stine Janvin Motland live in Riga, Latvia' (5 August 2016), statement given to The Attic](https://theatticmag.com/news/1810/audio:-stine-janvin-motland-live-in-riga,-latvia.html)

> — Stine Janvin, [The Attic, 'AUDIO: Stine Janvin Motland live in Riga, Latvia' (5 August 2016), statement given to The Attic](https://theatticmag.com/news/1810/audio:-stine-janvin-motland-live-in-riga,-latvia.html)

<sub>says</sub>

> Initially trying to imitate synthetic sounds with my voice, I found that the combined voice and echo became its own hybrid instrument, making a mono signal from an amplified voice, sound like a detailed electronic production, just from the physiological features of the listener.
