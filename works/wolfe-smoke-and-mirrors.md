# Smoke and Mirrors

**Beatie Wolfe**, 2024 · Screen / monitor / Network / data / Sound

A 4m30s data animation based on NASA's Blue Marble photograph: a counter shows atmospheric methane in parts per billion from 1970 to the present while the rotating globe fills with pink smoke, and advertising slogans from the oil industry (e.g. "Out to clean the air", "Unsettled Science", "Doomsday is cancelled") appear across the screen at their dates. It is set to the song "Oh My Heart" and was produced with the studio Parliament, using NOAA, NASA and EEA data. It received the Prix Ars Electronica Golden Nica (New Animation Art) in 2024 and has been shown as a video and in exhibitions.

## In their words

> Smoke and Mirrors is about visualising not just the methane data (smoke) in a way people can really absorb but also the disinformation campaign (mirrors) which has caused the data to be denied, doubted and delayed through the decades.
>
> — Beatie Wolfe, [Statement of Intent, Smoke and Mirrors (beatiewolfe.com)](https://beatiewolfe.com/smoke-and-mirrors)

<sub>says</sub>

> Realising that a crucial piece of the climate puzzle (how we got to this critical point) has been the fossil fuel industry’s response to the emerging environmental awareness of the 1970s, and that methane emissions (30 times more potent than carbon for trapping heat) are increasingly linked with that industry, I wanted to illuminate this key parallel timeline.
>
> — Beatie Wolfe, [Statement of Intent, Smoke and Mirrors (beatiewolfe.com)](https://beatiewolfe.com/smoke-and-mirrors)

<sub>says</sub>

> The ‘smoke and mirrors’ tactics of the big oil industry (and many political bodies and corporations) are so hard to grasp, which leaves a lot of people feeling disempowered, so I wanted this project to help make this slippery reality visible and tangible.
>
> — Beatie Wolfe, [Hannah Bachl, "From Blue Marble to Bioplastic Record", Ars Electronica Blog, 17 June 2024](https://ars.electronica.art/aeblog/?p=77214)
