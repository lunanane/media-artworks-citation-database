# Oikeiōsis

**Pavlina Vagioni**, 2026 · Sound / Material / sculpture / Sensor / interaction

Oikeiōsis is a two-room installation by Pavlina Vagioni at Spazio Tana, Venice, presented by the Hellenic Diaspora Foundation during the 61st Biennale Arte (6 May - 25 October 2026). In the first room, Neikos, a polygonal plexiglass cube reflects visitors in fragmented form; in the second, Philotes, six rock-salt seats surround a hexagonal mirrored structure, and when visitors sit, sound and light respond, building a layered vocal soundscape that includes the artist's soprano voice. The salt seats change shape over the exhibition through visitors' touch.

## In their words

> The world is loud with reasons to turn away from one another. I wanted to make quiet work. Not a protest, not a commentary, but a space where strangers can sit together and remember that this, too, is real.
>
> — Pavlina Vagioni, [Quoted in 'Pavlina Vagioni Oikeiōsis: A Greek Artist Asks Venice to Remember How to Belong', Artlyst, 2026](https://artlyst.com/?p=196876)

<sub>says</sub>

> In a Biennale composed in the minor keys, this is the frequency I wanted to tune to: not a sound, but what the sound reveals.
>
> — unattributed (first person, in exhibition announcement), [Exhibition announcement, Hellenic Diaspora Foundation, e-flux](https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/6787233/pavlina-vagionioikei-sis-kinship)

>
> — Pavlina Vagioni, [Interview, Greek News Agenda](https://www.greeknewsagenda.gr/?p=23095)
