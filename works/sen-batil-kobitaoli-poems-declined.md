# Batil-Kobitaoli (Poems Declined)

**Mithu Sen**, 2014 · Text / print / Projection

Batil-Kobitaoli (Poems Declined) is a multi-media installation commissioned for the second Dhaka Art Summit (2014), built from unpublished and rejected poems that the artist collected from aspiring poets and publishers in Dhaka. It includes a unique, unedited book of 1,800 declined poems, painted works made from the correction marks on the manuscripts, and doodled self-corrections from the manuscripts projected onto the gallery ceiling as shadow drawings. The work was shown again in Sen's survey mOTHERTONGUE at the Australian Centre for Contemporary Art, Melbourne, in 2023.
