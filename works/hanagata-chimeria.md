# CHIMERIA

**Shin Hanagata**, 2026 · Performance / body / VR / AR / XR / Camera / computer vision

CHIMERIA is a project, performance and installation in which head-mounted displays move a participant's vision to other parts of the body, such as the toes, so that the feet act as the head. Volunteers recruited through a publicly circulated phone number lived together in a Tokyo residence, adapting to an environment built for upright bodies with tube-fed liquid food, wire-driven prosthetics and a toilet used kneeling. Outsiders could visit the space, but only by imitating the environment and becoming part of it.
