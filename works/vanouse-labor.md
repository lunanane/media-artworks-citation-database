# Labor

**Paul Vanouse**, 2019 · Material / sculpture

Labor is a self-regulating installation in which three glass bioreactors, each incubating one species of human skin bacteria (Staphylococcus epidermidis, Corynebacterium xerosis, Propionibacterium avidum), produce the smell of sweating, stressed bodies without any people involved. The scents combine in a central chamber holding a white T-shirt, and a Raspberry Pi-based system of stirrers, sensors, pumps, heaters and valves runs the process; the installation also includes sweat-stain-transfer prints.
