# Transits

**Ursula Damm**, 2012 · Camera / computer vision / AI / generative

Transits is a generative video work, about one hour long, made from 24 hours of footage of Aeschenplatz, Basel's busiest traffic junction, where cars, trams and pedestrians pass. Software written by Martin Schneider processes the footage with an artificial neural network in which every pixel corresponds to one neuron, sorting movement patterns and colour schemes into overlapping image layers in which still elements of the square and moving passers-by appear; the video runs from night through dawn and noon to dusk. It was produced for the exhibition Sensing Place at HeK (House of Electronic Arts Basel) in 2012 and is in the HeK collection.

## In their words

> “Transits” captures and alienates the recorded stream of motion with an intelligent algorithm that was developed by the artist and her team: It evaluates the audiovisual data and categorizes the patterns of movement and the color schemes.
>
> — Ursula Damm, [Transits \[2012\] – work page, ursuladamm.de (artist's website; written in the third person)](https://ursuladamm.de/transits-2012)
