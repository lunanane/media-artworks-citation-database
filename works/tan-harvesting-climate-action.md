# Harvesting Climate Action

**Ling Tan**, 2025 · Material / sculpture / Network / data / Online / net art

Harvesting Climate Action was a six-week installation in Hackney Service Centre, London (autumn 2025), part of Ling Tan's Low Carbon Chinatown project, made with Hackney Council's Climate Team. Its centre was 100 recyclable cardboard stools, each printed on one side with a 'Climate Action' (from individual to borough-wide) and on the other with the carbon footprint of a meal ingredient, alongside data maps, food-making videos, audio stories and living produce. The installation then became a 'Low Carbon Banquet for a Hundred', where 100 members of the public each took a stool, sat with strangers over low carbon East and Southeast Asian dishes, debated and voted on the actions, and took their stool home; the 100 Climate Actions also exist as an online toolkit. It received an Honorary Mention (Digital Humanity) at Prix Ars Electronica 2026.
