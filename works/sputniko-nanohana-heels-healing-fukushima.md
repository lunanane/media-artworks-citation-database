# Nanohana Heels - Healing Fukushima

**Sputniko!**, 2013 · Material / sculpture / Robotics / kinetics

A pair of mechanical high-heeled shoes that plant rapeseed (nanohana) into the soil as the wearer walks, made by Sputniko! in collaboration with shoe designer Masaya Kushino. The project refers to experiments in which rapeseed absorbed caesium and strontium from contaminated soil after Chernobyl and to rapeseed-planting initiatives in Fukushima after the 2011 nuclear accident. It received an Honorary Mention in Hybrid Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2013.
