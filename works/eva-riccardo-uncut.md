# Riccardo Uncut

**Eva and Franco Mattes**, 2018 · Online / net art / Screen / monitor

Riccardo Uncut is an online work commissioned by the Whitney Museum's artport: the artists posted a call on social media offering to buy someone's phone for $1,000 and turned its photo and video contents into a customized, chronological slideshow. Viewers see roughly three thousand images taken between 2004 and 2017 by the chosen applicant, Riccardo — travels, friends and family, partners, home and office life, food and art. The accompanying song, from the soundtrack of Fassbinder's film Querelle (1982), also appears in one of Riccardo's videos.

> In the 90s we were obsessed with visibility, with traffic logs, with reaching an ever wider audience, but as time went by, and the advent of internet 2.0, we became more and more interested in the opposite: invisibility and disappearance, anonymous, deleting data, frying hard drives in the microwave…
>
> — Eva & Franco Mattes, [Generazione Critica, interview 'Eva & Franco Mattes' (2022)](https://www.generazionecritica.it/en/interviews/eva-franco-mattes/)
