# Liquid Crystal

**Rick Silva**, 2023 · Online / net art / Screen / monitor

A Whitney artport commission of seven short videos, one for each day of the week, shown on whitney.org at sunrise and sunset in New York (Apr–Jul 2023). Each video opens on a natural surface (leaves and moss, sand, gravel, snow, broken ice) that the artist's hands push aside to reveal a screen of synthesized video patterns.
