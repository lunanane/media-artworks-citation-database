# Black Friday

**Sophia Al-Maria**, 2016 · Projection / Screen / monitor / Sound

Black Friday is a 16:36-minute digital video with sound, projected vertically, shot largely in an unopened shopping mall in Doha; an unseen narrator speaks over images of marble halls, shuttered shops, blurred faces and a figure lying on the mall floor. At the Whitney Museum (26 July–31 October 2016) the projection hung above The Litany, a heap of old electronic screens playing short glitchy loops, in a free-access ground-floor gallery.

## In their words

> a weirdly neutral shared zone between cultures that are otherwise engaged in a sort of war of information and image,
>
> — Sophia Al-Maria, [Sophia Al-Maria: Black Friday, exhibition text, Whitney Museum of American Art, 2016](https://whitney.org/exhibitions/sophia-al-maria)
