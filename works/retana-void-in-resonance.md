# Void in Resonance

**Jerónimo Reyes-Retana**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / Sound

Void in Resonance is a multi-channel film installation about El Campo Pesquero de Playa Bagdad, a community at the eastern end of the Mexico–U.S. border, four kilometres from SpaceX's spaceport in Boca Chica, Texas. It shows a SpaceX launch through the perspectives of people working in the oyster field, juxtaposing Playa Bagdad's vernacular architecture with elements evoking SpaceX designs in deliberately underexposed images. It was produced through the 2025 CIFO x Ars Electronica Award and premiered at Ars Electronica Festival 2025 (POSTCITY, Linz).
