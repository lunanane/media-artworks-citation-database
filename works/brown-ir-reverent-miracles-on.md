# \[ir\]reverent: Miracles on Demand

**Adam Brown**, 2019 · Material / sculpture

\[ir\]reverent: Miracles on Demand is an installation built around a bio-incubator modelled on a Roman Catholic monstrance. A Eucharist host is placed in its oculus and inoculated with micro-droplets of the bacterium Serratia marcescens, which over about two days grows a blood-like red fluid on the wafer, reproducing the 'bleeding host' miracles recorded since antiquity.

## In their words

> For more than a decade, I have been interested in historical relationships between humans and non-human agents and how what we know or think we know shapes how we understand ourselves as humans
>

> Life is far stranger, more complex and more beautiful than any contrived fiction that humans create. >
> — Adam Brown, [MSU College of Arts & Letters news (2020)](https://cal.msu.edu/news/professor-wins-grand-prize-at-japan-media-arts-festival/)
