# Antraal

**Sahej Rahal**, 2019 · AI / generative / Screen / monitor / Projection

Antraal is an artificial intelligence program by Sahej Rahal presented on cathode-ray television monitors and as a video projection, shown with his sculptures Other limbs (2019) in ACCA's exhibition Feedback Loops (Melbourne, 7 December 2019 – 22 March 2020). What the visitor sees is not edited video but the program's environments, in which characters devised by the artist move according to algorithmic instructions. ACCA's label gives the title as roughly meaning 'interstice', a space between, in Sanskrit.

> My practice is this one story that's unfolding in an infinite kind of iteration. With each work, each object, image, performance becomes one aspect that's added to the story—one more motif or tone to that unfolding.
>
> — Sahej Rahal, [Jamin Warren, 'Sahej Rahal's AI Instruments and Cooperative Creatures', Killscreen](https://www.killscreen.com/sahej-rahals-ai-instruments-and-cooperative-creatures/)
