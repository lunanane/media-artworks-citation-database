# HANDMADE

**Leslie Thornton**, 2023 · Screen / monitor

HANDMADE is a video diptych of about 11 minutes, originally conceived for two 4K monitors and presented at MoMA from November 2023 in the ground-floor Gund Lobby on a large media wall. One part shows digitally manipulated footage of a gale-force storm on Lake Huron, filmed by Thornton with a handheld camera while lying down; the other shows a physicist at Caltech making a handwoven sensing device for a research project on antimatter. These are interspersed with film recordings of fluid-dynamics studies that Thornton found during her 2019 residency in the Caltech-Huntington Program in Visual Culture.

<sub>says · about their practice</sub>

> There’s a parallel between art and science, a shared sense of intuition engaged in a process where the result is unknown. But science has a goal of knowledge, a prediction toward an outcome. In my definition of art, or in my kind of practice, there’s no prediction or goal beyond each work.
>
> — Leslie Thornton, [Artforum, 'Leslie Thornton on saturation and solitude in her moving-image works' (2020)](https://www.artforum.com/columns/leslie-thornton-on-saturation-and-solitude-in-her-moving-image-works-247246/)
