# Tinieblas

**Edgardo Aragón**, 2011 · Projection / Sound / Performance / body

Tinieblas (Darkness) is a video installation recording an action in which 13 musicians, placed at 13 spots marking the territorial limits of a town in southern Mexico that is in constant conflict over its borders, play a funeral procession tune known as Tinieblas. It belongs to the artist's investigation of the boundaries of his birthplace, Ocotlán de Morelos, Oaxaca, where boundary markers (mojones) fix the limits between municipalities. It was shown at MUAC, Mexico City, in 2012 in the Sexta Sur programme, co-produced with Museo Amparo, Puebla.
