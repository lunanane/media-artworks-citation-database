# Parasitic / Symbiotic

**Ann-Katrin Krenz**, 2016 · Robotics / kinetics / Material / sculpture / Text / print

A technical device mounted on a living tree in a forest contains a milling machine that moves along the trunk and carves an encoded version of Joseph von Eichendorff's Romantic poem "Abschied" into the bark. The carving remains on the tree, which continues to grow around it. The work was Krenz's master thesis at the University of the Arts Berlin and received an Award of Distinction in Interactive Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2016.
