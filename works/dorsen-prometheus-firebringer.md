# Prometheus Firebringer

**Annie Dorsen**, 2024 · Performance / body / AI / generative

Prometheus Firebringer is a lecture-performance of about an hour on generative AI, technology and power. On one side of the stage, a chorus of AI-generated Greek masks with AI-generated voices performs a new version each night of the lost final play of Aeschylus' Prometheia trilogy, generated by a GPT language model; on the other side, Dorsen delivers a talk composed entirely of passages quoted from other texts. It premiered at Bryn Mawr College in January 2023 and was then presented at The Chocolate Factory, Theatre for a New Audience and REDCAT.
