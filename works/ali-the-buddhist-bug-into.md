# The Buddhist Bug, Into the Night

**Anida Yoeu Ali**, 2015 · Projection / Performance / body

The Buddhist Bug, Into the Night is a two-channel video projection (9:48 minutes, colour, sound, looped) commissioned for the 8th Asia Pacific Triennial of Contemporary Art (APT8, QAGOMA, Brisbane, 2015-16), with video by Masahiro Sugano (Studio Revolt). It shows the Buddhist Bug - Ali in a long saffron, caterpillar-like costume whose hood resembles a hijab - in its first night-time appearance, moving through Phnom Penh among motorbikes, food stalls and nightclubs, with frames sped up and slowed down. It belongs to the ongoing Buddhist Bug series of performances, photographs and videos that Ali began in 2012 after returning to Cambodia.

## In their words

> What is she, who is she, why is she in this setting, and does she belong?” Ms. Yoeu Ali asked in a recent interview. “These are the questions that I’m trying to get the viewers to ask themselves.
>
> — Anida Yoeu Ali, [Aria Danaparamita, "A Curious Critter", The Cambodia Daily, 20 Dec 2014](https://english.cambodiadaily.com/?p=74562)

> I’m exploring a diasporic being, a being that for me has to be able to stretch as if she could be a bridge or a tunnel, connecting one place to another
>
> — Anida Yoeu Ali, [Aria Danaparamita, "A Curious Critter", The Cambodia Daily, 20 Dec 2014](https://english.cambodiadaily.com/?p=74562)

> It really speaks about, for me, the diasporic dilemma of the person such as myself who is in that state of flux, constantly insider or outsider, going in between those two identities, and at the same time not having one place to call home or one community of affinity where you belong
>
> — Anida Yoeu Ali, [Aria Danaparamita, "A Curious Critter", The Cambodia Daily, 20 Dec 2014](https://english.cambodiadaily.com/?p=74562)

> I was trying to solve—in both this form and the execution of the complete series—this idea of connecting very distant points
>
> — Anida Yoeu Ali, [Deborah Germaine Augustin, "What Are You?: On Mixed-Race Identity and The Buddhist Bug", Catapult, 31 Mar 2020](https://magazine.catapult.co/people/stories/deborah-augustin-buddhist-bug-anida-yoeu-ali-malaysia-mixed-race)
