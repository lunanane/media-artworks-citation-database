# Us Dead Talk Love

**Ed Atkins**, 2012 · Screen / monitor / Sound

Us Dead Talk Love is a 37-minute two-channel video with surround sound, commissioned by and first shown at Chisenhale Gallery, London (21 September - 11 November 2012), alongside a suite of painted and collaged panels. A computer-generated bald head mapped with the artist's own features speaks a monologue that starts from finding an eyelash under the foreskin; the artist described the premise as two cadavers in dialogue about their lives, decay and love, interrupted by a chorus.

## In their words

> The work is entitled Us Dead Talk Love and begins with the premise of two cadavers in dialogue. They talk about their status ontologically – their lives priorly, their decay, their spirit – all in relation to love and particularly its expression in physical intimacy.
>
> — Ed Atkins, ['Ed Atkins: In Conversation With', Aesthetica, 2012 (Wayback Machine copy)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130508161957/http://www.aestheticamagazine.com/ed-atkins)

> Love is the biggest shift – at least in its conspicuousness – from previous work. Love as the original locus of intimacy and emotional excess prior to death’s terrific parody.
