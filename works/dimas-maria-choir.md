# Maria CHOIR

**Maria Arnal Dimas**, 2024 · Sound / AI / generative / Sensor / interaction

An interactive installation in which a synthetic reproduction of singer Maria Arnal's voice, produced by models trained on her voice, harmonises in real time with what a visitor sings into a microphone, forming a human-digital choir; an AI-animated avatar responds to the vocal input. Visitors can also record their voice to contribute to a collective synthetic voice model. It was shown in the exhibition AI: Artificial Intelligence at CCCB, Barcelona (18 Oct 2023 – 17 Mar 2024), developed with the Barcelona Supercomputing Center and axolot.cat, and received an Honorary Mention at the S+T+ARTS Prize 2024.

## In their words

> During this phase of the project, I have been learning a great deal about composing with synthetic voices and the various software developed to utilize them. Additionally, as a singer, the desire to dissociate body and voice through technology in this project has offered me new perspectives, both physically and compositionally.
>
> — Maria Arnal Dimas, ["Maria CHOIR: reflection and thoughts on the project by Maria Arnal Dimas", MEET THE ARTISTS: Maria Arnal Dimas, S+T+ARTS AIR (starts.eu)](https://starts.eu/air-meet-the-artists-maria-arnal-dimas/)
