# The Refusal of Time

**William Kentridge**, 2012 · Projection / Sound / Robotics / kinetics

The Refusal of Time is a half-hour, five-channel video installation made for dOCUMENTA (13) in Kassel in 2012, developed from conversations between Kentridge and historian of science Peter Galison about the history of the control of time. Films are projected on three walls around a large wooden breathing machine ('the elephant'), with music and voices played through four megaphones; imagery includes metronomes, maps, torn-paper figures and a shadow procession. It was made with filmmaker Catherine Meyburgh, composer Philip Miller and dancer Dada Masilo.
