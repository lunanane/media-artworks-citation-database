# REPETAE

**Sasha Stiles**, 2024 · AI / generative / Text / print / Sound

A series of language artworks combining AI-generated text, code-based visuals sketched in p5.js, and digitally looped and layered spoken word, presented as standalone works and longer interconnected cycles built around repetition. Parts include a browser-based piece that generates a new line of poetry every few minutes from the mantra WORDS CAN COMMUNICATE BEYOND WORDS. It received a Prix Ars Electronica 2024 Award of Distinction (AI in ART) and was shown as REPETAE: Again, Again at the Lentos Museum, Linz.

## In their words

> It has to do with challenging the notion that creativity is solely about originality or "first-ness," and emphasizes how innovation comes from revisiting, reimagining and reinventing the familiar.
>
> — Sasha Stiles, [Flaunt — Sasha Stiles' 'REPETAE' \| The Seduction and Synthesis of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.flaunt.com/post/sasha-stiles-repetae-prix-ars-electronica)

> So much dialogue around creative AI has to do with appropriation, copying, imitation, and how such "repetition" can never be authentic. With REPETAE, I want to probe how repeating patterns, words and ideas are at the heart of both human and machine creativity—how "again-ness" can yield fresh perspectives, lead to innovation and epiphany.
>
> — Sasha Stiles, [Flaunt — Sasha Stiles' 'REPETAE' \| The Seduction and Synthesis of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.flaunt.com/post/sasha-stiles-repetae-prix-ars-electronica)

>
> — Sasha Stiles, [Flaunt — Sasha Stiles' 'REPETAE' \| The Seduction and Synthesis of Artificial Intelligence](https://www.flaunt.com/post/sasha-stiles-repetae-prix-ars-electronica)
