# Fashion Show for Elders

**Malik Afegbua**, 2023 · AI / generative

Fashion Show for Elders is a series of AI-generated images by Nigerian filmmaker Malik Afegbua showing elderly African models on a runway in outfits mixing traditional Nigerian dress with futuristic designs. The show never took place: the models and clothes were produced with several AI image generators, and the images were shared online, where many viewers took them for a real event.
