# Ongoing, Individual Adaptability or How to Quiet Quit

**Devin Kenny**, 2022 · AI / generative / Online / net art

A two-part web work commissioned for the Whitney Museum's artport, made with a generative adversarial network (GAN) trained on thousands of installation photographs of Whitney exhibitions. The first part, Ongoing, Individual Adaptability, shows twelve GAN images collaged with manipulated excerpts of artist correspondence and ephemera from the Whitney's library; the second, How to Quiet Quit, is three videos of the GAN producing images, with soundtracks of TED-talk excerpts by Nick Bostrom, Kai-Fu Lee and Janelle Shane interrupted by the artist's comments and questions. Visitors change the sequence of images and videos by refreshing the page; the title quotes Octavia E. Butler's Parable of the Sower.
