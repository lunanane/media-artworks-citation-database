# La laguna parla

**Steye Hallema**, 2026 · Performance / body / Sound / Sensor / interaction

La laguna parla is a 35-minute collective, smartphone-guided ritual about the Venice lagoon, water and collective power, made for the tenth anniversary of Venice Immersive (2026), in which hundreds of participants take part with their own phones. It is an adaptation of The Waterbodies Orchestra, an earlier work by The Confluence of European Water Bodies and The Smartphone Orchestra made to give a voice to European water bodies. In Smartphone Orchestra experiences, participants receive instructions, prompts, questions and games on their phones that lead them to interact with the other people in the room.

>
> — Steye Hallema, [Director's statement, La laguna parla, Venice Immersive 2026, La Biennale di Venezia](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2026/venice-immersive/la-laguna-parla)
