# The Teaching Tree

**Muhannad Shono**, 2022 · Material / sculpture / Robotics / kinetics

The Teaching Tree is a 40-metre-long sculptural installation of palm fronds painted black on a metal structure, animated internally by pneumatics, made for the National Pavilion of Saudi Arabia at the 59th Venice Biennale (Arsenale, Sale d'Armi, 23 April–27 November 2022), curated by Reem Fadda. The form starts as a thin point and widens along the length of the pavilion towards the entrance; visitors walk beside it as it rises and falls as if breathing, with the scent of dried palm leaves in the room.

## In their words

> My work embodies the irrepressible spirit of creative expression: the power of the imagination that grows despite what may attempt to limit it but instead makes it more resilient. This is a resilience that is taught by nature, in its continuous cycles of death and re-growth, like trees nourished by the ashes of wildfires.
>
> — Muhannad Shono, [Muhannad Shono: The Teaching Tree, Saudi Arabia Pavilion announcement, e-flux, 27 April 2022](https://www.e-flux.com/announcements/451107/muhannad-shono-the-teaching-tree)
