# Café Kuba

**David Shongo**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / Sound

A cinematic installation (film, about 27 minutes, in French and Lingala, with 3D sound) built around a mobile street coffee cart in Kinshasa that Shongo converted into a recording device. Filmed after the M23 capture of Goma and Bukavu in early 2025, it follows the cart and a coffee vendor through the city's streets and conversations, with fictional elements such as masked figures holding electronic waste and licence plates; it won the State of the ART(ist) prize at Ars Electronica 2025.
