# AmaHubo

**Buhlebezwe Siwani**, 2018 · Projection / Sound

AmaHubo is a 13-minute digital 4K video shown as a three-panel projection with sound, filmed on a wine farm in Tulbagh, Western Cape, that still has an old slave bell. It features black women on the farm; in its 2023–24 showing in 'Seers Seekers Soothsayers' at Zeitz MOCAA the artist is heard narrating a psalm while the gallery walls carry raised outlines of Cape landmarks such as Table Mountain.

## In their words

> I thought about how the land was taken and how Christianity demonized African spiritualisms. And then how people subsists in those spaces, how they eat and how the land has been co-opted.
>
> — Buhlebezwe Siwani, [Silent and Complex Histories: In conversation with Buhlebezwe Siwani, interview by Sikhumbuzo Makandula, ArtThrob, 2018](https://artthrob.co.za/2018/11/05/silent-and-complex-histories-in-conversation-with-buhlebezwe-siwani/)

> I went to the winelands because of what the winelands does to people, it's really complex what happens there: issues are layered.
>
> — Buhlebezwe Siwani, [Silent and Complex Histories: In conversation with Buhlebezwe Siwani, interview by Sikhumbuzo Makandula, ArtThrob, 2018](https://artthrob.co.za/2018/11/05/silent-and-complex-histories-in-conversation-with-buhlebezwe-siwani/)

> They are there representing faces of people who are always erased, particularly in the Western Cape black women are erased from the narratives as if they never existed before, as if amaXhosa, baSotho, baTswana, Khoi-San they are not interlinked.
>
> — Buhlebezwe Siwani, [Silent and Complex Histories: In conversation with Buhlebezwe Siwani, interview by Sikhumbuzo Makandula, ArtThrob, 2018](https://artthrob.co.za/2018/11/05/silent-and-complex-histories-in-conversation-with-buhlebezwe-siwani/)
