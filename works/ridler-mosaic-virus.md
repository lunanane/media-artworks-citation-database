# Mosaic Virus

**Anna Ridler**, 2018 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative / Network / data

Mosaic Virus is a GAN-generated video of tulips whose appearance changes with the price of bitcoin: the generated petals show more stripes as the price rises and a single colour as it falls. The model was trained on a dataset of about ten thousand tulip photographs the artist took and categorised by hand. The 2018 version is a single-screen work showing a grid of tulips (commissioned by Impakt within EMAP/EMARE); a 2019 version is a three-screen installation.

## In their words

> Drawing together ideas around capitalism, value, and the tangible and intangible nature of speculation and collapse, my work brings together these two very different, yet surprisingly similar moments in history. This is accentuated by the use of AI as a material, which is going through its own cycle of hype and speculation.
>
> — Anna Ridler (Prix Ars Electronica catalogue text), [Mosaic Virus, Prix Ars Electronica 2019 archive, catalog text](https://archive.aec.at/prix/244958/)
