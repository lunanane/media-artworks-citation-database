# Toposcan / Baden-Württemberg

**Shiro Takatani (Dumb Type)**, 2016 · Screen / monitor / Camera / computer vision

A computer-based video installation on eight horizontally aligned 16:9 flat screens driven by eight Mac mini computers, with two loudspeakers. A one-monitor-wide "window" of 360-degree panning landscape footage travels slowly left to right and back across the row; as it advances, the single-pixel edge at its front is stretched into horizontal colour bands, and on the way back the trailing edge leaves pixel-wide vertical freeze-frame stripes that build up a still panorama. It is a version of Takatani's Toposcan series (Ireland 2013, Morocco 2014, Tokyo 2020), developed with ZKM \| Karlsruhe for the exhibition "New Sensorium. Exiting from Failures of Modernization" (GLOBALE, 2016) and held in the ZKM collection.
