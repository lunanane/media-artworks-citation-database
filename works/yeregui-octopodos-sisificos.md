# Octópodos Sisíficos

**Mariela Yeregui & Miguel Grassi**, 2010 · Robotics / kinetics / Screen / monitor

Octópodos Sisíficos (Sisyphean Octopods) is an installation of six mobile robots developed by the Artes Electrónicas group at the Universidad Nacional de Tres de Febrero, Argentina, directed by Mariela Yeregui, with robotic design by Miguel Grassi. Each robot carries an LCD screen showing endoscopic video images that resemble internal body organs and moves on an erratic course; the robots have no self-positioning or navigation mechanism and each is attached to the wall by a flexible corrugated metal tube.

## In their words

> The robots move erratically, without any purpose but to reveal their own technological animality; they display a corporeal behavior that is both artificial and organic, material and phenomenological, exposing their own absurd existence as “living” artificial objects.
>
> — Mariela Yeregui, Miguel Grassi (artist statement), [Artist Statement, ACM SIGGRAPH History Archives entry for Octópodos Sisíficos (SIGGRAPH 2017: Unsettled Artifacts)](https://history.siggraph.org/?p=18504)
