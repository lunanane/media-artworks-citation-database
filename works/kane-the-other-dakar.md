# The Other Dakar

**Selly Raby Kane**, 2017 · VR / AR / XR / Performance / body

The Other Dakar is a 360° virtual reality film directed by the Dakar-based designer and artist Selly Raby Kane and produced by Electric South. A young girl receives a message and is guided through a series of encounters with mythological beings in a hidden 'other' Dakar, filmed in locations in the city. It premiered at the Berlinale in 2017 and was shown at the Tribeca Film Festival the same year.

## In their words

> The Other Dakar was made with these two ingredients: accident and pro-action with the underlying aim to contribute to the rebrand of an understated city. For herself, Africa and the world.
>
> — Selly Raby Kane, [Radiance VR, 'Selly Raby Kane – The Other Dakar', Filmmakers note](https://www.radiancevr.co/artists/selly-raby-kane/)

> The Other Dakar is a manifesto in a sense, it is for me a way to reconnect the urban space with its mythology and to use design and creativity as a platform for the invisible Dakar to express her uniqueness.
>
> — Selly Raby Kane, [Radiance VR, 'Selly Raby Kane – The Other Dakar', Filmmakers note](https://www.radiancevr.co/artists/selly-raby-kane/)

<sub>says</sub>

> In a time where materialism occupies the mainstream, there is a need to re-invest imaginary spaces and use them as a fertile soil for the necessary adjustments we need to implement as a country facing several cultural and political changes.
>
> — Selly Raby Kane, [Radiance VR, 'Selly Raby Kane – The Other Dakar', Filmmakers note](https://www.radiancevr.co/artists/selly-raby-kane/)
