# Balance From Within

**Jacob Tonski**, 2014 · Robotics / kinetics / Material / sculpture / Sound

Balance From Within is a 170-year-old sofa that balances on one of its four legs. A computer-controlled mechanism inside it, based on the reaction wheel used in satellites, keeps correcting its position, and its motors make humming and creaking sounds. A push from a visitor or another outside force can knock it over; its main parts are held together with magnets so that it can be put back together. It received an Award of Distinction for Interactive Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2014.
