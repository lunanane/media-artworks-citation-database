# Wisdoms for Love 3.0

**Keiken (with Obso1337, Ryan Vautier, Sakeema Crook)**, 2022 · Online / net art / Sensor / interaction / Material / sculpture

A decision-making web game and NFT project, also presented as an installation with an interactive fibreglass sculpture with LED lights and water fountains, controlled by iPad. Players move through a labyrinth of metaphorical decisions (the path of the Divine Mother, Human God or Moral High Horse), collect 'wisdom tokens' as free downloadable artworks with a 'moral contract', and can see other online players on a map. Iterations were shown at HEK / Transfer Gallery, the Thailand Biennale 2021, Gray Area Festival 2021 and SeMA Bunker, Seoul; it received an Honorary Mention in Computer Animation at Prix Ars Electronica 2022.

## In their words

> The game explores the bubbled-up feelings, collective consciousness and fervent beliefs of the contemporary moment, where the desire for change and a more equal, decentralised future is collectively being challenged. The game is rooted in decision-making where you must decide your fate, do you take the path of the Divine Mother, become a Human God or stay on your Moral High Horse?
>
> — Keiken, [Wisdoms for Love 3.0 (Trimester 2) — keiken.cloud](https://keiken.cloud/work/wisdoms-for-love-3-0-2/)

<sub>asks</sub>

> Wisdoms for Love 3.0 explores and maps out different feelings and beliefs in the contemporary consciousness. It considers ‘wisdom’ as a tool for growth and change and proposes it as a form of currency to transact with that could be applied to the blockchain and integrated into future Web 3.0 technologies, creating alternative definitions of wealth for the ‘life’ game.
>
> — Keiken, [Wisdoms for Love 3.0 (Trimester 2) — keiken.cloud](https://keiken.cloud/work/wisdoms-for-love-3-0-2/)

<sub>says</sub>

> This piece asks: What wisdom do we need to get to Love 3.0 not just Web 3.0? What kind of decentralized systems do we want in our base reality (the Earth), and the metaverse? And how do we access global kin-making for the future of humanity?
>
> — Keiken, [Statement, Wisdoms for Love 3.0, Gray Area Festival 2021 (New Art City catalog)](https://newart.city/catalog/gaf2021-room-6)
