# Blade Runner—Autoencoded

**Terence Broad**, 2016 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative

Blade Runner—Autoencoded is a film made by training an autoencoder, a type of generative neural network, on every frame of Ridley Scott's Blade Runner (1982) and then having it reconstruct each frame in order. The reconstructed frames are resequenced into a version of the film that stays close to the original in static scenes and becomes hard to recognise in scenes with much movement. It was made as the artist's MSci dissertation project in Creative Computing at Goldsmiths, University of London.
