# Máquina Stella

**Marcela Armas**, 2012 · Robotics / kinetics / Light / Material / sculpture

Máquina Stella is an electromechanical sculpture made of aluminium, Teflon, resistive filaments, electricity, a mechanical system and electronic control, built as a dodecahedron with twelve limbs. Along each limb an electromechanical terminal moves lengthwise to energise a metal filament that glows red hot, in a programme that distributes the available energy between the parts. It received the ARCO/BEEP Electronic Art Award at ARCO Madrid in February 2012.
