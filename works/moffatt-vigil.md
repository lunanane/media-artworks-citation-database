# Vigil

**Tracey Moffatt**, 2017 · Screen / monitor / Sound

Vigil is a two-minute digital video with sound, made for Moffatt's exhibition My Horizon in the Australian Pavilion at the 2017 Venice Biennale. It intercuts news images of asylum-seeker boats, including coverage of the December 2010 boat wreck off Christmas Island, with close-ups from Hollywood films of actors such as Elizabeth Taylor, Julie Christie and Donald Sutherland looking through windows or binoculars, so that they appear to watch the disaster; the refugees' bodies are blocked out with colour and the sea is tinted red. It is shown as a looped screen work and is held by QAGOMA and M+, Hong Kong.
