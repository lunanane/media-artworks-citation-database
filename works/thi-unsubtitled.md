# Unsubtitled

**Nguyen Trinh Thi**, 2010 · Projection / Performance / body / Sound

A video and sound installation first shown at NhaSan Studio, Hanoi, in 2010: ten projectors loop life-size figures onto 19 wooden cut-out screens in a darkened room. The figures are artists from the Nha San Studio community, each of whom faces the camera, eats an item of food and then states their name and the name of the food. Exhibition texts place it after a directive by the cultural police to pause exhibitions at Nha San Studio following photographs of a nude performance by artist La Thi Dieu Ha; it was later shown at the Singapore Biennale 2013.
