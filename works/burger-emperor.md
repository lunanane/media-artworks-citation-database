# EMPEROR

**Marion Burger, Ilan J. Cohen**, 2024 · VR / AR / XR

EMPEROR is a 40-minute single-user interactive VR narrative, produced by Atlas V with Reynard Films and France Télévisions, set inside the mind of a father with aphasia, drawn as a hand-drawn monochrome landscape. The user takes the father's point of view alongside his daughter, using hand tracking to pick up objects, write letters and handle word cards in speech-therapy scenes, some of which are designed to fail at first. It is based on the story of co-director Marion Burger's father.

## In their words

> EMPEROR is born from a true story. The story of Marion’s father, who has been suffering from aphasia for the past fifteen years. He confuses words, and stumbles over each syllable. He understands everything, but can no longer make himself understood.
>
> — Marion Burger & Ilan J. Cohen, [Directors Statement, Atlas V project page](https://atlasv.io/emperor/)
