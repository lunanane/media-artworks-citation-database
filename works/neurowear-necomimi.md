# Necomimi

**neurowear**, 2013 · Sensor / interaction / Robotics / kinetics

Necomimi is a headband with motorised cat ears driven by a brainwave sensor: the ears rise when the wearer is concentrating, lie down when the wearer is relaxed, and rise and move when both happen at once. It was developed by the neurowear project team at Dentsu, first shown as a prototype at an event in Omotesando, Tokyo, and later manufactured and sold by NeuroSky. It received an Honorary Mention in Interactive Art at the Prix Ars Electronica 2013.
