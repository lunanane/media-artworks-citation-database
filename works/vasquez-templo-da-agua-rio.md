# Templo da água: rio Tietê

**Leonel Vásquez**, 2025 · Sound / Robotics / kinetics / Material / sculpture

A circular sound installation in which long copper flutes connected to glass bulbs are suspended and moved by a mechanical system that dips them into a basin of water; the filling and emptying of the bulbs pushes air through the tubes and makes them sound. The water was collected from the upper Tietê River, is kept oxygenated during the show and was to be returned to the river afterwards. Visitors sit on benches arranged around the basin and listen; at the 36th Bienal de São Paulo the work was also activated by performances by Vásquez and Esmeralda Ramírez (Werebere group).
