# There Goes Nikki

**Idris Brewster, Michele Stephenson, Joe Brewster**, 2025 · VR / AR / XR / Material / sculpture / Sound

There Goes Nikki is a five-minute augmented-reality memorial to the poet Nikki Giovanni, in which a volumetric capture of Giovanni recites her poem 'Quilting the Black-eyed Pea (We're going to Mars)' against a cosmic backdrop. At Tribeca Immersive 2025 visitors walked through a living garden installation to a pedestal that anchored the AR experience, with videos before and after it. It was made with Kinfolk Tech and Rada Collaborative.
