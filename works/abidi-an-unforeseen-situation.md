# An Unforeseen Situation

**Bani Abidi**, 2015 · Screen / monitor

An Unforeseen Situation is a video work by Bani Abidi relating to a series of state-run competitions organised by the Ministry of Sport in Punjab, Pakistan, in 2014, during which the country was reported to have broken several world records. Abidi tells her own version of such an event: a failed competition for the greatest mass singing of the national anthem, in which, according to a review in Open magazine, plastic chairs are piled up because nobody turned up. It was the title work of her solo exhibition at Neuer Berliner Kunstverein (n.b.k.), Berlin, 7 March – 30 April 2017.
