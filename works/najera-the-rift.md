# The Rift

**Janire Najera, Matthew Wright**, 2025 · Projection / Performance / body

The Rift is a 6-minute 360° fulldome dance film shot in Zimbabwe, in which four dancers perform a shared choreography across forests, plains, urban settings and abandoned or painted buildings, with water as the unifying element. It premiered at IDFA DocLab in November 2025, shown in the ARTIS-Planetarium dome in Amsterdam and in a smaller dome in the DocLab Exhibition. It was made by 4Pi Productions (Wales) with Matamba Film Labs and AfriKera Arts Trust (Harare) through the XR Labs collaboration.
