# Do Electric Sheep Dream 1, 2

**Tak Young Hwan and Lee Kyung Nam**, 2018 · Screen / monitor / Sound / Text / print

Two single-channel videos (part 1, 2 min with sound; part 2, 1 min 30 sec), digital compositions of images and text that look like a commercial advertisement or a juxtaposition of informational sign images, about human life in a near future as artificial intelligence permeates daily life. Commissioned by the 10th Seoul Mediacity Biennale (Eu Zên, Seoul Museum of Art, 2018); part 1 was also shown on the Seoullo Media Canvas building screen.
