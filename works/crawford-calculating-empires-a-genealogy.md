# Calculating Empires: A Genealogy of Power and Technology, 1500-2025

**Kate Crawford, Vladan Joler**, 2024 · Text / print / Network / data

A diagram more than 24 metres long and 3 metres high, made as a diptych of hundreds of hand-drawn illustrations and short texts, that maps communication, computation, classification and control from 1500 to the present. Time runs vertically and systems run horizontally, so visitors can follow a theme, a period or a set of ideas. Two handmade books are shown with it, and visitors are invited to annotate them in pencil.
