# Columbos

**KAWAI+OKAMURA**, 2014 · Screen / monitor

A nine-minute stop-motion animated film by the Japanese duo KAWAI+OKAMURA (Hiroki Okamura and Takumi Kawai) in which a puppet of the TV detective Columbo appears at a homicide scene with the corpse of an actress dressed in red and a man with a gunshot wound. The catalogue describes it as a meta-mystery told through a montage of time and space and of fantasy and reality. It premiered at IFFR 2013 and received an Honorary Mention in Computer Animation / Film / VFX at Prix Ars Electronica 2014.
