# From Hand to Mouth

**Thomas Feuerstein**, 2023 · Material / sculpture

An installation of steel, glass, a workshop crane, tubes and a pump in which a glass fermenter holds a replica of the right hand of Michelangelo's David, 3D-printed in the bioplastic PHB. PHB-producing bacteria (including Cupriavidus necator) in the fermenter gradually metabolise the hand and alter its form. It belongs to chapter 5 of Feuerstein's METABOLICA project and was shown in Renaissance 3.0 at ZKM Karlsruhe (2023), whose collection holds it.

## In their words

> Where in the Renaissance sculptural material once came from quarries like Carrara, with the chisel as tool, in METABOLICA it is bacteria that act simultaneously as “quarry” and “chisel”. They produce a new material for sculpture, only to digest it again, alter its form and return it to the metabolic cycle.
>
> — Thomas Feuerstein, [METABOLICA project text, artist's website (thomasfeuerstein.net)](https://www.thomasfeuerstein.net/05_METABOLICA/10_METABOLICA/20_METABOLIC-ART)
