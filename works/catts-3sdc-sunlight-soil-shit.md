# 3SDC (Sunlight, Soil & Shit (De)Cycle)

**Oron Catts, Ionat Zurr & Steve Berrick**, 2023 · Material / sculpture / Sensor / interaction / Network / data

A working installation made of four linked parts: a compost-heated incubator growing mouse muscle cells as lab-grown meat, an alkaline hydrolysis unit that turns tissue and slaughterhouse refuse into fertiliser, hydroponic beds growing plants without soil, and a control room collecting sensor data from the other parts. It was first staged as a three-week durational experiment at PS Art Space, Fremantle, in February 2022, with public workshops, a symposium, dinner events, live streaming and a documentary. It received an Award of Distinction in Artificial Intelligence & Life Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2023.

## In their words

> In times of ecological crisis, there is a vital need for seriously playful artistic interventions into these fantasies of separating humans from Nature. By using aesthetics to intervene, sense and gain data of the different processes involved in food manufacturing we are allowing the public to consider their own relations with both Nature and human-made technology.
>
> — Tissue Culture & Art Project (Oron Catts & Ionat Zurr), [Sunlight, Soil & Shit (De)Cycle, Tissue Culture & Art Project website](https://tcaproject.net/portfolio/sunlight-soil-and-shift-decycle/)
