# After Voices

**Jompet Kuswidananto**, 2016 · Material / sculpture / Sound / Robotics / kinetics

After Voices is an installation commissioned by Sherman Contemporary Art Foundation (SCAF), Sydney, as its Project 32 (2016, curated by Alia Swastika). Visitors walk through a procession of life-size 'ghost figures' without bodies, built from helmets, uniforms, mechanical clapping hands, musical instruments and motorcycle headlights, among rows of empty shoes, loudspeakers, flags, megaphones, drums and monitors, with kinetic movement and a layered soundscape. Video elements include a re-enactment of the 1 March 1949 Yogyakarta General Offensive; the work refers to the protests and upheaval after the 1998 fall of Suharto's New Order.

## In their words

> I have been well known for making these bodiless crowd installations, like in After Voices. I used these ghost figures to talk about the fluidity of Indonesian history.
>
> — Jompet Kuswidananto, [Allie Tepper, "Sad Songs to Euphoria: Jompet Kuswidananto on the Making of Celestial Sorrow", Walker Art Center, 8 April 2019](https://walkerart.org/magazine/jompet-kuswidananto-meg-stuart-celestial-sorrow/)

<sub>says</sub>

> The country has been governed by many different regimes for different purposes, so the way you see the history and identity of Indonesia is always changing.
>
> — Jompet Kuswidananto, [Allie Tepper, "Sad Songs to Euphoria", Walker Art Center, 8 April 2019](https://walkerart.org/magazine/jompet-kuswidananto-meg-stuart-celestial-sorrow/)

<sub>says</sub>

> I was a student when the regime fell, and I think I was really taken by euphoria.
>
> — Jompet Kuswidananto, [Allie Tepper, "Sad Songs to Euphoria", Walker Art Center, 8 April 2019](https://walkerart.org/magazine/jompet-kuswidananto-meg-stuart-celestial-sorrow/)

<sub>says</sub>

> Working with these states of transition, I am dealing with a culture that is never really fixed, never really in a state in which you can grab it or touch it. It’s like a ghost. That’s why I create ghost figures.
>
> — Jompet Kuswidananto, ["Jompet Kuswidananto interview: Order and After, Sonica 2015", Studio International](https://studiointernational.com/jompet-kuswidananto-interview-order-and-after-sonica-2015)

<sub>says</sub>

> The political ups and downs during this period have been very significant in shaping people’s collective memory. Many victims fall at each point of transition, but this is never discussed properly.
>
> — Jompet Kuswidananto, ["Jompet Kuswidananto interview: Order and After, Sonica 2015", Studio International](https://studiointernational.com/jompet-kuswidananto-interview-order-and-after-sonica-2015)
