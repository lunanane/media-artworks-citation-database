# Spray and Pray

**Veneta Androva**, 2026 · Screen / monitor / AI / generative / Network / data

Spray and Pray is a 15-minute speculative animated documentary in Bulgarian about 'mushroom websites', automatically generated news sites that earn advertising revenue by spreading disinformation. Its CGI characters are built from anonymised research data and performed roles and speak with synthetic voices; a mushroom figure speaks only in headlines collected from such sites, which were also analysed with Bulgarian-language machine-learning models. It premiered at Visions du Réel in 2026 and is shown in the Bulgarian Pavilion at the 2026 Venice Biennale.

> It examines how digital media infrastructures produce belief, working through a speculative documentary form that reconstructs automated news networks and algorithmic content systems. Rather than documenting single events, the film stages the mechanisms through which credibility is manufactured.
>
> — Veneta Androva, [ArtReview, Venice Biennale: Artist Q&As (Bulgaria), 28 May 2026](https://artreview.com/four-artists-on-representing-bulgaria-at-the-61st-venice-biennale/)
