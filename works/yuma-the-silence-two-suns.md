# The Silence (Two Suns)

**Tomiyasu Yuma**, 2025 · Light / Screen / monitor / Material / sculpture

An installation at Aichi Triennale 2025 in a former greengrocer's shop in a Seto shopping arcade, with a display window covered in mirrors. Visitors enter through an automatic door into a room where the floor is covered with Suna Kira (tailings from refining silica sand mined in Seto). Unfired flowers made of Seto clay lie on it and fall into the sand over time. Paintings, video and lighting that turns on and off are also used.
