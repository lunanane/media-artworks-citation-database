# Visualizing Palestine

**Visualizing Palestine**, 2013 · Network / data / Text / print / Online / net art

Visualizing Palestine is a project, initiated by Ramzi Jaber and co-founded with Joumana al Jabri, that turns research and data on Palestinian experiences into infographics, published first on its website and republished online, in print and in several languages. Its first infographic, Hunger Strikes (February 2012), set out the physical effects of a hunger strike alongside a history of hunger strikes as a tool of non-violent struggle, at the time of Khader Adnan's hunger strike. It received a Distinction in the Prix Ars Electronica Digital Communities category in 2013.
