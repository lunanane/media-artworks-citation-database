# Pocket Money

**Emmaline Zanelli**, 2026 · Screen / monitor / Performance / body

Pocket Money is a video work of about 15 minutes, shot between September and December 2025, featuring about 20 young people from in and around Adelaide in their first paid jobs, such as fast food and shelf-stacking, as well as at rest in their bedrooms, spending money and pursuing hobbies. It was shown in the 2026 Adelaide Biennial of Australian Art: Yield Strength (27 February – 8 June 2026).
