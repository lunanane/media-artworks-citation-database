# Eunoia

**Lisa Park**, 2013 · Sensor / interaction / Sound / Performance / body

Eunoia is a performance in which Lisa Park wears a commercial NeuroSky EEG headset while meditating; custom software turns her 'Attention' and 'Meditation' values and brainwave data into sound, adjusting volume, pitch and panning. The sound plays through five speakers under five water-filled metal dishes arranged in a circle around her, so that her mental states appear as vibrations on the water. Her stated aim was to reach a stillness in which no sound is heard and the water does not move.

## In their words

> The motivation behind this performance was to achieve inner calmness, having control over the artist's consciousness and thoughts. The goal was to reach a moment of complete stillness so that no sound could be heard and no vibrations could be seen on the water.
>
> — Lisa Park (artist's website), [Eunoia, work page, thelisapark.com](https://www.thelisapark.com/work/eunoia)

> The reason why I started the Eunoia project was I wanted to find a way to visualise my emotions and mind-scape, so I did some research about working with biosensors and brainwave sensors and then I began using the commodity as a tool as part of my work.
>
> — Lisa Park, ['Eunoia – beautiful thoughts', interview by Richard Bright, Interalia Magazine, April 2015](https://www.interaliamag.org/interviews/lisa-park-eunoia-beautiful-thought/)
