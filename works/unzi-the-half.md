# The Half

**Intermedia Chef (Kim Unzi)**, 2013 · Sound / Material / sculpture

A media installation of 25-letter pixelated typography made from 100 speaker units that play sound waves generated from archived mass-media material. Flour on the speakers makes the sound waves visible, and the letters eventually spell the phrase "Growing Gap Between the Halves". It received an Honorary Mention in Digital Musics & Sound Art at Prix Ars Electronica 2013 and was a Jury Selection in the Art Division of the 17th Japan Media Arts Festival.
