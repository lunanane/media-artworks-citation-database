# SONG 1

**Doug Aitken**, 2012 · Projection / Sound

SONG 1 was a video installation projected with 11 high-definition projectors onto the entire circular facade of the Hirshhorn Museum and Sculpture Garden in Washington, D.C., from dusk to midnight between 22 March and 13 May 2012, forming a 360-degree image seen from the National Mall. On a loop, figures such as a businessman, a waitress, dancers and singers, including Tilda Swinton and John Doe, appear in diners, factories, cars and other everyday places, singing or moving to some 40 newly recorded versions of the song 'I Only Have Eyes for You'. The installation has no dialogue or fixed beginning and is meant to be come upon in passing.

## In their words

> Song 1 is based on one song, what I think is a very perfect pop song. I wanted to take this one specific song and see if that could become the structure for this artwork, and if that song could be reinterpreted in many different ways and eventually create its own landscape.
>
> — Doug Aitken, [Daoud Tyler-Ameen, 'Millions Of People Go By: Doug Aitken's "Song 1" Distills City Life', NPR (via KUNC), 20 May 2012, quoting Aitken's press statement](https://www.kunc.org/2012-05-20/millions-of-people-go-by-doug-aitkens-song-1-distills-city-life)
