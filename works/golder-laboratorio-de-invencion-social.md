# Laboratorio de invención social (o posibles formas de construcción colectiva)

**Gabriela Golder**, 2018 · Screen / monitor / Sound / Text / print

A four-channel video installation from Gabriela Golder's series Reoccupation, made with workers of three Argentine cooperatives in recovered factories (Cintoplom, Cadenas Ancla and Cristales San Justo): two channels show the factory (42'20" each) and two show the workers (31'02" each), and the original installation also included printed newspapers. Visitors see and hear images and audio recordings of workers involved in the factory occupations and cooperatives. It received the main award (Estado da Arte – Eletrica Cinema e Vídeo) at the 21st Bienal Sesc\_Videobrasil in 2019.

## In their words

> Laboratorio de Invención Social, my work exhibited the 21st Biennial Sesc\_Videobrasil, is proposed as space of encounter. Of the workers and myself, initially, but fundamentally of cooperative models, of recovery, of reoccupation with other groups, communities and people.
>
> — Gabriela Golder, [Statement 2019 (transcription of the statement for the 21st Biennial), Videobrasil archive](https://site.videobrasil.org.br/en/acervo/artistas/textos/39223)

<sub>says</sub>

> We are immigrants, workers, refugees, women, victims of state terrorism. I want to listen to learn, to know, to understand. How do we live, survive? What are the strategies? The means? The accounts? The fears?
>
> — Gabriela Golder, [Statement 2019 (transcription of the statement for the 21st Biennial), Videobrasil archive](https://site.videobrasil.org.br/en/acervo/artistas/textos/39223)

<sub>asks</sub>

> “Everyone does everything, we all earn the same.” That was one of the first sentences I heard from them. It is indeed a revolutionary proposition at a time when the governments of our countries crush us by implementing policies of repression, hunger and annihilation of rights gained by workers, by minorities.
>
> — Gabriela Golder, [Statement 2019 (transcription of the statement for the 21st Biennial), Videobrasil archive](https://site.videobrasil.org.br/en/acervo/artistas/textos/39223)

<sub>says</sub>

> When I started contacting the workers of recovered factories, when I learned about the cooperatives, their modes of operation, I felt that somehow this had to be useful to other communities, that we must organize ourselves to be stronger and resist.
>
> — Gabriela Golder, [Statement 2019 (transcription of the statement for the 21st Biennial), Videobrasil archive](https://site.videobrasil.org.br/en/acervo/artistas/textos/39223)
