# The Full Story

**Daisy Jacobs**, 2018 · Screen / monitor

The Full Story is a short film (about eight minutes), directed by Daisy Jacobs and co-directed by Christopher Wilder, in which Toby, selling his childhood home, recalls his parents' divorce in the 1970s and 80s. It is animated life-size with painted 2D characters on walls, 3D arms, half-painted half-real sets that turn and morph between rooms, and live actors (pixilation) blended into the painted scenes. It received an Honorary Mention in Computer Animation at Prix Ars Electronica 2018.

## In their words

> This time I wanted to explore themes of family breakdown: why people leave each other and how this affects the children.
>
> — Daisy Jacobs, [Prix Ars Electronica 2018 archive entry, catalog text (first person, no named author)](https://archive.aec.at/prix/236538/)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> I wrote the script about a child who is confused and caught up in his parents divorce and feels helpless. I wanted the surroundings to reflect how the child was feeling, so used a very dense patterned environment – to show how overwhelmed he felt.
>
> — Daisy Jacobs, [Dragonframe blog, 'The Full Story by Daisy Jacobs' (interview quotes)](https://www.dragonframe.com/blog/the-full-story-by-daisy-jacobs/)

<sub>says</sub>

> I was also interested in how the boy as an adult would feel and reflect on this period in his life and that we can never really know why people choose to leave each-other and things are never that simple, hence the title ‘The Full Story’
>
> — Daisy Jacobs, [Dragonframe blog, 'The Full Story by Daisy Jacobs' (interview quotes)](https://www.dragonframe.com/blog/the-full-story-by-daisy-jacobs/)

>
