# The Revolving Internet

**Constant Dullaart**, 2010 · Online / net art

The Revolving Internet is a website that shows the Google home page steadily rotating while it remains usable for searching, soundtracked by Dusty Springfield's 'Windmills of your Mind'. Visitors search and browse through the turning page, and could reach other sites through it. It launched in 2010 as part of a series of browser-based works by Dullaart that animate the Google search page; it originally embedded Google in an iframe and now runs through a dedicated proxy built with Jonas Lund.
