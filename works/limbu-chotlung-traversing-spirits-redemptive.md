# Chotlung: traversing spirits, redemptive songs

**Mekh Limbu**, 2025 · Screen / monitor / Material / sculpture

A single-channel video (8:50 minutes) shown with a textile installation of pieces handwoven by the artist, co-commissioned by Colomboscope and Ghost 2568 and exhibited at Colomboscope: Rhythm Alliances in Colombo. The video shows looms and Yakthung rituals at Mukkumlung sacred mountain and the Imbiri Yangthangwa (Tamor river) region of eastern Nepal, alongside protests against hydropower dams and a proposed cable car; the textiles have also been carried in demonstrations.
