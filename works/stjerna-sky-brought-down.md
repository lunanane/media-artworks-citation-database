# Sky Brought Down

**Åsa Stjerna**, 2019 · Sound / Network / data

A permanent site-specific sound installation in the atrium and main staircase of the Center for Imaging and Intervention at Sahlgrenska University Hospital, Gothenburg. A weather station on the hospital roof sends real-time data (pressure, precipitation, wind, light) that software turns into sound textures, played through 16 speakers mounted behind floor-to-ceiling wooden panelling beneath a glass ceiling. People passing through the atrium hear the sky outside sonified along the vertical space; the work received an Honorary Mention at Prix Ars Electronica 2019.
