# Hotel Infinity

**William Chyr**, 2026 · VR / AR / XR / Sensor / interaction

Hotel Infinity is a room-scale VR puzzle game by Studio Chyr, set in a hotel built from non-Euclidean, looping architecture. Players move through hallways and ballrooms by physically walking inside a play area of about 2 x 2 metres, with portals folding the space, and solve puzzles to uncover the hotel's secrets. It was released for Meta Quest and PS VR2 on 13 November 2025 and selected for Venice Immersive 2026.

## In their words

> When we set out to make Hotel Infinity, our priority was to enhance immersion in VR by making movement feel completely natural. Instead of using joysticks or teleportation, players explore the world by physically walking through it.
>
> — William Chyr, [Main Creator's Statement, La Biennale di Venezia, Venice Immersive 2026](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2026/venice-immersive/hotel-infinity)

<sub>says</sub>

> The project is inspired by mathematician David Hilbert’s thought experiment about a hotel with an infinite number of rooms; even though the hotel is fully occupied, it can somehow always accommodate new guests. While the paradox was originally intended to highlight the mathematical peculiarities of infinity, we wondered what it would be like to visit such a place.
>
> — William Chyr, [Main Creator's Statement, La Biennale di Venezia, Venice Immersive 2026](https://www.labiennale.org/en/cinema/2026/venice-immersive/hotel-infinity)

<sub>says</sub>

> Instead of adapting Manifold Garden for VR, we went back to the original inspiration: the desire to experience space that’s impossible in the real world. What would experiencing impossible space in VR be like?
>
> — William Chyr, [Email interview, UploadVR, 'Hotel Infinity Started As Manifold Garden In VR', 26 November 2025](https://uploadvr.com/hotel-infinity-started-as-manifold-garden-in-vr)
