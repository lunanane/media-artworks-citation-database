# khalil

**Khaled Sabsabi**, 2026 · Projection / Sound / Material / sculpture

khalil is a site-specific multimedia installation by Khaled Sabsabi in In Minor Keys, the main exhibition of the 61st Venice Biennale (2026), made of object, video, scent and sound. Its title is the Arabic word for close friend or companion. Sabsabi showed it at the same time as conference of one's self in the Australia Pavilion, and he describes the two as complementary works.
