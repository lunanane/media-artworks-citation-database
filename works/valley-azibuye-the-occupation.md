# Azibuye – The Occupation

**Dylan Valley & Ngako Mosiea**, 2020 · VR / AR / XR

Azibuye – The Occupation is a 360-degree virtual reality documentary following Masello Motana, a performance artist, and Evan Abrahamse, an activist and former Umkhonto we Sizwe member, who occupy a mansion in an affluent part of Johannesburg that has stood empty and decaying for about 20 years, as an act about land ownership. The viewer is placed inside the house with them; the film includes a recorded phone interview with the house's owner. Produced by Electric South, it premiered in the New Frontier section of the Sundance Film Festival in 2020.

## In their words

> They were unapologetic about their goals and managed to take action on an issue where there has been so little movement by elected officials. The history of the house itself also reflects layers of South African history and is visually arresting – there are details and textures everywhere,
>
> — Dylan Valley, [Azibuye – The Occupation // SA's first VR documentary to show at Sundance Film Festival, Christa Dee, Bubblegum Club, 2020](https://archive.bubblegumclub.co.za/?p=20703)

<sub>says</sub>

> through filming this as a VR film, the audience is immersed in the occupied house, which is visually arresting as it's a mansion that has been left to decay for about 20 years. The viewer is placed in the role of someone inside of this occupation, and is then, in a sense, a collaborator in this radical act. When questions about who actually owns the house comes to surface, the viewer has to face their own biases and assumptions. It gets complicated.
>
> — Dylan Valley, [Azibuye – The Occupation // SA's first VR documentary to show at Sundance Film Festival, Christa Dee, Bubblegum Club, 2020](https://archive.bubblegumclub.co.za/?p=20703)
