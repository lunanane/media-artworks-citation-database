# Moving Earth, Crossing Water, Eating Soil

**Zarina Muhammad**, 2022 · Material / sculpture / Sound / Performance / body

A multipart mixed-media installation on St John's Island (Pulau Sekijang Bendera), Singapore, commissioned for Singapore Biennale 2022 (Natasha), with performances and activations. In the island's main administrative building a four-pillared structure based on the Javanese saka guru holds a shrine-like arrangement of objects referring to the island's history, with painted cutouts of eight animals from Bugis divination diagrams; visitors follow nine archetypal symbols (The Guide, The Witness, The Wrathful Deity and others). In the garden, eight wind-driven wooden instruments strike bamboo plates, and visitors can also turn them by hand.
