# Lead the Way

**Marcus Neustetter**, 2018 · Light / Robotics / kinetics / Performance / body

Lead the Way is a triptych made from the physical and audiovisual remains of collaborative performances in Dakar, Johannesburg and Karlsruhe within the project Digital Imaginaries (2018), dedicated to the damaged South African satellite SumbandilaSat, whose Venda name means 'lead the way'. At ZKM Karlsruhe the installation is a shadowscape of ritual objects, Chinese consumer-electronics junk and the shadows of visitors and performers, with robotic lights that pan the space and at times follow the satellite's path. Earlier iterations were a five-hour shadow performance with musician Lamine Kora Kouyaté and dancer Fatou Cissé in Dakar and a video and shadow installation with poets in Johannesburg.
