# Acoustic Additive Synthesizer

**Krzysztof Cybulski**, 2015 · Sound / Robotics / kinetics / Sensor / interaction

The Acoustic Additive Synthesizer (AAS) is a small computer-controlled pipe organ of seven pipes, each with a motorised piston that changes pitch continuously and a motorised air valve that changes volume; a Pure Data patch uses Fourier analysis to resynthesise sound with the pipes. Visitors speak or sing into a microphone on a stand and the instrument tries to repeat what it hears. The first version, with motorised bellows, was commissioned for the INSTALAKCJE 4 festival in 2015; a second version was built for the multimedia opera Aaron S at Warsaw Autumn 2016.

## In their words

> I think that would be the Acoustic Additive Synth, which builds upon the idea of spectral resynthesis, but in a physical, acoustic form. I like how it combines the centuries-old techniques of organ and bellows building with a modern implementation of the Fast Fourier Transform, microcontrollers, servomechanisms and digitally fabricated mechanical parts.
>
> — Krzysztof Cybulski, [Bela blog, 'Krzysztof Cybulski on Instrument Design' (interview)](https://blog.bela.io/krzysztof-cybulski-interview/)

<sub>says</sub>

> It all summed up to a device which could be used both as an instrument and an interactive installation, and the sensation of hearing glimpses of recognisable vowels in the combined sound of a couple of acoustic pipes is really thrilling (how come, no speakers?)
>
> — Krzysztof Cybulski, [Bela blog, 'Krzysztof Cybulski on Instrument Design' (interview)](https://blog.bela.io/krzysztof-cybulski-interview/)

<sub>asks</sub>

> lt doesn't necessarily resynthesize comprehensible speech, but the correlation between input and output is obvious, and the impression of controlling an actual physical instrument with the voice in real time is much stronger than in the case of electronic or digital voice transformation.
>
> — Krzysztof Cybulski, [Prix Ars Electronica 2017 archive entry, catalog text (no named author)](https://archive.aec.at/prix/227033/)

<sub>says · unsigned project text</sub>

> Acoustic Additive Synthesizer is another step - after SPECTRAL SCORE - to create fully acoustic device that could resynthesize any sound by the means of Fourier analysis and resynthesis, thus utilising basic principles of spectral music in realtime.
>
> — Krzysztof Cybulski, [krzysztofcybulski.com, 'ACOUSTIC ADDITIVE SYNTH (AAS) /// 2015-16' (artist's site, no named author)](http://krzysztofcybulski.com/aas2.html)
