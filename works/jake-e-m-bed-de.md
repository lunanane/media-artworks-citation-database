# E.m-bed.de/d, Datamosh, Augmented Real

**Yung Jake**, 2013 · Online / net art / Screen / monitor / VR / AR / XR

E.m-bed.de/d is an interactive HTML5 music video by the rapper and artist Yung Jake. It opens a string of pop-up browser windows, each showing a different part of the same video, together with rising YouTube view counts and 'endorsements' from other accounts and sites. In Datamosh, the pixels that make up his image are digitally eroded while he raps about datamoshing. The 2013 Sundance Film Festival New Frontier listing describes him streaming into screenings, Skype-bombing DJ sets and appearing in augmented-reality music videos.
