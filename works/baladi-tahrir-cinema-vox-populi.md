# Tahrir Cinema (Vox Populi)

**Lara Baladi**, 2011 · Projection / Network / data

Tahrir Cinema was a public screening space in Tahrir Square, Cairo, that Lara Baladi co-founded with the Egyptian media collective Mosireen during the summer 2011 sit-in; she describes it as the first project to emerge from Vox Populi, her ongoing archive of the 2011 Egyptian revolution. Films and video clips of the revolution were projected on a makeshift screen at the entrance of the square to an audience seated on the ground, and people could also bring and exchange raw footage on USB flash drives and DVDs.
