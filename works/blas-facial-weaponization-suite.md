# Facial Weaponization Suite

**Zach Blas**, 2012 · Material / sculpture / Camera / computer vision / Performance / body

A series of community workshops in which participants' faces are scanned and the aggregated facial data is modelled into amorphous "collective masks" that biometric facial recognition does not detect as human faces. The masks are used in public interventions and performances and shown with videos and prints; individual masks address queer men's faces (Fag Face Mask), blackness, feminism and veil legislation, and biometrics at the Mexico-US border.
